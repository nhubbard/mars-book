# Step 14 Advance one column

Each destination moves four bytes because each pixel occupies a word. The bit mask shifts right from 128 through 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, and 1. After testing bit 0, the shift produces zero.

## Write or inspect

Append the destination and mask updates.

```asm
    daddiu $t2, $t2, 4
    daddiu $t7, $t7, 4
    srl $t4, $t4, 1
```

## Expected observation

After one pair, the pointers advance to the next cells and the mask becomes 64. The framebuffer row base in $s0 remains unchanged.

## Check your understanding

What is the mask after processing all eight columns?

- **A** Zero.
- **B** One, because bit zero can never be shifted away.
- **C** 255.
