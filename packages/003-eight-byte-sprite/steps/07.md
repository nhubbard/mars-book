# Step 7 Append the extracted bit

Shifting the accumulator left creates a zero position at its low end. OR then inserts the extracted bit into that position. Existing accumulated bits move one position toward the high end.

## Write or inspect

Append the shift and combination.

```asm
    sll $t8, $t8, 1
    or $t8, $t8, $t5
```

## Expected observation

After processing the first two low bits of 0x16 in order, the accumulator is binary 01. The complete eight-iteration trace ends at 01101000.

## Check your understanding

Why shift before OR?

- **A** Shifting makes the source address smaller.
- **B** The shift makes room for the next bit at position zero.
- **C** OR always reverses a byte by itself.
