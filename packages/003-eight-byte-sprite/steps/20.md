# Step 20 Explain and vary the representation

The program separates source representation, reversal, and drawing. Each has a distinct contract. A different correct implementation may combine them, but the final bytes and pixels must still agree for substituted source rows.

## Write or inspect

Choose an eight-byte asymmetric marker of your own, predict its mirror, and test it. Explain the row invariant and why the final store stays within the frame. Submit the required trace and address derivation.

## Expected observation

The grader accepts equivalent implementations of the published contract. Source spelling, private labels, and register choices are not part of free-writing grading.
