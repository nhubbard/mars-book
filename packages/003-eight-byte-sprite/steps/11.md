# Step 11 Prepare the two destination positions

The normal image starts at column zero. The mirror starts at column eight, 32 bytes into the row. Both drawing pointers advance by one word per column. A mask of 128 tests bit 7 first.

## Write or inspect

Append the pointer and mask setup.

```asm
    move $t2, $s0
    daddiu $t7, $s0, 32
    li $t4, 128
```

## Expected observation

The destinations are separated by eight words. The mask is binary 10000000. daddiu updates an address at the full 64-bit register width.
