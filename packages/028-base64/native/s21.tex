\section{Step 21 Read all four characters before storing a byte}\label{base64-s21}

Classify a complete quartet into four private words, using zero for the already-validated pad positions. Retain the loop index across helper calls. Loading the entire quartet before publication is what permits exact in-place decoding without overwriting a character still needed by this group.

Replace the body of region \texttt{dec\_quartet}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

decode_quartet:
    daddu $t0, $s0, $s4

decode_digit_store:
    dsll $t0, $s4, 2
    daddu $t0, $sp, $t0

    daddiu $s4, $s4, 1
    sltiu $t0, $s4, 4
    bnez $t0, decode_quartet
    nop
