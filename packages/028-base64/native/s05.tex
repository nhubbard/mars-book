Form the first two numerical sextets from the loaded bytes. Use each as an unsigned offset into the alphabet table and store the selected character. Inspect the numerical index before interpreting its table result as ASCII. The remaining regions are still unfinished at this boundary.

Replace the body of region \texttt{enc\_pair}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.
