\section{Step 29 Apply the first alphabet correction}\label{base64-s29}

Compare the original sextets with 25, turn the true masks into sixes and add that correction into a separate result vector. Keep the original sextets intact. Adding a raw all-ones mask would apply the wrong arithmetic correction.

Replace the body of region \texttt{enc12\_upper}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    ldi.b $w2, 25
    clt_u.b $w3, $w2, $w1
    andi.b $w3, $w3, 6
    addv.b $w4, $w1, $w3
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s29-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{after\_enc12\_upper} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

Only sextets above 25 receive the correction six.
