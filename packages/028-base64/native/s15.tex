\section{Step 15 Validate one final pad}\label{base64-s15}

A pad is permitted only at the very end or as the first of two final pads. For a single final pad, check that the preceding sextet low two bits are zero, then subtract one from the decoded size. Earlier pads and nonzero unused bits reach the supplied bad-text return.

Replace the body of region \texttt{val\_one}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
validate_padding:
    li $t0, 2
    beq $s1, $t0, validate_two
    nop
    li $t0, 1
    bne $s1, $t0, validate_bad
    nop
    andi $t0, $s3, 3
    bnez $t0, validate_bad
    nop
    daddiu $s2, $s2, -1
    b validate_good
    nop
\end{minted}
