\section{Step 22 Publish the first decoded byte}\label{base64-s22}

Combine the first two sextets and store the low byte. Decrease remaining output and stop immediately if the validated size has been reached. This early exit is required for a one-byte decoded tail even though four text characters were loaded.

Replace the body of region \texttt{dec\_first}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.
