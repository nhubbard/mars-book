\section{Step 6 Publish the third character when it exists}\label{base64-s06}

A one-byte input tail needs padding at the third position, so route it to the supplied pad label. Otherwise combine the low four bits of the second byte with the high two bits of the third. A two-byte tail uses a zero third input register.

Replace the body of region \texttt{enc\_third}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    sltiu $t3, $s1, 2
    bnez $t3, encode_pad_two
    nop
    andi $t3, $t1, 15
    sll $t3, $t3, 2
    srl $t4, $t2, 6
    or $t3, $t3, $t4
    daddu $t4, $s4, $t3
    lbu $t4, 0($t4)
    sb $t4, 2($s2)
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s06-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{after\_enc\_third} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.
