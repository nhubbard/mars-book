\section{Step 28 Join the right contribution and mask sextets}\label{base64-s28}

Gather the complementary source bytes, shift them and apply their position-specific masks. OR the two contributions and mask every lane with 63. The original working vector now contains numerical sextets and must remain available for every later alphabet-threshold comparison.

Replace the body of region \texttt{enc12\_right}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    dla $t1, enc_right
    ld.b $w3, 0($t1)
    vshf.b $w3, $w0, $w0
    dla $t1, enc_right_shift
    ld.b $w2, 0($t1)
    srl.b $w3, $w3, $w2
    dla $t1, enc_right_mask
    ld.b $w2, 0($t1)
    and.v $w3, $w3, $w2
    or.v $w1, $w1, $w3
    andi.b $w1, $w1, 63
\end{minted}
