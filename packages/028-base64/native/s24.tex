\section{Step 24 Complete the quartet and continue}\label{base64-s24}

Form the third byte, decrease remaining output and advance by four input characters and three output bytes. Repeat through the supplied scalar loop. The normal return uses the retained total rather than the decreased remaining count. Now check padded final groups as separate recipes.

Replace the body of region \texttt{dec\_third}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.
