\section{Step 38 Join and compact the twelve useful bytes}\label{base64-s38}

Gather and shift the complementary sextets, merge them, then select the twelve useful byte positions into a compact vector. Select known zeros for its four trailing positions. The next region will publish only the twelve-byte result.

Replace the body of region \texttt{dec16\_compact}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

    ld.b $w1, 0($t1)

    or.v $w6, $w6, $w7

    ld.b $w7, 0($t1)
    vshf.b $w7, $w6, $w6
