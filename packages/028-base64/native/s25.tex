\section{Step 25 Follow exact in-place decoding}\label{base64-s25}

The scalar decoder loads a whole quartet before writing its first output byte. Source then advances four while output advances three. Trace a second quartet to see why unread text stays ahead of the output pointer.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The decoded prefix changes while the remaining source suffix stays intact.
