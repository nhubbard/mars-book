\section{Step 8 Write the final one or two pad characters}\label{base64-s08}

Share the last pad store between both short-tail paths. The one-byte tail first writes a pad at position two, then falls through to the final position. Finish without subtracting three from a smaller remaining count. The stored padding determines text shape, not additional binary output.

Replace the body of region \texttt{enc\_padding}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

\subsection{Check your understanding}
Does a pad represent a decoded zero byte?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} Yes, always.
  \item \textbf{B} Only when output is aligned.
  \item \textbf{C} No; it marks an incomplete input group.
\end{itemize}
