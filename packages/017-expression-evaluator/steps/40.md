# 40. Distinguish an unfinished group from a zero denominator

Use 1/(2-2 and then 1/(2-2). The first group cannot supply a completed denominator because the close is missing. The second supplies zero successfully, allowing the outer divide to diagnose its own failure. These examples exercise error ordering rather than only operator arithmetic.

## Expected observation

The missing close reports syntax at six; the complete denominator reports zero division at one.

## Check your understanding

Which failure wins for the input without its closing parenthesis?

- **A** Division by zero at one.
- **B** Success after treating the input end as an implicit close.
- **C** Syntax at the six-byte input end.
