# 17. Consume an accepted digit and classify its successor

Advance the cursor after the candidate passes. If that reaches the length, finish the number immediately. Otherwise load the next available byte and apply the same digit test. A digit loops back to accumulation; a nondigit leaves the cursor at that byte for the surrounding parser.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary return in the `primary_next` region. Preserve its surrounding labels and frame scaffolding.

```asm
    addiu $s1, $s1, 1
    beq $s1, $s2, primary_number
    nop
    daddu $t0, $s0, $s1
    lbu $t0, 0($t0)
    addiu $t1, $t0, -48
    sltiu $t2, $t1, 10
    bne $t2, $zero, primary_digit
    nop
```

## Expected observation

For 123, the accumulator becomes 123 and the cursor reaches three. The number-result region is still unfinished.
