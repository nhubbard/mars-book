# 10. Compute an exact wide sum

Add the two sign-extended signed-word values with `daddu`, retaining the candidate in `t0`. Every possible sum in this input domain fits in a signed doubleword. The operation therefore supplies an exact candidate for the later word-range test; its nontrapping form does not authorize a wrapped word result.
