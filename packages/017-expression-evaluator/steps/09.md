# 9. Reject zero before signed division

Test the right operand before issuing `ddiv`. A zero denominator transfers to the zero-status region, which will be completed shortly. A nonzero denominator produces a signed doubleword quotient and transfers to the shared range check. Using the wide quotient also makes the signed-word minimum divided by minus one representable long enough to diagnose its range.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary return in the `apply_divide` region. Preserve its surrounding labels and frame scaffolding.

```asm
    beq $a1, $zero, apply_zero
    nop
    ddiv $t0, $a0, $a1
    beq $zero, $zero, apply_check
    nop
```
