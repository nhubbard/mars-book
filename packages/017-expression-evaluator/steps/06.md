# 6. Recognize the four whitespace bytes

Compare the byte with decimal 32, 9, 10, and 13. Each match transfers to the common advance label; a nonmatch returns without consuming that byte. This explicit list is part of the language. A general character-class routine might also accept bytes that this contract rejects.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary return in the `space_classify` region. Preserve its surrounding labels and frame scaffolding.

```asm
    li $t2, 32
    beq $t1, $t2, space_advance
    nop
    li $t2, 9
    beq $t1, $t2, space_advance
    nop
    li $t2, 10
    beq $t1, $t2, space_advance
    nop
    li $t2, 13
    bne $t1, $t2, space_return
    nop
```

## Expected observation

Before the advance region is written, the space match reaches its temporary return. The cursor still remains zero.
