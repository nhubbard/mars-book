# Related reading
Robert Nystrom's *Crafting Interpreters*, chapter 6, discusses precedence and
recursive-descent parsing. Theodore Norvell's *Parsing Expressions by Recursive
Descent* develops precedence climbing as another expression-parsing method.
These are background reading for the parser designs; this chapter's byte bounds,
integer range and diagnostic-order rules are defined by the project contract.

- [Nystrom: Parsing expressions (author's source)](https://github.com/munificent/craftinginterpreters/blob/master/book/parsing-expressions.md)
- [Norvell: Parsing expressions by recursive descent](https://www.engr.mun.ca/~theo/Misc/exp_parsing.htm)
