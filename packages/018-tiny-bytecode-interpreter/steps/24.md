# Route the four stack operations

Select separate DUP, DROP, SWAP and OVER entries. Their live-value requirements and depth changes differ, so sharing their opcode width does not make their execution effects interchangeable. DUP and OVER will later share the actual copy after choosing different source indices.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch in `dispatch_stack`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

```asm
    li $t1, 32
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_dup
    nop
    li $t1, 33
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_drop
    nop
    li $t1, 34
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_swap
    nop
    li $t1, 35
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_over
    nop
```

## Expected observation

A DUP byte selects its handler with the opcode still available.
