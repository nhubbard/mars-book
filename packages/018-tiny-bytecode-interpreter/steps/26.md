# Retire halt and publish success

Advance PC past the one-byte halt, increment retired count, consume one budget unit, and select status one. Then publish through the provided output/restore path. Halt retains every live operand and does not require a particular depth. A zero budget never enters this handler.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch in `halt`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

```asm
    addiu $s6, $s6, 1
    addiu $v1, $v1, 1
    addiu $a3, $a3, -1
    li $v0, 1
    beq $zero, $zero, vm_commit
    nop
```

## Expected observation

A one-byte halt with budget one returns status one, PC one and retired count one.
