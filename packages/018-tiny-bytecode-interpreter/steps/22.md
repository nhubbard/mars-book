# Route halt and both push forms

Use the fetched opcode to select halt, PUSH8 or PUSH32. These are native branches to interpreter labels, not direct guest jumps to native addresses. The handlers remain temporary returns until their later edits; selecting a path is not yet executing its complete guest effect.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch in `dispatch_constants`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

```asm
    beq $t9, $zero, vm_halt
    nop
    li $t1, 1
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_push8
    nop
    li $t1, 2
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_push32
    nop
```

## Expected observation

A halt byte reaches vm_halt without retiring at this unfinished stage.
