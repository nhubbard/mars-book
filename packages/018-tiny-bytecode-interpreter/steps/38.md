# Check a zero divisor before a wide quotient

Reject a zero right value before executing ddiv. Otherwise calculate the signed doubleword quotient and transfer to the shared word check. The quotient can exceed the guest range even when doubleword division itself succeeds, as with minimum word divided by minus one.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch in `divide`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

```asm
    beq $t2, $zero, zero_divisor
    nop
    ddiv $t3, $t1, $t2
    beq $zero, $zero, vm_check_word
    nop
```

## Expected observation

For minus seven and three, t3 holds minus two while the still-unfinished result check leaves both operands live. Zero division already reports minus eight.
