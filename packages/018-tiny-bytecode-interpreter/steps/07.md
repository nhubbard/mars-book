# Recognize the one-byte stack operations

Recognize decimal 32 through 35 as the four stack opcodes. They also have encoded size one. Structural validity is independent of whether the future execution stack has enough live values; a well-formed DROP can still fault if execution later reaches it with depth zero.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch in `format_stack`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

```asm
    li $t2, 32
    beq $t9, $t2, format_size
    nop
    li $t2, 33
    beq $t9, $t2, format_size
    nop
    li $t2, 34
    beq $t9, $t2, format_size
    nop
    li $t2, 35
    beq $t9, $t2, format_size
    nop
```

## Expected observation

An OVER byte selects the size-one path; its two-value requirement is not yet being evaluated.
