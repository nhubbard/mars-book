# Route constant records to their known sizes

Recognize PUSH32 and PUSH8 and transfer to their respective size assignments. The assignments themselves will be completed below. These paths advance over constant payloads without treating them as branch targets or opcodes. Other legal records continue to the branch tests.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch in `targets_sizes`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

```asm
    li $t2, 2
    beq $t9, $t2, target_wide_size
    nop
    li $t2, 1
    beq $t9, $t2, target_short_size
    nop
```

## Expected observation

PUSH32 selects target_wide_size; its assignment is still a temporary return at this stage.
