# Distinguish yielding from a stack fault

Set capacity two and compare fuel two with fuel three. Both calls begin fresh and reach the DUP at byte four with values zero and three. The smaller budget returns before attempting DUP. The larger call attempts it, discovers no room and preserves the two live values.

## Expected observation

Fuel two yields with PC four and fault minus one. Fuel three reports status minus six and fault four; both have depth two and retired count two.

## Check your understanding

Does the faulting DUP increase retired count?

- **A** No.
- **B** Yes, because it was decoded.
- **C** Only when its target is in range.
