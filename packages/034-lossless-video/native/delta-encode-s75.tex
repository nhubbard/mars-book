\section{Step 75 Charge triples and command headers separately}
Fill \texttt{dr\_measure\_change}. Count the consecutive nonzero XOR words after the skip, advancing the same pixel cursor. Add four plus three times that changed count to \texttt{\$s7}. The four bytes belong to the skip/change header even for a trailing skip-only command. A changed count of zero at image end is valid because its skip made progress.

Use \texttt{s75-1} and stop before \texttt{dr\_change\_measured}.

\paragraph{Observe} At the first \texttt{dr\_change\_measured} stop, cursor is one, changed count is one and accumulated payload bytes are seven. After the remaining commands, the few-change pair totals 24 payload bytes.
