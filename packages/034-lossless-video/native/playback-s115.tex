\section{Step 115 Observe time after producing the picture}
Fill \texttt{vp\_sample}. Call \texttt{video\_now} after the accepted picture and after each requested sleep. The caller's saved deadline and frame state must survive these service calls. The supplied loop returns here after waiting.

The grading timeline can make decoding finish at 1010 with deadline 1040. That reading leaves thirty milliseconds to request. A real run may instead finish after the deadline. Neither result changes the file's logical duration; it changes how much waiting is appropriate and whether this interval is late.

Use \texttt{s115-1} and stop before \texttt{vp\_sampled}.

\paragraph{Observe} The default exact timeline samples 1040 after the first picture. A waiting timeline may instead sample 1010.
