\section{Step 79 Emit XOR triples and close each command}
Fill \texttt{dr\_emit\_change}. Count consecutive nonzero scratch words, emit their red/green/blue difference bytes, and advance one pixel and three payload bytes per triple. Then store the count in the retained two-byte field. Do not serialize current colors: a delta must XOR into the old image to obtain them. When a final skip reaches 4096, write changed count zero and stop without a zero/zero terminator.

Use \texttt{s79-1} and stop before \texttt{dr\_change\_written}.

\paragraph{Observe} At the first \texttt{dr\_change\_written} stop, cursor is one and seven payload bytes exist: \texttt{00 00 00 01 00 00 01}. After all commands, the output cursor is exactly destination plus forty.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\paragraph{Check} What does the triple 00 00 01 mean in this first command?
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
  \item Replace the pixel with numeric color one.
  \item XOR one into the old blue channel.
  \item Skip one pixel without reading the old value.
\end{enumerate}
