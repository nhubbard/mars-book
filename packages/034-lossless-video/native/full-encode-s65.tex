\section{Step 65 Apply the reflected CRC bit update}
Fill \texttt{ef\_crc\_bit}. Retain the accumulator's low bit, logically shift the accumulator right once, and XOR the polynomial only when the retained bit was one. The supplied loop repeats eight updates per fed byte. Keep the accumulator's upper word zero. Use a logical doubleword right shift for the unsigned recurrence. A signed word right shift can propagate bit 31 and sign-extend the result.

Use \texttt{s65-1} and stop before \texttt{ef\_crc\_bit\_ready}.

\paragraph{Observe} At the first \texttt{ef\_crc\_bit\_ready} stop, the first header byte is zero and the accumulator is \texttt{0x92477CDF}. The supplied bit counter is still eight; its decrement follows this checkpoint.

\paragraph{Check} Which shift belongs in the reflected CRC update?
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
  \item A signed word right shift.
  \item A rotation through the previous image.
  \item A logical doubleword right shift.
\end{enumerate}
