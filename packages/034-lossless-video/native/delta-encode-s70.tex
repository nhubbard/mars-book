\section{Step 70 Admit both prediction and XOR storage}
Fill \texttt{dr\_context}. Require capacity at least twenty, next sequence below 256 and period one through 64. Require previous and scratch capacities at least 16\,384 and both pointers four-byte aligned. Do not assume that a tiny changed-pixel count removes the need for the full XOR word array. These checks precede source, previous-image and scratch access.

Use \texttt{s70-1} and stop before \texttt{dr\_context\_ready}.

\paragraph{Observe} At \texttt{dr\_context\_ready}, every native window has been admitted. The output's eventual record count is still unknown; twenty is only the smallest possible admitted capacity.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\paragraph{Check} What does the twenty-byte minimum establish?
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
  \item Every full frame will fit.
  \item Only that some record could fit; the selected record still needs admission.
  \item The frame contains at most twenty changed pixels.
\end{enumerate}
