\section{Step 51 Start record sequence state at the selected full frame}
Fill \texttt{seek\_record\_context}. Retain the chosen start in \texttt{\$s6}. Initialize the private record context: capacity 16\,384 at offset 128, expected sequence \texttt{\$s5} at 136, and the accepted period at 144. Clear its output and retained-suffix slots at 152, 160 and 168. This context belongs to nested record calls; it is distinct from the caller's final seek context. A nonzero full-frame index is valid, so its expected sequence is the chosen start rather than zero.

Use \texttt{s51-1} and stop before \texttt{seek\_replay\_ready}.

\paragraph{Observe} At \texttt{seek\_replay\_ready}, the ordinary example retains start four and initializes expected sequence four. Scratch still has no required image contents; the first replayed full frame will replace them.

\paragraph{Check} Which expected sequence belongs to a seek starting at full frame four?
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
  \item Zero.
  \item The final target plus one.
  \item Four.
\end{enumerate}
