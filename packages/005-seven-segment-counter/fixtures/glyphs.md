| Index | Glyph shape | Mask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | `3f` |
| 1 | 1 | `06` |
| 2 | 2 | `5b` |
| 3 | 3 | `4f` |
| 4 | 4 | `66` |
| 5 | 5 | `6d` |
| 6 | 6 | `7d` |
| 7 | 7 | `07` |
| 8 | 8 | `7f` |
| 9 | 9 | `6f` |
| 10 | A | `77` |
| 11 | b | `7c` |
| 12 | C | `39` |
| 13 | d | `5e` |
| 14 | E | `79` |
| 15 | F | `71` |

Masks are hexadecimal bytes. The lowercase b and d shapes distinguish them from
8 and 0 in this segment arrangement. The decimal point is off in every default mask.
