# Step 15 Design a custom glyph

Bit 7 controls the decimal point independently of the seven bars. Turning it on for the A mask changes 77 to f7. In a source byte initializer, the signed value -9 expresses that pattern without a truncation warning.

## Write or inspect

In a separate copy, change the A table entry to -9 and predict its display. Keep the submitted baseline table unless your assignment intentionally uses the custom design.

## Expected observation

The A bars remain and the decimal point is added. The bus value is the unsigned byte 247, regardless of the signed initializer used to store its bits.
