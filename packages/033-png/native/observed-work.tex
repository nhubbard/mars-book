\section{Compare complete calls on the same image}
The following counts cover one call to the complete private reference prototype,
from its entry through return. They include chunk validation and bitwise CRC,
DEFLATE table construction and decompression, Adler-32, row reconstruction, pixel
packing and ordinary call overhead. They exclude assembly, file loading and caller
inspection. Scalar and MSA modes use the same compressed input and the same ordinary
address layout in the MARS64 grader. Both return exact independently decoded pixels.

  $17\times5$, mixed filters    & 85     & 72434    & 70505    & 852.16 / 829.47                         \\
  $512\times5$, mixed filters   & 2560   & 1176035  & 1113847  & 459.39 / 435.10                         \\

\end{longtable}
The five-row files use None, Up, Sub, Average and Paeth in order. Reproduce them with
\path{packages/033-png/construction/png-fixtures/fixture.py}, specifying their width and
\texttt{--compression auto}. The full-size input comes from
\path{packages/033-png/construction/png-fixtures/gradient.py}. Its first row uses None. Every later row has
identical RGB samples, so Up represents those samples with zero residuals. Each
channel at column $x$ is $(37x+97c)\bmod256$, for channel numbers $c=0,1,2$.
The checked file occupies 2854 bytes with host zlib 1.2.12; the measurement receipt
pins its digest as well as the exact decoder and CLI sources.

The full-size image spends much of its reconstruction work in Up, and its rows have
no vector tail. The five-row images spend four of their five rows elsewhere, while
the vector packing change still applies to every complete group of sixteen pixels.
Compare the modes within each row of the table. Different rows change compression,
filter distribution and fixed overhead as well as dimensions.

The two-pixel image contains no complete vector group. Its small reduction comes from
the specialized Up tail path, which also avoids the generic predictor dispatch.
A lower total count alone does not establish that a vector addition executed.
The counts describe these prototypes, including their bitwise chunk CRC; they are
not measurements of the smoke viewer's table-driven CRC or elapsed launch time.
No exact instruction count is an acceptance condition for equivalent student solutions.
