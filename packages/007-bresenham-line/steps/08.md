# Step 8 Separate vertical magnitude from direction

Begin with direction +1. If the signed y difference is negative, negate it and choose direction −1. `bgez` has an explicit `nop` delay slot. The result is a nonnegative vertical magnitude plus a separate signed step.

## Write or inspect

Append the absolute-value and direction block.

```asm
    li $s6, 1
    bgez $s5, absolute_ready
    nop
    subu $s5, $zero, $s5
    li $s6, -1
absolute_ready:
```

## Expected observation

`$s5` is the vertical magnitude D; `$s6` is +1 or −1. For the default they are three and one.
