# 13. Convert each stride to bytes

Load each word stride and shift it left by two. One pixel word occupies four bytes. The supported stride range makes this multiplication safe in word arithmetic. Keep the two results separate; neither depends on the clipped copy width.

## Write or inspect

Append in the `strides` region. Keep the supplied labels and balanced scaffold.

```asm
    lw $s2, 16($a0)
    lw $s3, 16($a1)
    sll $s2, $s2, 2
    sll $s3, $s3, 2
```

## Expected observation

The default byte strides in s2 and s3 are sixty-four and twenty-eight.
