# Advance all three full pointers together

Advance a0, a1 and a2 by two with daddiu, consume one sample, and repeat while a3 is nonzero. Addresses need the full-width operation even though sample arithmetic fits a word. Consuming count after the complete store makes the loop invariant direct: pointers identify the next sample and a3 counts samples still to process. v0 keeps the original count.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `next_sample`. Keep surrounding labels and markers.

```asm
    daddiu $a0, $a0, 2
    daddiu $a1, $a1, 2
    daddiu $a2, $a2, 2
    addiu $a3, $a3, -1
    bne $a3, $zero, mix_sample
    nop
```

Use the `s19-*` input recipes from [the observation guide](../fixtures/guided-inputs.md). Begin a fresh Debug execution and pause at mix_ready.

## Expected observation

The scalar mixer now handles every allowed count. Two opposite clipping events return v0=2 and v1=2, with all pointers advanced four bytes.

## Check your understanding

Why must a1 advance as well as a0?

- **A** Each iteration needs the corresponding next sample from both inputs.
- **B** Advancing a0 changes a1 automatically.
- **C** The second input is a constant stream by contract.
