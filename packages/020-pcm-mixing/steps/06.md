# Reconstruct A as an unsigned halfword

Shift the high byte left by eight and OR it into the low byte. Their set-bit positions are disjoint, so OR reconstructs low+256*high exactly. The result still has an unsigned interpretation in the register. A value whose halfword sign bit is set needs another operation before signed arithmetic. The source pointer has not moved.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `join_a`. Keep surrounding labels and markers.

```asm
    sll $t1, $t1, 8
    or $t0, $t0, $t1
```
