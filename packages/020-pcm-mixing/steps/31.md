# Convert mask bits back to clipped samples

Shift the total bit count right by four, dividing by sixteen. Every halfword mask is either all zeros or all ones, so the total is an exact multiple of sixteen. This is a consequence of the comparison representation. It would be wrong for an arbitrary bit mask or for a comparison that represented true as numerical one.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `vector_lane_count`. Keep surrounding labels and markers.

```asm
    srl $t2, $t2, 4
```

Use the `s31-*` input recipes from [the observation guide](../fixtures/guided-inputs.md). Begin a fresh Debug execution and pause at mix_ready.

## Expected observation

t2 becomes three clipped samples for the default group.

## Check your understanding

Why is division by sixteen exact here?

- **A** The sample rate is a multiple of sixteen.
- **B** All samples are positive.
- **C** Each clipped lane contributes exactly sixteen one bits.
