# Replace a positive overflow with the endpoint

The high path replaces the still-exact sum with 32767, then transfers to the shared mix_clipped label. It must jump past the negative replacement. A clamp chooses one representable endpoint; it cannot retain the overflow magnitude. Keeping the count in a separate register preserves the distinction between an ordinary endpoint and a clipped result.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `upper_value`. Keep surrounding labels and markers.

```asm
    li $t0, 32767
    beq $zero, $zero, mix_clipped
    nop
```
