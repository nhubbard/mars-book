# Assemble B without changing A

Shift B’s high byte in t2 and combine it with its low byte in t1. The same byte-order rule applies to both inputs. Registers have working roles rather than permanent types; t1 now contains a numerical halfword instead of one byte. A remains in t0 and both pointers still refer to this sample.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `join_b`. Keep surrounding labels and markers.

```asm
    sll $t2, $t2, 8
    or $t1, $t1, $t2
```

B=2000 reconstructs as 2000 in t1; A remains -1000 in t0.
