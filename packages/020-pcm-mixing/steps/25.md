# Mark lanes whose two sums agree

Compare w2 and w3 with ceq.h. Equal halfwords become all one bits, hexadecimal ffff; unequal halfwords become zero. A representable sum is unchanged by either operation. An overflowing signed sum wraps to the opposite sign and cannot equal its saturated endpoint, so equality is a complete test in this two-input domain.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `vector_equal`. Keep surrounding labels and markers.

```asm
    ceq.h $w4, $w2, $w3
```

Use the `s25-*` input recipes from [the observation guide](../fixtures/guided-inputs.md). Begin a fresh Debug execution and pause at mix_ready.

## Expected observation

Equality lanes are ffff, ffff, ffff, 0000, 0000, ffff, ffff and 0000.
