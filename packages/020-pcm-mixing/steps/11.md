# Add before narrowing the result

Use addu to form the exact sum in t0. Its word operation is sufficient here because the two signed halfwords sum to a value between -65536 and 65534, within signed thirty-two-bit range. The u suffix suppresses an overflow exception; it does not reinterpret the inputs as unsigned mathematical samples. Do not store or discard high sum bits yet.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `exact_sum`. Keep surrounding labels and markers.

```asm
    addu $t0, $t0, $t1
```

Use the `s11-*` input recipes from [the observation guide](../fixtures/guided-inputs.md). Begin a fresh Debug execution and pause at mix_ready.

## Expected observation

A=30000 and B=10000 produce exact sum 40000. A=-30000 and B=-10000 produce -40000.

## Check your understanding

Why does this word addition preserve the exact sum?

- **A** Halfword inputs can never clip.
- **B** Their complete sum fits signed thirty-two bits.
- **C** addu automatically saturates at 32767.
