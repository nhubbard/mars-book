\section{Step 14 Replace a positive overflow with the endpoint}\label{pcm-s14}

The high path replaces the still-exact sum with 32767, then transfers to the shared \texttt{mix\_clipped} label. It must jump past the negative replacement. A clamp chooses one representable endpoint; it cannot retain the overflow magnitude. Keeping the count in a separate register preserves the distinction between an ordinary endpoint and a clipped result.
