\section{Step 12 Detect sums strictly above the positive endpoint}\label{pcm-s12}

Load 32767 and ask whether that endpoint is less than the exact sum. Branch to \texttt{mix\_high} only when \texttt{slt} produces one. Equality is allowed: 30000+2767 is representable and must not increase the clipping count. The branch’s following nop is retained as explicit source padding; this Release 6 lesson does not depend on a delay slot.

\subsection{Write and inspect}
