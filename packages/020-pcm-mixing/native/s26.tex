\section{Step 26 Turn equality into a clipping mask}\label{pcm-s26}

Use \texttt{nor.v} with the equality vector as both operands. This computes its bitwise complement, changing unclipped lanes to zero and clipped lanes to ffff. The operation is whole-register logic, but it preserves the established halfword boundaries because it complements each bit independently. A comparison alone had the opposite meaning from the count we need.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{vector\_invert}. Keep the surrounding labels and region markers.
