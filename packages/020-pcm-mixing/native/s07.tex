\section{Step 7 Extend A from its halfword sign bit}\label{pcm-s07}

Apply \texttt{seh} to the reconstructed halfword. It copies bit fifteen into the higher register bits, making the numerical signed value agree with the sixteen-bit sample. For the worked negative sample the register changes from positive 64536 to -1000. The stored input bytes do not change. The instruction is needed even though both loads used \texttt{lbu}.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{sign\_a}. Keep the surrounding labels and region markers.
