# Step 11 Turn west and start the third leg

Heading 270 decreases x. The next endpoint is zero, not another addition of the side length. The zero register provides that target without loading another constant.

## Write or inspect

Append the west turn and start.

```asm
    li $t1, 270
    sw $t1, 0x10($s0)
    sw $t0, 0x50($s0)
```

## Expected observation

Movement begins from (side, side) toward (0, side).

## Check your understanding

What is the west leg's x target?

- **A** Twice the side length.
- **B** The original side length.
- **C** Zero.
