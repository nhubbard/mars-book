# Step 14 Stop at the origin

The final loop polls y against zero. Its stop makes the return explicit instead of relying on the board boundary to hold a still-enabled robot in place.

## Write or inspect

Append the north polling loop.

```asm
north_wait:
    lw $t2, 0x40($s0)
    bne $t2, $zero, north_wait
    nop
    sw $zero, 0x50($s0)
north_stopped:
```

## Expected observation

The trail is closed at (0, 0), heading zero, movement disabled, pen still down.
