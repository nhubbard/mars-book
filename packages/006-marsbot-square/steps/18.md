# Step 18 Pause and rewind at a named boundary

With no instructions completing, the robot clock does not advance. The device also participates in MARS instruction checkpoints, so backstep can restore its position, countdown, and pen history alongside CPU state. The visible panel still needs to refresh after that restoration.

## Write or inspect

Use the first-corner breakpoint; inspect one-instruction forward and backstep states in a fresh Debug run.

## Expected observation

A pause itself adds no travel. Record the instruction boundary as well as the displayed position.
