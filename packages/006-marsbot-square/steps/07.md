# Step 7 Poll x and stop the east leg

The loop reloads x, compares it with the target, and repeats while they differ. `bne` is a retained delayed branch: its `nop` executes for both outcomes. Once equal, `sw` disables movement before the next heading change.

## Write or inspect

Append the polling loop, stop, and corner label.

```asm
east_wait:
    lw $t2, 0x30($s0)
    bne $t2, $s1, east_wait
    nop
east_stop:
    sw $zero, 0x50($s0)
east_stopped:
```

## Expected observation

At `east_stopped`, position is (side, 0), movement is disabled, heading is 90, and the pen remains down.
