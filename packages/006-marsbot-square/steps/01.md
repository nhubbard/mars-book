# Step 1 Read the robot clock and starting state

MarsBot advances one pixel when enough simulated instructions have completed while movement is enabled. A paused execution performs no such instructions. Its initial position is the top-left corner, not the center of the board.

## Write or inspect

Inspect the header and predict the first outward direction.

## Expected observation

A new session starts at (0, 0), heading zero, stopped, with pen up. The header requests 64 instructions per tick.

## Check your understanding

What advances the connected robot?

- **A** Elapsed wall-clock seconds alone.
- **B** Completed simulated instructions while moving.
- **C** Opening its tool tab.
