# Finalize once and preserve the result across close

At successful EOF, complement the retained raw state, close the descriptor, then place the checksum and success status in their result registers. Keep the checksum in a saved register during close because `v0` is also the syscall selector. The final branch goes directly to restoration, bypassing temporary cleanup.

## Write and inspect

Replace the marked temporary region `f_finish`, retaining surrounding labels.

```asm
    dli $t0, 0xffffffff
    xor $s5, $s5, $t0
    move $a0, $s4
    li $v0, 16
    syscall
    move $v0, $s5
    move $v1, $zero
    beq $zero, $zero, file_return
    nop
```
