# Complete and store one decoded word

Load source bytes two and three, place byte two in bits 15 through 8, and combine both with the high fields. Store the completed low word through t0. This is the first observable schedule write. Its bytes in little-endian memory have a different display order from the original block, but loading the numeric schedule word recovers the intended value.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary instructions between `# BEGIN sch_low` and `# END sch_low`, retaining markers and surrounding labels.

```asm
    lbu $t3, 2($a0)
    sll $t3, $t3, 8
    or $t2, $t2, $t3
    lbu $t3, 3($a0)
    or $t2, $t2, $t3
    sw $t2, 0($t0)
```

Use `s05-1`. Prepare each recipe in a separate image from the cumulative source through this step.

## Expected observation

The first schedule word is 61626380; later schedule words retain their old contents.
