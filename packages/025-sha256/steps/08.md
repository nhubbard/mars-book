# Compute small sigma one independently

Load the word two positions before the destination. Form the rotations by seventeen and nineteen and the logical shift by ten, placing their XOR in t4. Keep the small-sigma-zero result in t3 intact. Computing both terms before adding them makes the equation correspond to separate inspectable register values.

