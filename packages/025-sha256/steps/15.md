# Form capital sigma zero of old a

Rotate old a by two, thirteen and twenty-two bits and XOR those words into t1. Retain T1 in t0. Two distinct destination temporaries prevent the second calculation from destroying the first. The next region will add the majority term to t1.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary instructions between `# BEGIN comp_sigma0` and `# END comp_sigma0`, retaining markers and surrounding labels.

```asm
    rotr $t1, $s0, 2
    rotr $t2, $s0, 13
    xor $t1, $t1, $t2
    rotr $t2, $s0, 22
    xor $t1, $t1, $t2
```

Use `s15-1`. Prepare each recipe in a separate image from the cumulative source through this step.

## Expected observation

For the first abc round, t1 has low word ce20b47e while t0 still holds T1.
