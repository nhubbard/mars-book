# Write each expanded schedule word

Add the two small-sigma terms and the predecessors at minus sixty-four and minus twenty-eight bytes. Store the low word, advance the destination and repeat forty-eight times. All predecessor reads now refer to entries already written by decoding or an earlier expansion iteration. Completing this region replaces the provisional result with the schedule routine's normal zero status.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary instructions between `# BEGIN sch_sum` and `# END sch_sum`, retaining markers and surrounding labels.

```asm
    addu $t3, $t3, $t4
    lw $t4, -64($t0)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t4
    lw $t4, -28($t0)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t4
    sw $t3, 0($t0)
    daddiu $t0, $t0, 4
    addiu $t1, $t1, -1
    bne $t1, $zero, schedule_expand
    nop
```
