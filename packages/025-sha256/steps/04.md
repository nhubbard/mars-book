# Place the first two bytes in their word fields

Load the first two bytes without sign extension. Shift the first into bits 31 through 24 and the second into bits 23 through 16, then combine them. The source has not moved, so offsets zero and one refer to the same four-byte group. The remaining low fields will be filled by the next edit.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary instructions between `# BEGIN sch_high` and `# END sch_high`, retaining markers and surrounding labels.

```asm
    lbu $t2, 0($a0)
    lbu $t3, 1($a0)
    sll $t2, $t2, 24
    sll $t3, $t3, 16
    or $t2, $t2, $t3
```

Use `s04-1`. Prepare each recipe in a separate image from the cumulative source through this step.

## Expected observation

For the padded abc block, the low word of t2 is 61620000.
