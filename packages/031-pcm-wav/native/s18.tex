\section{Step 18 Require the supported sample width}\label{pcm-wav-s18}
Read the two-byte bits-per-sample field at payload offset fourteen and require sixteen. The later filter will interpret exactly two little-endian bytes per signed sample. Reject eight-, twenty-four- and thirty-two-bit encodings here rather than allowing them to reach a routine whose address arithmetic assumes two bytes.

Author region \texttt{p\_bits}. Use \texttt{s18-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_p\_bits}.

\paragraph{Observe} The accepted field is sixteen. Neither output bytes nor caller metadata have changed.

\paragraph{Check} Why reject a twenty-four-bit PCM description?
\begin{itemize}
  \item[A.] The published processing interface supports sixteen-bit samples only.
  \item[B.] RIFF can never contain that representation.
  \item[C.] Stereo requires eight-bit samples.
\end{itemize}
