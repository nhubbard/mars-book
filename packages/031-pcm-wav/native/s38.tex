\section{Step 38 Copy history safely within the window}\label{pcm-wav-s38}
Copy the selected fourteen-byte suffix to the window's first fourteen bytes in increasing address order. The source begins above the destination; forward copying preserves not-yet-read source bytes when the ranges overlap. This rule also handles a one-sample block, where most retained history comes from the preceding history rather than the new input.

Author region \texttt{f\_history}. Use \texttt{s38-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_f\_history}.

\paragraph{Observe} The new history is three zeros followed by 12000, zero, minus 8000 and 32767.

\paragraph{Check} Why is forward copying safe here?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Filtered samples are all zero.
\item[B.] The destination begins below the overlapping source.
\item[C.] The two regions are always disjoint.
\end{itemize}
