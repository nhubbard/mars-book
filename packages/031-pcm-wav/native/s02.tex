\section{Step 2 Bring forward the tested FIR entry}\label{pcm-wav-s02}
Copy your completed \texttt{fir\_q15} routine from the earlier FIR project into \texttt{kernel\_import}, retaining its entry label only once. Bring any private labels it uses as well. The caller supplies seven zero history samples followed by four left-channel samples. Stop at \texttt{kernel\_return} before the caller converts the result to a WAV status. Here \texttt{v0} counts processed samples; four means success for this kernel. The unimplemented kernel returns minus one, which the supplied wrapper recognizes as unfinished.

Author region \texttt{kernel\_import}. Use \texttt{s02-1} and stop at \texttt{kernel\_return}.

\paragraph{Observe} The raw kernel returns four processed samples and zero clips. Its four output codes are 1500, 1500, 500 and 4596.

\paragraph{Check} What does the raw FIR return value four mean?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Four clipped samples.
\item[B.] Four output samples were processed.
\item[C.] The WAV development status.
\end{itemize}
