\section{Step 33 Gather one channel from interleaved PCM}\label{pcm-wav-s33}
For each index in the selected block, compute the input byte offset as twice the sum of channel index and channels times the absolute frame index. Copy its two bytes into the window after the seven history samples. Byte copies preserve the signed code without requiring aligned source addresses. The kernel will interpret the assembled halfword as signed; gathering must not combine adjacent left and right samples into one channel.

Author region \texttt{f\_gather}. Use \texttt{s33-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_f\_gather}.

\paragraph{Observe} The first channel window contains seven zeros followed by 12000, zero, minus 8000 and 32767.

\paragraph{Check} What is the byte stride between successive left samples in stereo?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Four.
\item[B.] Two.
\item[C.] Eight.
\end{itemize}
