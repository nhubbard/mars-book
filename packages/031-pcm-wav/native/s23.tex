\section{Step 23 Continue beyond accepted sample bytes}\label{pcm-wav-s23}
Set the data-seen flag and move the validated next offset into \texttt{s3}. Resume the chunk walk instead of returning success. The trace has a note chunk after its PCM payload. A repeated format or data chunk, a truncated unknown payload or a missing pad there must still invalidate the complete file.

Author region \texttt{p\_record}. Use \texttt{s23-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_p\_record}.

\paragraph{Observe} The next offset is seventy-two. The walker validates the final note chunk and then reaches the complete count eighty-two.

\paragraph{Check} Does accepting data finish the parser?
\begin{itemize}

  \item[B.] No; all trailing chunks must also pass.
  \item[C.] Yes; bytes after audio are ignored.
\end{itemize}
