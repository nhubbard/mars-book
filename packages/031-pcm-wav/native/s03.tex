\section{Step 3 Read the prefix at its declared widths}\label{pcm-wav-s03}
Load the unsigned rate and channel fields from prefix offsets zero and four, and the full sixty-four-bit frame count from offset eight. Use \texttt{t0}, \texttt{t1} and \texttt{t2} for those three values in this construction. The prefix is eight-byte aligned; its fields are little-endian machine words. Do not read offsets sixteen or twenty-four: callers of the bound helper need not provide a full parser descriptor.

Author region \texttt{b\_fields}. Use \texttt{s03-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_b\_fields}.

\paragraph{Observe} The three working values are 16000, two and four; no file or sample bytes have been accessed.

\paragraph{Check} How wide is the frame-count field?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Sixty-four bits.
\item[B.] Sixteen bits.
\item[C.] Thirty-two bits.
\end{itemize}
