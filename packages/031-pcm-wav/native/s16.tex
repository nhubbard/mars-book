\section{Step 16 Verify the redundant byte rate}\label{pcm-wav-s16}
Read the four-byte byte-rate field at payload offset eight and require it to equal twice channels times sample rate. Compute the expected value from the already accepted fields. This field is redundant, but accepting inconsistent redundancy leaves different readers with different timing and extent interpretations. Retain the frame count as unknown until a data chunk supplies a length.

Author region \texttt{p\_byte\_rate}. Use \texttt{s16-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_p\_byte\_rate}.

\paragraph{Observe} The stored and computed rates are both 64000 bytes per second.

\paragraph{Check} Which expression gives the supported byte rate?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Sample rate divided by channels.
\item[B.] Four times frame count.
\item[C.] Two times channels times sample rate.
\end{itemize}
