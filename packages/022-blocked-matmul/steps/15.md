# Consume the row and column until K products are complete

Advance the left walker by four and the right walker by 4N, then increment `t2`. Continue at `naive_dot` while the index differs from K. The pre-entry zero test and unit increments make equality sufficient for this bounded loop. At exit, `t3` includes exactly K products.

```asm
    addiu $t2, $t2, 1
    bne $t2, $s1, naive_dot

```
