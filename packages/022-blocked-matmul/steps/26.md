# Clip the row-tile exclusive end

At `block_row`, form I+T in `a0`. Compare it with M and retain the smaller value. Keep the start in `s7` separate from the exclusive end. This test must work for a short matrix as well as for the final row tile of a larger matrix.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `t_row_bound`. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

```asm
    addu $a0, $s7, $s3
    sltu $t9, $s0, $a0
    beq $t9, $zero, row_bound
    nop
    move $a0, $s0
```

Use the `s26-*` recipes with your cumulative source. Pause at `matmul_done` before the caller stores the count.

## Expected observation

For M=2,T=2, `a0`=2. For M=1,T=3, the clipped end is one.
