# Reload the initialized partial sum

Load the complete doubleword through `t7` into `t4`. For L=0 it is the zero established by the initialization loop. For later reduction tiles it is the sum already contributed by earlier positions. Clearing `t4` here would silently erase that earlier work.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `t_partial`. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

```asm
    ld $t4, 0($t7)
```

## Expected observation

The first partial sum is zero; later tiles must reload their existing contribution.
