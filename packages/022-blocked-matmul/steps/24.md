# Initialize the complete product interval

Advance the zeroing pointer by eight, decrement the remaining cell count and repeat while nonzero. The preceding empty-output guard ensures the first iteration exists. At completion, every selected product byte is initialized, with no prefix or suffix writes.

## Write and inspect

```asm
    daddiu $t1, $t1, 8
    addiu $t0, $t0, -1
    bne $t0, $zero, zero_product
    nop
```
