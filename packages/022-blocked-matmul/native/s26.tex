\section{Step 26 Clip the row-tile exclusive end}\label{matmul-s26}

At \texttt{block\_row}, form I+T in \texttt{a0}. Compare it with M and retain the smaller value. Keep the start in \texttt{s7} separate from the exclusive end. This test must work for a short matrix as well as for the final row tile of a larger matrix.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{t\_row\_bound}. Keep its surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    addu $a0, $s7, $s3
    sltu $t9, $s0, $a0
    beq $t9, $zero, row_bound
    nop
    move $a0, $s0
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s26-1}, \texttt{s26-2} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{matmul\_done} before the caller stores the returned count.

\subsection{Expected observation}
For M=2,T=2, \texttt{a0}=2. For M=1,T=3, the clipped end is one.

\subsection{Check your understanding}
Which row index is excluded from this tile?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} I.
  \item \textbf{B} Every index below I.
  \item \textbf{C} The exclusive end min(I+T,M).
