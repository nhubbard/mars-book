\section{Step 15 Consume the row and column until K products are complete}\label{matmul-s15}

Advance the left walker by four and the right walker by 4N, then increment \texttt{t2}. Continue at \texttt{naive\_dot} while the index differs from K. The pre-entry zero test and unit increments make equality sufficient for this bounded loop. At exit, \texttt{t3} includes exactly K products.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{n\_reduce}. Keep its surrounding labels and region markers.

\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    daddiu $t4, $t4, 4

    bne $t2, $s1, naive_dot
    nop

Use \texttt{s15-1}, \texttt{s15-2} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{matmul\_done} before the caller stores the returned count.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The first default sum is 58 after three products; a two-term minimum-value case sums to 2147483648.
