\section{Step 9 Form the right-column address and row stride}\label{matmul-s09}

The first pair in column j is B[0,j], so multiply \texttt{t1} by four and add it to the right base. Keep the resulting walker in \texttt{t5}. Compute 4N in \texttt{t6} once for this dot product; that stride moves from one right-input row to the next without changing the selected column.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{n\_right}. Keep its surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    dsll $t5, $t1, 2
    daddu $t5, $s5, $t5
    dsll $t6, $s2, 2
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s09-1} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{matmul\_done} before the caller stores the returned count.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The first right walker addresses right[0], and \texttt{t6} is eight bytes for N=2.
