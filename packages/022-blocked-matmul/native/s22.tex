\section{Step 22 Count the product cells to initialize}\label{matmul-s22}

Compute M*N in \texttt{t0} and copy the full output base to \texttt{t1}. This count is a number of doublewords, not bytes. The output pointer will advance by eight for each zeroed cell. Prior output contents may come from another call and cannot serve as initial sums.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{t\_zero\_setup}. Keep its surrounding labels and region markers.

Use \texttt{s22-1} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{matmul\_done} before the caller stores the returned count.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The default zeroing count is four and \texttt{t1} addresses the supplied product.
