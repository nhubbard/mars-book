\section{Step 35 Address the left input at the reduction-tile start}\label{matmul-s35}

Compute i*K+L, scale by four, and add the A base. This walker begins at the current reduction tile rather than at zero. It must be reconstructed for every output cell because the previous dot segment advanced its own walker.
