# Expand the low five-bit field

Mask the low five bits directly, then use left shift three and right shift two to repeat their high bits. The original packed word remains available in t0, so channel extraction does not depend on the destructive shifts used for other temporary values.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `unpack_blue`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

```asm
    andi $t3, $t0, 31
    sll $t4, $t3, 3
    srl $t3, $t3, 2
    or $t3, $t3, $t4
```

Use the `s16-1` scenes in [the guided input recipes](../fixtures/guided-inputs.md). Set the listed source bytes and count, then run the supplied caller to its named checkpoint.
