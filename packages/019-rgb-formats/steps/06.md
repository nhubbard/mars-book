# Place the retained red bits

Shift the red byte right by three to discard its low bits. Shift the retained five-bit value left by eleven to place it in the high field of the packed halfword. Both shifts operate on a zero-extended byte, so the result cannot spill outside bits fifteen through eleven.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `pack_red`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

```asm
    srl $t0, $t0, 3
    sll $t0, $t0, 11
```
