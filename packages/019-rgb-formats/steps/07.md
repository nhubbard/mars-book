# Keep six green bits

Discard two green low bits and position the resulting six-bit value at bit five. Green has a different retained width from red and blue. Reusing the red shift count here would silently remove one of the green levels that the format can represent.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `pack_green`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

```asm
    srl $t1, $t1, 2
    sll $t1, $t1, 5
```
