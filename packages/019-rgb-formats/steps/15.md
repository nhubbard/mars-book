# Mask the six-bit green field

Shift the packed word right by five, then mask with 63 to exclude the red field that remains above green. Expand the six-bit value using left shift two and right shift four. A shift alone locates green but does not isolate it from red.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `unpack_green`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

```asm
    srl $t2, $t0, 5
    andi $t2, $t2, 63
    sll $t3, $t2, 2
    srl $t2, $t2, 4
    or $t2, $t2, $t3
```
