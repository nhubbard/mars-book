# Write low byte before high byte

Store the low byte of the packed value, shift the value right by eight and store the high byte at the next address. Byte stores keep the path valid at arbitrary destination byte alignment and change only two output bytes. The temporary packed register is consumed by the shift; the return count is separate.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `pack_store`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

```asm
    sb $t0, 0($a1)
    srl $t0, $t0, 8
    sb $t0, 1($a1)
```
