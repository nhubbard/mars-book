# Repeat the high red bits

Extract the top five-bit field by shifting the packed value right by eleven. Keep one copy shifted left by three and another shifted right by two. OR the copies to fill an eight-bit component. This reproduces the specified bit-repetition rule rather than leaving low positions zero.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `unpack_red`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

```asm
    srl $t1, $t0, 11
    sll $t2, $t1, 3
    srl $t1, $t1, 2
    or $t1, $t1, $t2
```
