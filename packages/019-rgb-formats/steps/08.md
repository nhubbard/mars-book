# Retain blue in the low field

Shift blue right by three and leave it in the low five positions. It needs no positioning shift because the packed blue field begins at bit zero. The three temporary registers now contain nonoverlapping contributions, ready to combine without carries between channels.

## Write or inspect

```asm
    srl $t2, $t2, 3
```
