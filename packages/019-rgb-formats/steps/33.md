# Isolate red, green and blue lanes

Extract red with a halfword shift by eleven. Shift green by five and mask with a vector of 63, then mask the original packed values with 31 for blue. The masks are halfword values, so their high bytes are zero. This removes other fields before expansion.

## Write or inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `vector_unpack_fields`. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

```asm
    srli.h $w1, $w0, 11
    srli.h $w2, $w0, 5
    ldi.h $w7, 63
    and.v $w2, $w2, $w7
    ldi.h $w7, 31
    and.v $w3, $w0, $w7
```

Use the `s33-1` scenes in [the guided input recipes](../fixtures/guided-inputs.md). Set the listed source bytes and count, then run the supplied caller to its named checkpoint.

## Expected observation

w1, w2 and w3 contain the retained five-, six- and five-bit channel fields.

## Check your understanding

Why is green masked after shifting?

- **A** To swap byte order.
- **B** To restore the original low bits.
- **C** To remove the red bits that remain above it.
