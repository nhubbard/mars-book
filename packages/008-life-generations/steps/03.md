# Step 3 Distinguish the three row strides

The seed has eight bytes per row. A working board includes a dead halo and has ten. The framebuffer has eight words per row, or thirty-two bytes. These are different representations of the same logical cells.

## Write or inspect

Locate the allocations and the stride table.

## Expected observation

Working interior (0,0) is at offset eleven; framebuffer (0,0) is at offset zero.
