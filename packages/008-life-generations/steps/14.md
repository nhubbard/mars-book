# Step 14 Count exactly eight source neighbors

The relative byte offsets are −11, −10, −9, −1, +1, +9, +10, and +11. Each `lbu` reads the stable source, and `addu` adds its binary contribution. Offset zero is deliberately absent.

```asm

    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, -9($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, -1($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1

neighbors_counted:
```

At the first default cell, the neighbor count is zero. Every read remains within the padded allocation.
