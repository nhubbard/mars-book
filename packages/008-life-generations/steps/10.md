# Step 10 Map zero and one into color words

For binary c, `subu` zero,c gives zero when dead and all-one bits when alive. AND with 0x00ffffff produces black or white. The full word store clears an old live pixel when its new state is dead.

## Write or inspect

Append the render-cell body.

```asm
    lbu $t4, 0($t0)
    subu $t4, $zero, $t4
    and $t4, $t4, $t5
    sw $t4, 0($t1)
    daddiu $t0, $t0, 1
    daddiu $t1, $t1, 4
```

## Expected observation

The first default cell is black; no other frame cell has been rendered at this partial stage.

## Check your understanding

Why store zero for a dead cell?

- **A** Zero terminates every program.
- **B** A previous live pixel must be erased.
- **C** Dead cells have no framebuffer address.
