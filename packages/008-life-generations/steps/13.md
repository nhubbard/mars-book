# Step 13 Address matching source and destination cells

The physical offset is ten times the interior row number plus its interior column, both from one through eight. Shifts by three and one form 8×row+2×row. Adding the same offset to the two bases gives corresponding cells.

## Write or inspect

Append the destination-sweep setup and address calculation.

```asm
    li $s4, 1
cell_row:
    li $s5, 1
cell_loop:
    sll $t0, $s4, 3
    sll $t1, $s4, 1
    addu $t0, $t0, $t1
    addu $t0, $t0, $s5
    daddu $s7, $s0, $t0
    daddu $t7, $s1, $t0
```

## Expected observation

The first source cell is current+11 and the first destination cell is other+11.
