# Step 9 Prepare a complete frame render

Rendering begins at the current board's first interior byte and the frame base. The board cursor advances in bytes; the output cursor advances in four-byte words. White is the constant color for live cells.

## Write or inspect

Append the rendering cursor setup.

```asm
render:
    daddiu $t0, $s0, 11
    la $t1, frame
    li $t2, 8
    li $t5, 0x00ffffff
render_row:
    li $t3, 8
render_cell:
```

## Expected observation

The renderer has independent source and frame cursors.
