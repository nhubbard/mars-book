# Step 7 Close the copy loops and skip halo columns

After eight cells, the destination cursor must skip two bytes before the next interior row: the current row's right halo and the next row's left halo. The seed needs no such skip. Both retained branches have `nop` delay slots.

## Write or inspect

Append the copy-loop control.

```asm
    addiu $t3, $t3, -1
    bne $t3, $zero, seed_cell
    nop
    daddiu $t1, $t1, 2
    addiu $t2, $t2, -1
    bne $t2, $zero, seed_row
    nop
seed_copied:
```

## Expected observation

At `seed_copied`, all 64 interior bytes equal the seed; halos remain zero.
