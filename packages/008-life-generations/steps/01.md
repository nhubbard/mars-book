# Step 1 Read the local update rule

A cell's next state depends on its current state and the eight surrounding cells in the same generation. Three neighbors cause birth or survival; two preserve an already live cell. All other counts produce zero.

## Write or inspect

Apply the rule table to one live and one dead center.

## Expected observation

A dead center with two neighbors stays dead; a live center with two survives.

## Check your understanding

Is the center included in its own neighbor count?

- **A** Yes, always.
- **B** No; only the eight surrounding cells count.
- **C** Only at the edge.
