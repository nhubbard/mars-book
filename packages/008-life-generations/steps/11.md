# Step 11 Finish rendering and publish the checkpoint

Close the row and column loops, skip the board's halo columns, then store the working generation number. `generation_ready` marks the boundary after both board and frame are complete.

## Write or inspect

Append the render-loop control and publication fragment. Remove the starter's duplicate `generation_ready`/`nop` placeholder.

```asm
    addiu $t3, $t3, -1
    bne $t3, $zero, render_cell
    nop
    daddiu $t0, $t0, 2
    addiu $t2, $t2, -1
    bne $t2, $zero, render_row
    nop
    la $t0, generation
    sw $s3, 0($t0)
generation_ready:
```

## Expected observation

At the first ready checkpoint, generation is zero and the frame shows the seed.
