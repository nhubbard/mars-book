# Step 5 Prepare to copy the seed interior

The first interior byte is eleven bytes after the board base. The copy walks eight cells per row and uses separate row and column counters.

## Write or inspect

Append the seed-copy cursor setup.

```asm
    la $t0, seed
    daddiu $t1, $s0, 11
    li $t2, 8
seed_row:
    li $t3, 8
seed_cell:
```

## Expected observation

The input cursor starts at seed and the destination cursor at `board_a`+11.

Why is the first destination not `board_a` itself?

- **A** Offset zero is part of the dead halo.
- **B** The first eleven cells are always alive.
- **C** The board is word-addressed.
