# Test local logic and whole-board behavior at different scales

A center and its eight neighbors contain nine bits, so there are 512 local binary
configurations. Exhausting them at an interior position tests every local rule input.
Corners have only four in-board positions including the center, and edge midpoints
have six, so their realizable local patterns can also be exhausted.

Those tests do not enumerate every 64-bit complete board or every future trajectory.
The private suite also evolves varied global patterns through zero to four steps,
including sparse, dense, stable, oscillating, and edge-touching states. An independent
oracle scatters contributions from live cells into an unpadded neighbor-count array.
It does not reproduce the assembly's halo gather loop.

At every public checkpoint, the checks inspect the actual current and previous board
allocations and every frame word. Between checkpoints, an execution-store trace rejects
any write to the current source, including a later-restored write. A student-maintained
log of intended generations is not substituted for these observed states.
