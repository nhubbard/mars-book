# Step 12 Exchange both words before publication

Keep both loaded values in temporaries until their opposite stores are complete. Decrement the inner index, update swap metadata, call the renderer, and return to the adjacent comparison.

## Write or inspect

Append this fragment in insertion_inner.

```asm
    sw $t2, -4($t0)
    sw $t1, 0($t0)
insertion_swapped:
    addiu $s3, $s3, -1
    addiu $s6, $s6, 1
    la $t0, swap_count
    sw $s6, 0($t0)
    li $t0, 1
    la $t1, phase
    sw $t0, 0($t1)
    move $a0, $s0
    move $a1, $s1
    jal render
    nop
    beq $zero, $zero, insertion_inner
    nop
```
