# Step 17 Exchange a selected minimum once

If the minimum is already at the suffix start, skip a self-exchange. Otherwise load and exchange the two selected words, increment swap metadata, and render the complete new array.

## Write or inspect

Insert this fragment in selection_exchange.

```asm
    beq $s4, $s2, selection_next
    nop
    sll $t0, $s2, 2
    daddu $t0, $s0, $t0
    lw $t1, 0($t0)
    sll $t2, $s4, 2
    daddu $t2, $s0, $t2
    lw $t3, 0($t2)
    sw $t3, 0($t0)
    sw $t1, 0($t2)
selection_swapped:
    addiu $s6, $s6, 1
    la $t0, swap_count
    sw $s6, 0($t0)
    li $t0, 1
    la $t1, phase
    sw $t0, 0($t1)
    move $a0, $s0
    move $a1, $s1
    jal render
    nop
```

## Expected observation

The first selection exchange gives [−4,−2,3,2,0,1,−3,−1].
