# Choose the negative endpoint

Load -32768 into t0 for the lower-overflow path. It falls through to scalar_clipped, sharing the increment with positive overflow. In-range flow already bypasses this path. The exact negative endpoint and a clipped negative result therefore remain distinguishable through their count even though their stored bytes agree.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `lower_clamp`. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

```asm
    li $t0, -32768
```

Use the `s20-*` input recipes in [the observation guide](../fixtures/guided-inputs.md). Start a fresh Debug execution and pause at filter_ready.

A large negative rounded result becomes -32768 with v1 still zero at the next unfinished region.
