# Add half a scale unit once

Add 16384 to the complete accumulator with daddiu. This is half of 32768 in accumulator units. It prepares one final nearest rounding with ties toward positive infinity. Adding the bias once per product would change the filter; the completed eight-tap loop must reach this region only after all products have accumulated.
