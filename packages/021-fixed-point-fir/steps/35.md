# Form two exact doubleword pair sums

Apply dotp_s.d to the first four widened sample/coefficient words. Its two destination lanes sum products zero/one and two/three. The d suffix describes doubleword results; the multiplied inputs are signed words. In this bounded domain, both pair sums are exact, including positive 2147483648 that cannot be held as a signed word result.
