# Add the product without narrowing the sum

Add t7 into t0 using daddu. A single product fits a signed word, but a partial sum of two maximum positive products does not. Doubleword accumulation preserves every allowed partial sum. The tap count is still eight here because consuming a tap belongs to the next region, after its product has contributed.

```asm

```
