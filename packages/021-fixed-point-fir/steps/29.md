# Widen the first four coefficient lanes

Interleave w1’s low halfwords with their corresponding mask halfwords into w3. Operand order makes each original code the low half of a word and its mask the high half. In little-endian interpretation this sign-extends the value. Zero extension would change a negative coefficient into a positive number before multiplication.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `coefficient_low_words`. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

```asm
    ilvr.h $w3, $w2, $w1
```
