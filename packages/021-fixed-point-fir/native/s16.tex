\section{Step 16 Use an arithmetic shift for floor division}\label{fir-s16}

Shift \texttt{t0} right arithmetically by fifteen with \texttt{dsra}. Together with the bias, this implements
\[R=\left\lfloor\frac{S+16384}{32768}\right\rfloor.\]
A negative biased value must round downward under the division, so truncation toward zero would be wrong. A logical shift would replace the sign with zeros and produce a large positive value.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{round\_scale}. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The three tie-adjacent recipes produce rounded codes one, zero and -1. The maximum eight-product sum produces 262144 before saturation.
