    \begin{enumerate}
      \setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
            \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
      \item The next \texttt{daddiu} at \texttt{fir\_round} will add the rounding bias to the completed sum.
      \item Initialized counters \texttt{v0 = 1}, \texttt{v1 = 0} precede the function return.
      \item \texttt{t0} is $8589934592=2^{33}$. The exact sum exceeds 32-bit capacity.
      \item \texttt{w7} contains two integer lanes of 4294967296 (\texttt{0x0000000100000000}); their sum is $2^{33}$.
      \item All eight inputs are $-32768$ (\texttt{00 80}); this detail shows six of the eight codes.
      \item Eight coefficients of $-32768$ yield eight products of $2^{30}$, totaling $2^{33}$ before scaling.
      \item Output at \texttt{0x10010114} is zero; continuing rounds to 262144, then saturates to 32767.
      \item Count is 1; saved results are 99. Publication follows return at \texttt{filter\_ready}.
    \end{enumerate}
