\section{Step 15 Add half a scale unit once}\label{fir-s15}

Add 16384 to the complete accumulator with \texttt{daddiu}. This is half of 32768 in accumulator units. It prepares one final nearest rounding with ties toward positive infinity. Adding the bias once per product would change the filter; the completed eight-tap loop must reach this region only after all products have accumulated.
