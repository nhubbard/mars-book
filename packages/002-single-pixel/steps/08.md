# Step 8 Replace the lowest-addressed byte

sb writes one byte to the specified address. At offset zero in little-endian memory, that byte is the least significant byte of the word, which this format uses for blue. The other three bytes retain their prior values.

## Write or inspect

Append the byte store and final checkpoint label.

```asm
modify:
    sb $t2, 0($t0)
final_ready:
```

## Expected observation

The final bytes are ff, 66, 33, and 00. The word is 0x003366ff, so red and green remain 51 and 102 while blue becomes 255.

## Check your understanding

What is the final packed word?

- **A** 0x000000ff because sb clears the rest of the word.
- **B** 0x003366ff.
- **C** 0xff336699 because offset zero always means the highest byte.
