\section{Accumulate a full eight-limb product}
Multiplying the two expansions for \(A\) and \(B\) gives partial products
\(a_i b_j \beta^{i+j}\). Each pair contributes at output limb \(i+j\), then carries
into the next limb. Initialize all eight output words to zero. For one fixed \(i\),
start a carry \(c=0\) and visit \(j=0,1,2,3\). At each inner iteration calculate
\[
T=a_i b_j+z_{i+j}+c,\qquad
z_{i+j}=T\bmod\beta,\qquad c=\lfloor T/\beta\rfloor.
\]
Even though the final product needs 256 bits, this individual temporary fits in
64 unsigned bits. Its maximum is
\((\beta-1)^2+(\beta-1)+(\beta-1)=\beta^2-1\). The bound includes both the existing output limb
and the incoming carry. A signed-overflow-trapping addition is inappropriate for
values that can cross \(2^{63}-1\) while remaining inside this bound.

In the following fragment, \texttt{t1} holds \(a_i\), \texttt{t2} is the byte offset
\(4j\), \texttt{t0} is \(4i\), \texttt{t3} is the carry, \texttt{a1} points to the
second operand, and \texttt{a2} points to the product. Keep the complete temporary
in \texttt{t4} until extracting both its low limb and its high carry.

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{Accumulate one partial product without discarding its high word.}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
daddu $t4, $a1, $t2
    lwu $t4, 0($t4)
    dmulu $t4, $t4, $t1
    daddu $t5, $t0, $t2
    daddu $t5, $a2, $t5
    lwu $t6, 0($t5)
    daddu $t4, $t4, $t6
    daddu $t4, $t4, $t3
    sw $t4, 0($t5)
    dsrl $t3, $t4, 32
\end{minted}

At the end of a row, store the carry in limb \(i+4\). That position has not yet
received a previous row's final carry; the inner loop has consumed earlier
contributions through limb \(i+3\). The next row starts with carry zero, while
retaining the output words already accumulated.

For \(A=B=2\cdot2^{32}-1\), the eight output limbs, listed from low to high in
hexadecimal, must be
\[
[\mathtt{00000001},\mathtt{fffffffc},\mathtt{00000003},0,0,0,0,0].
\]
Use this square to inspect a nonzero carry across the first two columns. Then square
\(2^{128}-1\); its low-to-high result is
\[
[1,0,0,0,\mathtt{fffffffe},\mathtt{ffffffff},
 \mathtt{ffffffff},\mathtt{ffffffff}].
\]
Check all eight words. A test that reads only the low four words cannot establish
that full multiplication works.
