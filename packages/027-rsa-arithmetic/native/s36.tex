\section{Step 36 Guard the multiword power entry}\label{rsa-s36}

Test the full modulus before reading the base or exponent. The public interface accepts a full 128-bit exponent, so its eventual termination test will inspect every limb. Preserve the distinction between this exponent object and the zero-extended 32-bit scalar exponent from the earlier routine.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{power\_guard}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    lwu $t0, 0($a2)
    lwu $t1, 4($a2)
    lwu $t2, 8($a2)
    lwu $t3, 12($a2)
    or $t0, $t0, $t1
    or $t0, $t0, $t2
    or $t0, $t0, $t3
    beqz $t0, rsa_invalid
    nop
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s36-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{pow128\_frame} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
A zero modulus returns an error before invalid base, exponent or output pointers can be dereferenced.
