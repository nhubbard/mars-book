\section{Step 42 Interpret schemes after the arithmetic}\label{rsa-s42}

Return to the OAEP and PSS discussion after the numerical routines agree. The toy modular power supplies no message encoding or signature verification scheme. Retain this distinction when describing the completed project, and keep any later key-generation or scheme work under a separate contract.

\subsection{Expected observation}
A modular power is not itself an encryption or signature scheme.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
What remains outside the arithmetic lab?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Standardized scheme encoding and its complete key-handling behavior.
\item \textbf{B} The low-limb byte order.
\item \textbf{C} The need to retain a carry in multiplication.
\end{itemize}
