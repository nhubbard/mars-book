\section{Step 15 Place the row carry and continue}\label{rsa-s15}

Store the final carry in the next column, advance the first operand's offset and begin another row if necessary. The destination retains previous rows. At the final boundary inspect all eight words of the square, including high words that happen to be zero in this small fixture.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{product\_rows}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

Use recipe \texttt{s15-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{after\_product\_rows} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.
