\section{Step 11 Initialize every product column}\label{rsa-s11}

Clear all eight output limbs before accumulating a row. A caller can reuse a buffer containing any byte pattern, including a previous larger product. The clear loop uses a byte offset and stops after 32 bytes; its pointer arithmetic retains full addresses.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{product\_clear}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    move $t0, $zero
mul_clear:
    daddu $t1, $a2, $t0
    sw $zero, 0($t1)
    daddiu $t0, $t0, 4
    sltiu $t1, $t0, 32
    bnez $t1, mul_clear
    nop
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s11-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{after\_product\_clear} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
All eight product words are zero, with the byte after them still a sentinel.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why clear the high four words too?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} The high half is outside the result contract.
  \item \textbf{B} Clearing only one byte resets an entire word.
  \item \textbf{C} Later partial products add into those columns.
\end{itemize}
