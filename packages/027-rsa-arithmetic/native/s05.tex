\section{Step 5 Check the inverse on paper}\label{rsa-s05}

Use the four Euclidean divisions to substitute backwards from remainder one. Keep the signed coefficients separate from the unsigned gcd values. Verify 17 times 2753 equals 15 times 3120 plus one before using those fixed exponents in the toy calculation.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The coefficient of 17 is minus 367, equivalent to 2753 modulo 3120.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
What does the gcd alone establish about an inverse?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} The complete inverse coefficient automatically.
  \item \textbf{B} The byte length of an RSA message.
  \item \textbf{C} Whether the operands are coprime.
\end{itemize}
