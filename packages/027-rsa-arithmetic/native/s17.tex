\section{Step 17 Audit the full product width}\label{rsa-s17}

Read the complete product of two all-ones 128-bit inputs and compare all eight limbs. Keep the same observation after the smaller worked square passes. Separately place data and code above 4 GiB: that changes addresses without changing the integer products.

\subsection{Expected observation}
High product words and full return addresses participate in the check.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Does a correct low half prove the full product?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Yes, because multiplication is commutative.
\item \textbf{B} Only if the inputs are aligned.
\item \textbf{C} No; the high half must also be checked.
\end{itemize}
