\section{Step 20 Decide whether one subtraction is required}\label{rsa-s20}

A carried sum always needs subtraction. Otherwise compare from the most significant limb downward, stopping at the first unequal pair. Equality also requires subtraction. The small no-subtraction recipe reaches the done label with result two; the next region still owns the normal return.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{add\_compare}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

\Needspace{22\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    bnez $t1, addmod_subtract
    nop
    li $t0, 12
addmod_compare:
    daddu $t2, $a3, $t0
    lwu $t3, 0($t2)
    daddu $t2, $a2, $t0
    lwu $t4, 0($t2)
    sltu $t5, $t3, $t4
    bnez $t5, addmod_done
    nop
    sltu $t5, $t4, $t3
    bnez $t5, addmod_subtract
    nop
    addiu $t0, $t0, -4
    bgez $t0, addmod_compare
    nop
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s20-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{addmod\_done} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
For 1 plus 1 modulo 17, the low result remains two at the done boundary.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
When does equality with the modulus require subtraction?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Only if the modulus is negative.
\item \textbf{B} Never, because equality is already a reduced residue.
\item \textbf{C} Always, because the residue must be below the modulus.
\end{itemize}
