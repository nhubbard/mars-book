\section{Step 6 Initialize a residue identity}\label{rsa-s06}

Reject modulus zero before any output access. Reduce the base, then reduce the initial result one by the same modulus. The second reduction handles modulus one before the loop. Retain a copy of the exponent as the loop's remaining work.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{word\_identity}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    beqz $a2, rsa_invalid
    nop
    dmodu $t0, $a0, $a2
    li $t1, 1
    dmodu $t1, $t1, $a2
    move $t2, $a1
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s06-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{pow32\_loop} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Before the loop, base 65, result 1 and exponent 17 are separate numerical values.
