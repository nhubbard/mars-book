# Transfer the multiplier across 64-bit halves

Shift the private multiplier logically. Transfer the low bit of its high doubleword into bit 63 of the low doubleword. Test both halves after the shift: a zero low half does not imply that the 128-bit multiplier is zero. If bits remain, the next region will double the addend.

Replace only the body of region `modproduct_shift`, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

```asm
mulmod_shift:
    ld $t0, 16($sp)
    ld $t1, 24($sp)
    dsrl $t0, $t0, 1
    dsll $t2, $t1, 63
    or $t0, $t0, $t2
    dsrl $t1, $t1, 1
    sd $t0, 16($sp)
    sd $t1, 24($sp)
    or $t2, $t0, $t1
    beqz $t2, mulmod_done
    nop
```
