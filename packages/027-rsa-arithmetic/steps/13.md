# Split one widened partial sum

Form a 64-bit product, add the existing output limb and the previous carry, then split the temporary at bit 32. Store its low word before using a logical shift to recover the high carry. The earlier bound explains why this entire sum fits unsigned 64-bit arithmetic.

Replace only the body of region `product_partial`, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

```asm
mul_inner:
    daddu $t4, $a1, $t2
    lwu $t4, 0($t4)
    dmulu $t4, $t4, $t1
    daddu $t5, $t0, $t2
    daddu $t5, $a2, $t5
    lwu $t6, 0($t5)
    daddu $t4, $t4, $t6
    daddu $t4, $t4, $t3
    sw $t4, 0($t5)
    dsrl $t3, $t4, 32
```
