# Establish a valid modular-addition entry

Combine all four modulus limbs to test for zero. Do not test only the low word: a positive modulus can have that word zero. Once the modulus is known nonzero, initialize the result offset and carry. The caller remains responsible for supplying two residues below that modulus.

```asm
    lwu $t0, 0($a2)
    lwu $t1, 4($a2)
    lwu $t2, 8($a2)
    lwu $t3, 12($a2)
    or $t0, $t0, $t1
    or $t0, $t0, $t2
    or $t0, $t0, $t3
    beqz $t0, rsa_invalid
    nop
    move $t0, $zero
    move $t1, $zero
```
