# Propagate the subtraction borrow

Subtract the modulus limb and incoming borrow from each zero-extended result limb. Keep the signed 64-bit temporary until extracting its borrow bit. Its bounded range justifies that extraction. Store the low word and move upward, then return success after either the subtracting or no-subtraction path.

Replace only the body of region `add_borrow`, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

```asm
addmod_subtract:
    move $t0, $zero
    move $t1, $zero
addmod_borrow:
    daddu $t2, $a3, $t0
    lwu $t3, 0($t2)
    daddu $t4, $a2, $t0
    lwu $t4, 0($t4)
    dsubu $t3, $t3, $t4
    dsubu $t3, $t3, $t1
    dsrl $t1, $t3, 63
    sw $t3, 0($t2)
    daddiu $t0, $t0, 4
    sltiu $t2, $t0, 16
    bnez $t2, addmod_borrow
    nop
addmod_done:
    move $v0, $zero
    jr $ra
    nop
```
