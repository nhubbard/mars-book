# Step 14: Limit the f4 upper bound

For four-byte leads other than f0, distinguish f4. Its next byte can be at most decimal 143, hexadecimal 8f. Leads f1 through f3 retain ordinary bounds. Every accepted lead jumps past the other class setup.
