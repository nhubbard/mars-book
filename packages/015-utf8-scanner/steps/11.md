# Step 11: Dispatch a three-byte lead

After excluding the two-byte class, compare with 240, hexadecimal f0. Candidates below it are e0 through ef and branch to the two-continuation setup. That setup will distinguish e0 and ed from the ordinary three-byte leads.

## Write or inspect

Append the instructions in the `three_dispatch` region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate register observations do not yet establish the final function contract.

```asm
    sltiu $t9, $t8, 240
    bne $t9, $zero, scan_three
    nop
```

## Expected observation

The number of required bytes and the first-tail bounds are separate decisions.
