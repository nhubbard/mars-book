# Byte positions and delivery boundaries

The hexadecimal fixture is `41 e2 82 ac f0 9f 99 82 00 c3 a9`, at decimal offsets
zero through ten. Its encodings cover offsets 0, 1..3, 4..7, 8 and 9..10. Calls
contain offsets 0..1, 2..4, 5..6 and 7..10. Scalar spans and call boundaries do not
coincide. The native chapter includes a labelled vector diagram, not an IDE image.
