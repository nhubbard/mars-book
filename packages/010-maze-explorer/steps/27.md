# Step 27 Separate search work from publication work

A cell is enqueued once and has at most four candidate neighbors. The search work is linear in reachable cells and their edges, but this teaching program redraws 64 pixels after every processing step.

## Write or inspect

## Expected observation

Whole-program instruction counts include repeated frame construction. They are not a direct measure of only the graph algorithm, elapsed time, or processor cycles.
