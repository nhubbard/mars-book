# Step 19 Explain the layer ordering

FIFO processing places newly discovered cells after all waiting cells. Since they are one edge farther than the current cell, processing distances never decrease.

## Expected observation

The small room is processed at distances zero, one, one, two. The first discovery of nine records a shortest route even though another equally short route exists.
