# Step 8 Classify and draw one cell

Rendering is a separate calculation over walls, states, and path flags. Walls take priority, then the path mask, then the search state.

## Write or inspect

Append the renderer setup and first-cell classification. Follow the store for cell zero.

```asm
    la $t0, frame
    la $t1, walls
    la $t2, states
    la $t3, path
    li $t4, 64
render_cell:
    li $t5, 0x202020
    lbu $t6, 0($t1)
    bne $t6, $zero, render_store
    nop
    li $t5, 0x0072b2
    lbu $t6, 0($t3)
    bne $t6, $zero, render_store
    nop
    li $t5, 0xffffff
    lbu $t6, 0($t2)
    beq $t6, $zero, render_store
    nop
    li $t5, 0xe69f00
    li $t7, 1
    beq $t6, $t7, render_store
    nop
    li $t5, 0x009e73
render_store:
    sw $t5, 0($t0)
```

## Expected observation

The first cell becomes frontier orange. The other 63 frame words are still unfinished until the loop continuation is added.
