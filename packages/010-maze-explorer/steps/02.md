# Step 2 Map the small room to byte indices

The stepping fixture opens only cells zero, one, eight, and nine. All other cells are walls. This isolates a cycle and two equally short routes.

## Write or inspect

Locate the four zero bytes in `walls`. Express each cell as a row and column in an eight-column grid.

## Expected observation

Cell nine is row one, column one. Cell eight is directly below zero. Cell seven and cell eight belong to different rows and do not share a side.

## Check your understanding

Are cell seven and cell eight horizontal neighbors?

- **A** No; the row boundary lies between them.

- **B** Yes; their indices differ by one.

- **C** Only when the goal is eight.
