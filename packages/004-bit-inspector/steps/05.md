# Step 5 Add one at word width

addiu forms a non-trapping 32-bit word sum and sign-extends the result into its destination. The u does not request zero extension. For input -1, adding one produces zero. A maximum positive signed word exposes the wraparound boundary.

## Write or inspect

Append the word addition.

```asm
    addiu $s2, $s0, 1
word_added:
```

## Expected observation

For -1, `$s2` is zero. For 2147483647, it becomes 0xffffffff80000000, whose signed value is -2147483648.

## Check your understanding

What does the u mean for this addition?

- **A** The result is always a positive signed integer.
- **B** Signed overflow does not cause a trap.
- **C** The result is always zero-extended.
