# Step 8 Store the other three full-width results

Successive doublewords begin eight bytes apart. The remaining stores preserve the distinction between zero extension, word arithmetic, and doubleword arithmetic. The grader will inspect all eight bytes rather than only the low word.

## Write or inspect

Append the three stores and their checkpoint label.

```asm
    sd $s1, 8($t1)
    sd $s2, 16($t1)
    sd $s3, 24($t1)
values_stored:
```

## Expected observation

For -1, unsigned_value has low bytes ff ff ff ff and high bytes 00 00 00 00. Both plus-one fields are zero.
