# Step 13 Compare the full register values

The four formatting services report a word, so they cannot by themselves reveal a difference in the high register half. The full register view and the stored doublewords supply that evidence.

## Write or inspect

Debug the default input and stop at values_stored. Compare `$s0` with `$s1`, then compare the corresponding eight-byte fields. Resume and check the full output.

## Expected observation

`$s0` and `$s1` have different high halves, while both have low word ffffffff. Passing either as the word-format argument gives the same report.

## Check your understanding

Does matching hexadecimal console output prove two 64-bit registers are equal?

- **A** Yes; eight digits always describe a 64-bit register.
- **B** Yes; the service automatically prints hidden upper digits.
- **C** No; the service only reports their low words.
