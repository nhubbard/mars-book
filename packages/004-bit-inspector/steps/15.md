# Step 15 Compare an equivalent implementation

A 64-bit left shift by 32 followed by a logical right shift by 32 isolates the low word. A word shift by zero can normalize a wide sum to a sign-extended word result. These operations give an alternative under the same interface.

## Write or inspect

Study the alternative fixture, then implement an equivalent solution in a separate copy. The wide shift instructions below use immediate zero but their 32 suffix adds 32 to the shift distance.

```asm
    dsll32 $s1, $s0, 0
    dsrl32 $s1, $s1, 0
```

## Expected observation

The first operation moves the low word into the high half and discards the former high half. The logical right shift returns that word with zeroes above it.
