# Step 9 Print signed decimal

Service 1 prints the signed interpretation of the low 32 bits in `$a0`. The prefix and newline use service 4 as in the console chapter. The arithmetic results remain in their own registers and memory while `$v0` selects services.

## Write or inspect

Append the signed report line.

```asm
    la $a0, signed_msg
    li $v0, 4
    syscall
    move $a0, $s0
    li $v0, 1
    syscall
    la $a0, newline
    li $v0, 4
    syscall
signed_printed:
```

## Expected observation

The partial report is signed: -1 followed by one newline for the default sample.
