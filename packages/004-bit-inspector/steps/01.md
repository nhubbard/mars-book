# Step 1 Identify the input pattern

The input is the word stored at sample, not a line typed into the console. A word has 32 bits. Its signed interpretation and unsigned interpretation can differ even though its stored bits remain the same. The default -1 has all 32 bits set.

## Write or inspect

Inspect the supplied sample and result fields. Predict its four output lines and identify which parts depend on a signed interpretation.

## Expected observation

The same ffffffff pattern is signed -1 and unsigned 4294967295. Hex and binary describe the pattern directly.

## Check your understanding

Does switching from signed to unsigned interpretation change the stored word?

- **A** Yes; every bit is complemented.
- **B** Yes; the word grows automatically to eight bytes.
- **C** No; it changes the numerical interpretation of the same bits.
