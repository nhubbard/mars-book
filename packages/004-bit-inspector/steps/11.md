# Step 11 Print hexadecimal

Service 34 prints 0x and eight hexadecimal digits for the low word. A hexadecimal digit represents four bits, so eight digits describe all 32 word bits. Leading zeroes preserve that fixed width.

## Write or inspect

Append the hexadecimal line.

```asm
    la $a0, hex_msg
    li $v0, 4
    syscall
    move $a0, $s0
    li $v0, 34
    syscall
    la $a0, newline
    li $v0, 4
    syscall
hex_printed:
```

## Expected observation

The third line is hex: 0xffffffff. With a zero sample it would be hex: 0x00000000.
