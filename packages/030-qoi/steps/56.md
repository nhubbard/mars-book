# Accumulate only bytes actually read

Author l_read to request the unused suffix of the caller’s buffer. A negative result is an I/O error, zero selects EOF processing, and a positive result advances the accepted count by that result. The fixture limits each read to three bytes. Do not treat such a short positive result as EOF, and do not advance by the original request size.

Observe: After the first read, s4 is three and the buffer begins with 71 6f 69. Continuing the loop eventually reaches the unfinished EOF stage with all thirty-eight bytes buffered.
