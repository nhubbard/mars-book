# Give each cursor and predictor its own state

Author w_state to establish eight remaining pixels, input offset fourteen and payload end at offset thirty. Initialize previous color to opaque black, the previous-index marker to minus one and the cache pointer to frame offset 112. Preserve the total pixel count independently of the remaining count. Do not initialize the cache to opaque black: clearing that separate object is the next edit.

Observe: s2 and s7 are eight; s0 points to source plus fourteen; s1 points to source plus thirty. The previous color is 0xff000000.

Use observation s09-1. Native prose and typeset quizzes are in native/s09.tex.
