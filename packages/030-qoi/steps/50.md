# Emit green and two fitting residuals

Author e_luma to check the signed green range and the two red/blue residual ranges. A fitting command stores green plus thirty-two and each residual plus eight. The worked transition from 1,1,1 to 7,6,5 produces a5 97. A failed range test routes to the literal RGB body; emitting low bits of an out-of-range residual would silently change the pixel.

Observe: The output prefix has twenty-one bytes, including a5 97, and two later pixels remain.

Use observation s50-1. Native prose and typeset quizzes are in native/s50.tex.

Check: What ranges must the residuals satisfy?

**A** Zero through 255 without a bias.
**B** Minus eight through seven.
**C** Minus thirty-two through thirty-one.

Hint 1: Green and residual fields have different widths.
Hint 2: Subtract dG from dR and dB, test the biased residuals below sixteen and emit exactly two bytes on success.
