# Preflight the conservative encoder capacity

Author e_capacity to calculate the accepted pixel count and the bound twenty-two plus five times that count. Compare this bound with unsigned a3 before reading any pixel or writing the destination. A capacity below the bound returns status two even if a particular encoding could fit. Keep the remaining pixel count independently of the byte cursor.

Observe: The eight-pixel bound is sixty-two; the prepared capacity accepts it. No header byte has been stored.
