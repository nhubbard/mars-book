# Combine green change with its residuals

Author w_luma to require its second payload byte, recover green change from the low six tag bits and split the second byte into two residual nibbles. Subtract biases thirty-two and eight. Add green change to each residual to obtain the red and blue changes, then update modulo 256. For a5 97 after the previous step’s color, the complete changes are six, five and four.

Observe: The new components are seven, six, five and 255, with input cursor twenty-four.

Use observation s22-1. Native prose and typeset quizzes are in native/s22.tex.

Check: What does the high nibble of 97 contribute?

**A** An absolute red sample.
**B** The new alpha value.
**C** Red change minus green change.

Hint 1: The two nibbles are residuals, not independent absolute changes.
Hint 2: Decode dG=5, dR-dG=1 and dB-dG=-1 before applying any channel addition.
