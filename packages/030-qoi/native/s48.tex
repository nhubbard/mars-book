\section{Step 48 Emit a fitting DIFF command}\label{qoi-s48}
Author \texttt{e\_diff} to test whether all three changes fit minus two through plus one. Add two only when constructing the unsigned fields, position them and combine the class bits. The worked red pixel fits with fields 01,10,10 and emits 5a. A non-fitting triple must continue to LUMA rather than truncating fields that cannot represent it.

Work in \texttt{e\_diff}; use \texttt{s48-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_e\_diff}.

\paragraph{Observe} The prefix is the fourteen-byte header followed by c0 and 5a. The output cursor is sixteen.

\paragraph{Check} What happens to a change of plus two?
\begin{itemize}
  \item[A.] It fails the DIFF range test.
  \item[B.] It is stored as field 00.
  \item[C.] It is always clamped to plus one.
\end{itemize}
