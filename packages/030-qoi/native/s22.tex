\section{Step 22 Combine green change with its residuals}\label{qoi-s22}
Author \texttt{w\_luma} to require its second payload byte, recover green change from the low six tag bits and split the second byte into two residual nibbles. Subtract biases thirty-two and eight. Add green change to each residual to obtain the red and blue changes, then update modulo 256. For \texttt{a5} 97 after the previous step’s color, the complete changes are six, five and four.

Work in \texttt{w\_luma}; use \texttt{s22-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_w\_luma}.

\paragraph{Observe} The new components are seven, six, five and 255, with input cursor twenty-four.

\paragraph{Check} What does the high nibble of 97 contribute?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] An absolute red sample.
\item[B.] The new alpha value.
\item[C.] Red change minus green change.
\end{itemize}
