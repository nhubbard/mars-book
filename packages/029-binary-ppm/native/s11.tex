\section{Step 11 Prove the exact raster extent}\label{ppm-s11}

Require Maxval 255 and one final LF, then advance once to the raster offset. Multiply the bounded dimensions to obtain the pixel count and multiply that by three for sample bytes. Compare it with count minus raster offset; a trailer and a missing sample both fail before publication.

Author region \texttt{p\_extent}, keeping its BEGIN/END markers and the supplied caller, checkpoint labels and cleanup paths. Stop at \texttt{after\_p\_extent} for the observation.

Use recipe \texttt{s11-1} with the cumulative source through this edit.
