# Expand the full signed delta into a sign mask

Shift `t1` arithmetically by 63 into `t3`. This produces zero for a nonnegative doubleword and all one bits for a negative one. Shifting by 31 would incorrectly treat some positive exact endpoint differences as if their high word bits were a sign mask.

## Write and inspect

Replace the temporary branch and nop in `e_sign`, retaining labels and markers.

```asm
    dsra $t3, $t1, 63
```

## Expected observation

The mask is zero for 1000 and -1 for an initial negative reading.
