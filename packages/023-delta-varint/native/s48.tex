\section{Step 48 Turn mapped parity into an inverse sign mask}\label{delta-s48}

At \texttt{dec\_unmap}, isolate the unsigned accumulator low bit and negate it in a doubleword. The result is zero for even mapped values and all ones for odd values. Keep the full unsigned accumulator \texttt{t1} available for the magnitude shift.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the supplied temporary branch and nop in \texttt{d\_inverse\_mask}. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    andi $t5, $t1, 1
    dsubu $t5, $zero, $t5
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s48-1}, \texttt{s48-2} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{codec\_done} before caller result storage.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The default even mapped value produces inverse mask zero; mapped one produces -1.
