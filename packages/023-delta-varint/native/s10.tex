\section{Step 10 Expand the full signed delta into a sign mask}\label{delta-s10}

Shift \texttt{t1} arithmetically by 63 into \texttt{t3}. This produces zero for a nonnegative doubleword and all one bits for a negative one. Shifting by 31 would incorrectly treat some positive exact endpoint differences as if their high word bits were a sign mask.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the supplied temporary branch and nop in \texttt{e\_sign}. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    dsra $t3, $t1, 63
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s10-1}, \texttt{s10-2} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{codec\_done} before caller result storage.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The mask is zero for 1000 and -1 for an initial negative reading.
