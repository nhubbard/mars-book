\section{Step 58 Compare malformed and range failures after a valid prefix}\label{delta-s58}

Use 02 80 00 to obtain one valid reading followed by a nonminimal candidate. Use fe ff ff ff 0f 02 to obtain the signed maximum followed by a canonical increment that exceeds range. Both preserve the first output, but their error statuses and consumed-byte prefixes differ.

Use recipes \texttt{s58-1} through \texttt{s58-4} with the completed decoder. Inspect counters and status before the caller stores them.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The malformed case returns 1 byte,1 reading,status 2. The range case returns 5 bytes,1 reading,status 3.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why do these cases have different statuses?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} One violates wire canonicality; the other violates reconstructed reading range.
  \item \textbf{B} Their destination alignments differ.
  \item \textbf{C} One has a larger backing allocation.
\end{itemize}
