\section{Step 8 Subtract the committed predecessor in full width}\label{delta-s08}

Use \texttt{dsubu} to compute the current reading minus \texttt{s4}. At the first value \texttt{s4} is zero. Later iterations update it only after a candidate has been fully written. This subtraction must retain endpoint jumps wider than a word.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the supplied temporary branch and nop in \texttt{e\_difference}. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    dsubu $t1, $t0, $s4
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s08-1}, \texttt{s08-2} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{codec\_done} before caller result storage.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The first default delta is \texttt{t1}=1000; an initial minimum reading gives -2147483648.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why keep the predecessor unchanged during preflight?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} It is a caller-saved register.
  \item \textbf{B} A rejected candidate must not become the committed predecessor.
  \item \textbf{C} The next reading is always identical.
\end{itemize}
