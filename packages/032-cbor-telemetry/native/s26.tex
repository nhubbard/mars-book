\section{Step 26 Validate the counted identifier before copying}\label{cbor-s26}
Require byte-string type, length at most thirty-two and enough remaining bytes for the complete payload. Store the accepted length in the private prefix. Keep the payload cursor available for the following copy loop. The trace's identifier length is three; the zero at its first byte is data, not a terminator. This recipe moves the identifier pair first, keeping its bytes unchanged; its payload begins at offset five rather than the original trace offset thirty-eight.

Author region \texttt{d\_identifier}. Use \texttt{s26-1} and stop before \texttt{after\_d\_identifier}.

\paragraph{Observe} The candidate identifier length is three and its payload begins at source offset five.

\paragraph{Check} What ends this identifier?
\begin{itemize}
  \item[A.] Its accepted byte count.
  \item[B.] The first zero byte.
  \item[C.] The next byte that resembles a key.
\end{itemize}
