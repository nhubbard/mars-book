\section{Step 3 Extract and check the major type}\label{cbor-s03}
Shift the initial byte right by five to obtain its major type. Accept zero, one, two, four and five; reject three, six and seven for this exercise. Keep the decoded type in a temporary register while the lower five bits are interpreted. Do not publish a successful type with a failed head result.

Author region \texttt{h\_type}. Use \texttt{s03-1} and stop before \texttt{after\_h\_type}.

\paragraph{Observe} The trace's major type is five, while a2 is still a cleared failure result.

\paragraph{Check} What does major type five describe?
\begin{itemize}
  \item[A.] A map.
  \item[B.] Five argument bytes.
  \item[C.] An array with five elements.
\end{itemize}
