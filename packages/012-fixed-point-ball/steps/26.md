# Step 26 Reproduce the wrapped word result

The ball’s bounded sums cannot overflow, so use the separate probe to study a larger numeric domain. Its signed word operands still share the same scale. A word addition without an overflow exception keeps the low 32 bits and sign-extends that result in the wider register view.

## Write or inspect

Append the word addition in the `probe` region. Inspect the direct probe with raw 0x7fffff00 and 0x200. The wide result is not implemented yet.

```asm
    addu $v0, $a0, $a1
```

## Expected observation

The wrapped low word is 0x80000100, interpreted as minus 2147483392. That is a representation overflow for the intended positive sum, even though this instruction returns normally.
