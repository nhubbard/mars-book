# Step 24 Count publications at the supplied marker

The ready marker is one supplied instruction between two public labels. A native execution trace can count how many times that region executes. Stopping before a marker does not yet execute it; continuing through the final marker before exit makes the total N plus one.

## Write or inspect

Read the public checker’s stopped-boundary sequence and marker count. Preserve the provided marker and its labels in both guided and free-writing mode.

## Expected observation

Before inspecting tick t, exactly t earlier markers have executed. After normal exit, N plus one markers have executed. Extra repeated or omitted publications fail independently of the final image.
