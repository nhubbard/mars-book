# Step 5 Reflect at the lower wall

A nonpositive trial position needs reflection. Negate its displacement and its velocity. The zero case is included: an exact lower-wall contact must leave a nonzero incoming velocity pointing inward. The position and velocity bounds ensure that neither negation overflows.

## Write or inspect

Append the lower-wall branch and reflection. Trace raw position 128 with velocity minus 128, then raw position 64 with velocity minus 128.

```asm
    bgtz $v0, axis_above_low
    nop
    subu $v0, $zero, $v0
    subu $v1, $zero, $v1
    jr $ra
    nop
axis_above_low:
```

## Expected observation

The exact contact returns position zero and velocity 128. The overshoot returns position 64 and velocity 128. Positive trial positions currently fall through unchanged; the upper wall is still unfinished.
