# Step 2 Interpret a negative raw word as one signed value

A negative velocity uses the signed interpretation of the whole raw word. Its low byte is not a separate signed fraction. This matters when inspecting a negative value in hexadecimal or deciding whether a shift implements the intended division rule.

## Write or inspect

Use the worked raw value minus 96. Compare its represented value, its low byte, and the integer obtained by an arithmetic shift of eight. This is a paper or debugger interpretation exercise; the ball seeds remain unchanged.

## Expected observation

Raw minus 96 represents minus three eighths. Its low byte is 160, and its downward-rounded integer part is minus one. Those two pieces combine to the same signed value.
