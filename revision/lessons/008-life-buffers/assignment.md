# Snapshots and reusable storage

Read Chapter 8 and its lab. Predict the oscillator through generations zero,
one and two. Track both fixed allocation addresses and their changing roles.
For one cell, derive all eight neighbor addresses and identify the halo loads.
Then follow the complete worked life-buffers.asm through the guided Debug stops.
Distinguish completion of a destination from its publication as the current board.

Implement life_changes in the independent source. Return differences between old
and next cells rather than the number alive. Keep a trace of one birth, one death
and one unchanged cell. Use the public cases and deliberate lab faults to test
snapshot preservation, complete output and caller state independently.
