\section{Step 7 Append the extracted
  bit}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s07-step-7-append-the-extracted-bit}

Shifting the accumulator left creates a zero position at its low end. OR
then inserts the extracted bit into that position. Existing accumulated
bits move one position toward the high end.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s07-write-or-inspect}

Append the shift and combination.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    sll $t8, $t8, 1
    or $t8, $t8, $t5
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s07-expected-observation}

After processing the first two low bits of 0x16 in order, the
accumulator is binary 01. The complete eight-iteration trace ends at
01101000.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s07-check-your-understanding}

Why shift before OR?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} Shifting makes the source address smaller.
  \item
        \textbf{B} The shift makes room for the next bit at position zero.
  \item
        \textbf{C} OR always reverses a byte by itself.
\end{itemize}
