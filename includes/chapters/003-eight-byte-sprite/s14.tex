\section{Step 14 Advance one
column}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s14-step-14-advance-one-column}

Each destination moves four bytes because each pixel occupies a word.
The bit mask shifts right from 128 through 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, and 1.
After testing bit 0, the shift produces zero.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s14-write-or-inspect}

Append the destination and mask updates.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    daddiu $t2, $t2, 4
    daddiu $t7, $t7, 4
    srl $t4, $t4, 1
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s14-expected-observation}

After one pair, the pointers advance to the next cells and the mask
becomes 64. The framebuffer row base in \$s0 remains unchanged.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s14-check-your-understanding}

What is the mask after processing all eight columns?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} Zero.
\item
  \textbf{B} One, because bit zero can never be shifted away.
\item
  \textbf{C} 255.
\end{itemize}
