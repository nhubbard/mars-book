\section{Step 11 Prepare the two destination
  positions}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s11-step-11-prepare-the-two-destination-positions}

The normal image starts at column zero. The mirror starts at column
eight, 32 bytes into the row. Both drawing pointers advance by one word
per column. A mask of 128 tests bit 7 first.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s11-write-or-inspect}

Append the pointer and mask setup.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    move $t2, $s0
    daddiu $t7, $s0, 32
    li $t4, 128
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s11-expected-observation}

The destinations are separated by eight words. The mask is binary
10000000. daddiu updates an address at the full 64-bit register width.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-s11-check-your-understanding}

Why is the mirror displacement 32 bytes?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} It begins eight four-byte words after column zero.
  \item
        \textbf{B} An RGB color has 32 channels.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Eight source bytes always occupy 32 bytes.
\end{itemize}
