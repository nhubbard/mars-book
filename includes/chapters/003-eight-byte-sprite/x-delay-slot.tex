\section{A color load placed in the wrong
  slot}\label{003-eight-byte-sprite-x-delay-slot-a-color-load-placed-in-the-wrong-slot}

This sequence is incorrect for conditional color selection:

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
beq $t5, $zero, write_pixel
ori $t6, $zero, 0xffff
write_pixel:
sw $t6, 0($t2)
\end{minted}

The single-instruction color load is in the delayed
branch\textquotesingle s slot and executes for both outcomes, setting
the color to 0x0000ffff. The illustrated program instead places nop in
the slot and the conditional white load after it. Keep multi-instruction
pseudos out of delay slots.
