\section{Step 29 Restart the reduction tiles for this output tile}\label{matmul-s29}

At \texttt{column\_bound}, set \texttt{t0} to zero for L, the first reduction-tile start. Each output tile needs the complete reduction dimension. Carrying L from the previous output tile would skip contributions or bypass its reduction entirely.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{t\_reduction\_start}. Keep its surrounding labels and region markers.

\subsection{Expected observation}
