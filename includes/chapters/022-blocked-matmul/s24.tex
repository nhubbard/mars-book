\section{Step 24 Initialize the complete product interval}\label{matmul-s24}

Advance the zeroing pointer by eight, decrement the remaining cell count and repeat while nonzero. The preceding empty-output guard ensures the first iteration exists. At completion, every selected product byte is initialized, with no prefix or suffix writes.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{t\_zero\_next}. Keep its surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    daddiu $t1, $t1, 8
    addiu $t0, $t0, -1
    bne $t0, $zero, zero_product
    nop
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s24-1} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{matmul\_done} before the caller stores the returned count.

\subsection{Expected observation}
All four default cells are zero, \texttt{t0}=0, and \texttt{t1} is 32 bytes beyond the selected output start.
