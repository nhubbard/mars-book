\section{Step 34 Reload the initialized partial sum}\label{matmul-s34}

Load the complete doubleword through \texttt{t7} into \texttt{t4}. For L=0 it is the zero established by the initialization loop. For later reduction tiles it is the sum already contributed by earlier positions. Clearing \texttt{t4} here would silently erase that earlier work.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{t\_partial}. Keep its surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    ld $t4, 0($t7)
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s34-1} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{matmul\_done} before the caller stores the returned count.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The first partial sum is zero; later tiles must reload their existing contribution.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
What belongs in the accumulator before a later reduction tile?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} The already accumulated output partial sum.
  \item \textbf{B} Always zero.
  \item \textbf{C} The previous output column value.
\end{itemize}
