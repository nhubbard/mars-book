\section{Step 3 Prove the width of a partial sum}\label{matmul-s03}

Use two minimum input values in each reduction pair. One product fits a signed word, but adding two such products crosses the signed-word endpoint. The domain allows up to sixteen products per output. Keep the full result from every addition through the final store.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Two products sum to 2147483648; sixteen sum to 17179869184.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Which part must retain more than thirty-two bits?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} Only the loop index.
  \item \textbf{B} The accumulator and output.
  \item \textbf{C} Only the tile edge.
\end{itemize}
