\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why restart L after advancing J?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} A new column tile contains no output elements.
\item \textbf{B} The next output tile also needs every reduction position.
\item \textbf{C} The input rows have been repacked.
\end{itemize}
