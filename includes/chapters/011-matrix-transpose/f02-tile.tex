\item The condition \texttt{\$t1 == 4} selects the second visit to \texttt{next\_tile}. The blue \texttt{addiu} computes the next row limit. The remaining input column has not been read or written.
\item The tile origin is \texttt{\$t0 = 0}, \texttt{\$t1 = 4}; the input is $3\times5$. Initialized from their product on entry, \texttt{\$v0 = 15} holds the target element count, not completed stores.
\item The fresh cold cache has eight four-word blocks in one fully associative set: 128 bytes, with least-recently-used replacement. It does not continue the ordinary run\textquotesingle s history.
\item Twelve loads and twelve stores give 24 accesses. Seven misses leave 17 hits, rounded to $71\%$. The remaining column\textquotesingle s three loads and three stores are excluded.
\item The first twelve output words are $0,5,10,1,6,11,2,7,12,3,8,13$. They form four rows of three. Indices 12--14 remain zero. Byte \texttt{0d} in the twelfth word encodes decimal 13.
\end{enumerate}
