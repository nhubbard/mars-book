\section{Keep coordinates and addresses in different roles}\label{transpose-x-registers}
The guided ordinary function uses a pair of row starts plus a pair of advancing
inner pointers. The tiled function recomputes the inner pointers at each tile row.
These are explanatory conventions, not required register choices.
\tagpdfsetup{table/header-rows={1}}
\begin{longtable}{p{26mm}p{64mm}p{57mm}}
  \toprule
  State              & Ordinary walkthrough                                               & Tiled walkthrough                                                                 \\
  \midrule
  Current cell       & \texttt{\$t0} row, \texttt{\$t1} column                            & \texttt{\$t4} row, \texttt{\$t5} column                                           \\
  Tile bounds        & No tile state                                                      & \texttt{\$t0}, \texttt{\$t1} origins; \texttt{\$t2}, \texttt{\$t3} exclusive ends \\
  Source pointer     & Row start \texttt{\$t2}; inner pointer \texttt{\$t3}               & Inner pointer \texttt{\$t6}                                                       \\
  Destination        & Row's first destination \texttt{\$t4}; inner pointer \texttt{\$t5} & Inner pointer \texttt{\$t7}                                                       \\
  Destination stride & \texttt{\$t6}=4R                                                   & \texttt{\$v1}=4R                                                                  \\
  Source row stride  & \texttt{\$t7}=4C                                                   & Recomputed row start from \(iC+j_0\)                                              \\
  Copied word        & \texttt{\$t8}                                                      & \texttt{\$t8}                                                                     \\
  Return count       & \texttt{\$v0}=RC                                                   & \texttt{\$v0}=RC                                                                  \\
  \bottomrule
\end{longtable}
A register has no permanent source-language type. Its role follows from the code
that defines and consumes it. Mark each quantity as a coordinate, an exclusive
bound, a byte stride, a full address, or a data word when tracing. Adding a column
index directly to a pointer omits the four-byte scale. Adding a byte displacement
twice scales the address twice. Neither error is corrected by the cache.
