\section{Step 2 Locate the safe development returns}\label{chacha-s02}

Open the cumulative starter and locate its marked regions. Missing regions branch to a routine-specific development exit with status three. Framed exits restore saved registers; early wrapper exits do not restore a frame that has not been allocated. The default caller selects the quarter-round.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The unedited caller records development status three and leaves the selected words unchanged.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Does status three report valid encryption?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Yes, if the destination looks unchanged.
\item \textbf{B} Only for a zero key.
\item \textbf{C} No; it explicitly reports unfinished work.
\end{itemize}
