\section{Step 36 Copy a private block state}\label{chacha-s36}

Copy all sixteen input words into the first sixty-four bytes of the frame. Later block counters change only this private copy. The caller's constants, key, initial counter and nonce remain available unchanged.

\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the body of region \texttt{stream\_copy}. Retain its \texttt{\# BEGIN} and \texttt{\# END} comments and the supplied labels outside them.

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    daddu $t1, $a0, $t0
    lw $t2, 0($t1)
    daddu $t1, $sp, $t0
    sw $t2, 0($t1)
    daddiu $t0, $t0, 4
    sltiu $t1, $t0, 64
    bnez $t1, stream_copy_state
    nop
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s36-1} with the cumulative program through this edit. Inspect its named checkpoint before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The private state equals the caller state; no destination byte has changed.
