\section{Step 23 Finalize or diagnose truncation}\label{utf8-s23}

When final is one, prepare finished status one. If need is zero, publish it. Otherwise choose minus two and the absolute end position as the diagnostic. All bytes supplied in this otherwise possible prefix have been accepted.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{end\_final} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate values do not yet establish the final interface.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    li $v0, 1
    beq $t2, $zero, scan_commit
    nop
    li $v0, -2
    move $v1, $t0
    beq $zero, $zero, scan_commit
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
An empty final chunk can diagnose a prefix retained by an earlier call.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Where is a truncation detected?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} At the absolute end position.
  \item \textbf{B} At a nonexistent byte with value zero.
  \item \textbf{C} At the beginning of every chunk.
\end{itemize}
