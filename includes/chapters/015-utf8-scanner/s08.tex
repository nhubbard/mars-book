\section{Step 8 Record a candidate sequence start}\label{utf8-s08}

For a non-ASCII byte in an idle state, retain the current absolute total as its candidate start. Set ordinary continuation bounds 128 through 191. Later classification either refines those bounds or reports this byte as an impossible lead.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{candidate} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate values do not yet establish the final interface.

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    move $t5, $t0
    li $t3, 128
    li $t4, 191
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
A rejected standalone continuation still has a diagnostic sequence start at its own offset.
