\section{Step 13 Require the \texttt{f0} lower bound}\label{utf8-s13}

Set need to three for a four-byte lead. If the lead is \texttt{f0}, raise the next-byte minimum to decimal 144, hexadecimal \texttt{90}, and jump to byte acceptance. This prevents encodings below \texttt{U+10000} from using four bytes.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{four\_low} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate values do not yet establish the final interface.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    li $t2, 3
    li $t9, 240
    bne $t8, $t9, scan_four_high
    nop
    li $t3, 144
    beq $zero, $zero, scan_accept
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
After accepting only \texttt{f0}, the pending range is \texttt{90} through \texttt{bf} in hexadecimal.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}

  \item \textbf{B} Only after a final chunk.
  \item \textbf{C} No, only the next byte after the lead.
