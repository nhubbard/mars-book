\section{Step 1 Choose a scalar count}\label{utf8-s01}

Read the eleven-byte fixture and identify its five encoded scalar values. A leading byte is not itself a completed multibyte value. The zero byte represents \texttt{U+0000} in this interface, rather than terminating the input.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The complete fixture has eleven bytes and five scalar values.
