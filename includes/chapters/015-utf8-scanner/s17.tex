\section{Step 17 Limit the \texttt{ed} upper bound}\label{utf8-s17}

For \texttt{ed}, restrict the next maximum to decimal 159, hexadecimal \texttt{9f}. Other three-byte leads retain the ordinary maximum. This prevents the partial encoding from entering the surrogate range.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{three\_high} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate values do not yet establish the final interface.

An \texttt{ed} \texttt{a0} prefix fails on \texttt{a0}. A later \texttt{bf} is still allowed after a valid \texttt{ed} \texttt{9f} prefix.
