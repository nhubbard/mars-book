\section{Step 24 Prepare the malformed-byte result}\label{utf8-s24}

Select minus one and retain total as the detecting position. For an invalid continuation, total still points at that byte while start identifies the earlier lead. A final flag cannot turn an already impossible byte into a mere truncation.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{bad} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate values do not yet establish the final interface.
