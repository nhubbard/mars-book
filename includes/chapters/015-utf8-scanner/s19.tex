\section{Step 19 Advance the pending sequence}\label{utf8-s19}

After a continuation passes both bounds, reduce need and restore the ordinary 128 through 191 range. If more continuations remain, jump to common byte acceptance. If none remain, fall through to scalar completion.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{tail\_advance} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate values do not yet establish the final interface.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    addiu $t2, $t2, -1
    li $t3, 128
    li $t4, 191
    bne $t2, $zero, scan_accept
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
A valid \texttt{ed} \texttt{9f} prefix permits its final byte through \texttt{bf} because the special upper bound has been reset.
