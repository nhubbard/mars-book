\section{Step 12 Reject leads beyond \texttt{f4}}\label{utf8-s12}

The remaining candidates begin at \texttt{f0}. Require the value to be below 245, hexadecimal \texttt{f5}, before setting any pending count. A larger lead has no valid scalar completion under this format.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{four\_limit} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate values do not yet establish the final interface.

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    sltiu $t9, $t8, 245
    beq $t9, $zero, scan_bad
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
The final valid four-byte lead is \texttt{f4}; \texttt{f5} through \texttt{ff} branch to error.
