\section{Step 18 Check both continuation bounds}\label{utf8-s18}

When need is positive, compare the byte with the retained minimum and maximum. Either failure branches to error before advancing any counter. The lead start is retained from the earlier accepted bytes, possibly from another chunk.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{tail\_bounds} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub. Intermediate values do not yet establish the final interface.

For a pending \texttt{f0} lead, byte \texttt{8f} fails the lower bound and reports the earlier lead start.
