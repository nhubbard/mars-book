\section{Step 30 Preserve the first terminal diagnostic}\label{utf8-s30}

After a completed or failed stream, call again with different input. Return minus three and zero without reading the new bytes or writing state. The original status remains one, minus one, or minus two; minus three is a call rejection code.

Perform the described inspection or experiment while preserving the submitted interface.

\subsection{Expected observation}
A later valid chunk cannot clear an earlier failure or add to its count.
