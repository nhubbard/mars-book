\section{Step 28 Inspect unused capacity after an atomic stop}\label{delta-s28}

Use the completed encoder with byte capacity seven. Its first four values occupy five bytes, and the final three-byte value cannot fit the two remaining positions. Those two positions must retain the sentinel. A capacity bound authorizes potential output, not partial publication of a rejected candidate.

Use recipe \texttt{s28-1} with the completed encoder and pause at \texttt{codec\_done}.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The result is \texttt{v0}=4,\texttt{v1}=5,\texttt{a3}=1, with bytes five and six unchanged.
