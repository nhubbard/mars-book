\section{Step 45 Reject payload bits outside the 33-bit wire width}\label{delta-s45}

Require the fifth payload to be below 32. A terminal fifth byte can still be malformed if one of its two unused payload bits is set. A payload of 31 is permitted by wire width and will undergo reconstruction/range checks later.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the supplied temporary branch and nop in \texttt{d\_fifth\_payload}. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    sltiu $t6, $t5, 32
    beq $t6, $zero, dec_bad
    nop
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s45-1}, \texttt{s45-2}, \texttt{s45-3} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{codec\_done} before caller result storage.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Fifth payload 32 returns malformed status two; the minimum-reading encoding with fifth payload 15 passes this gate.
