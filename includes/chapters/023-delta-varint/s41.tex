\section{Step 41 Recognize a terminating raw byte}\label{delta-s41}

Mask bit seven of \texttt{t4} into \texttt{t6}. If it is clear, branch to \texttt{dec\_terminal}. Otherwise another group is required. This condition does not inspect the reconstructed reading sign; framing belongs to the raw byte.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the supplied temporary branch and nop in \texttt{d\_continuation}. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    andi $t6, $t4, 128
    beq $t6, $zero, dec_terminal
    nop
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s41-1} from the companion construction inputs. Pause at \texttt{codec\_done} before caller result storage.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The first default byte has continuation set; the second will terminate.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Which bit decides whether another group follows?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} The accumulator low bit.
  \item \textbf{B} Bit seven of the raw byte.
  \item \textbf{C} The most significant reading bit.
\end{itemize}
