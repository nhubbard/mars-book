\section{Step 7 Load the current signed reading through its full address}\label{delta-s07}

Multiply the committed reading index by four and add it to the full source base. Load the word into \texttt{t0} with \texttt{lw}. Keep the current reading while the byte-group logic uses other temporaries; it later becomes the next predecessor.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the supplied temporary branch and nop in \texttt{e\_load}. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.
