\section{Step 2 Predict a delta that does not fit a word}\label{delta-s02}

For a signed minimum reading followed by a signed maximum reading, calculate the exact subtraction before choosing an instruction. Both stored values fit words, but their difference is 4294967295. Retain that value before the signed mapping.

\subsection{Expected observation}
A word subtraction would turn the upward endpoint jump into -1.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Where must widening happen?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Only at the final byte store.
\item \textbf{B} Before subtracting the two readings.
\item \textbf{C} After the wrapped difference is mapped.
\end{itemize}
