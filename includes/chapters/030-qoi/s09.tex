\section{Step 9 Give each cursor and predictor its own state}\label{qoi-s09}
Author \texttt{w\_state} to establish eight remaining pixels, input offset fourteen and payload end at offset thirty. Initialize previous color to opaque black, the previous-index marker to minus one and the cache pointer to frame offset 112. Preserve the total pixel count independently of the remaining count. Do not initialize the cache to opaque black: clearing that separate object is the next edit.

Work in \texttt{w\_state}; use \texttt{s09-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_w\_state}.
