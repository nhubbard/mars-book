  \begin{minipage}{\linewidth}
    \small
    \begin{enumerate}
      \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
            \setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
      \item At \texttt{decode\_return}, both validation and pixel publication have completed, and the routine has restored its stack frame. The caller has not yet selected its next action or saved the returned values.
      \item \texttt{v0 = 0} reports success. \texttt{v1 = 4294967304} is $1\cdot2^{32}+8$: height one in the upper half and width eight in the lower half. It is not a count of billions of pixels or output bytes.
      \item The two populated rows hold eight little-endian \texttt{0xAARRGGBB} words. The seventh word has bytes \texttt{05 06 07 80}, or \texttt{0x80070605}; its alpha is 128. The eighth has \texttt{01 01 01 ff}, or \texttt{0xff010101}, with alpha 255. The next row is untouched \texttt{a5}. The RGB display uses the color components without alpha compositing; inspect these stored bytes to establish alpha preservation.
      \item The caller\textquotesingle s metadata arena remains \texttt{a5}. The decoder validated into private storage; its public output is the framebuffer. Metadata publication is the validator interface\textquotesingle s separate contract.
      \item The saved status and count are still $-1$. Mode two selects decoding. Continuing saves the full sixty-four-bit packed result, including height; a narrow store would lose that upper half.
    \end{enumerate}
