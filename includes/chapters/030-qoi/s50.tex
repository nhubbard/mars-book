\section{Step 50 Emit green and two fitting residuals}\label{qoi-s50}
Author \texttt{e\_luma} to check the signed green range and the two red/blue residual ranges. A fitting command stores green plus thirty-two and each residual plus eight. The worked transition from 1,1,1 to 7,6,5 produces \texttt{a5} 97. A failed range test routes to the literal RGB body; emitting low bits of an out-of-range residual would silently change the pixel.

Work in \texttt{e\_luma}; use \texttt{s50-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_e\_luma}.

\paragraph{Observe} The output prefix has twenty-one bytes, including \texttt{a5} 97, and two later pixels remain.

\paragraph{Check} What ranges must the residuals satisfy?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Zero through 255 without a bias.
\item[B.] Minus eight through seven.
\item[C.] Minus thirty-two through thirty-one.
\end{itemize}
