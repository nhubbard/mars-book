\section{Step 2 Separate the two packed dimensions}\label{qoi-s02}
Author \texttt{b\_unpack} to recover width from the low thirty-two bits of \texttt{a0} and height from the high thirty-two. Retain both as unsigned numerical fields. An arithmetic right shift would copy a sign bit into the height result. A word-sized shift cannot preserve both halves of the original argument. Put width in \texttt{t1} and height in \texttt{t2} for the following bound checks.

Work in \texttt{b\_unpack}; use \texttt{s02-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_b\_unpack}.

\paragraph{Observe} For packed \texttt{0x0000000100000008}, \texttt{t1} is eight and \texttt{t2} is one. No caller memory has been accessed.

\paragraph{Check} Which operation should recover the upper field?
\begin{itemize}
  \item[A.] A word-sized left shift by thirty-two.
  \item[B.] A logical sixty-four-bit right shift by thirty-two.
  \item[C.] A signed byte load.
\end{itemize}
