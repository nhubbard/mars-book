Author \texttt{w\_pack} to place alpha in bits thirty-one through twenty-four, red in twenty-three through sixteen, green in fifteen through eight and blue in the low byte. Use full-width operations or a consistent zero-extended word representation. Set the ordinary one-pixel repetition count for the common update stage. The first call to this packer follows the explicit RGB command.

Work in \texttt{w\_pack}; use \texttt{s19-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_w\_pack}.
