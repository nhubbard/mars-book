\section{Keep the leaf routine's exits visible}
The schedule builder makes no nested calls and uses only argument and temporary
registers. Its supplied normal exit returns zero. Until the last region is complete,
an empty region branches to \texttt{schedule\_development}, which returns three.
Neither exit restores a frame because this leaf routine never allocates one. The
source and destination are caller storage; returning does not undo schedule words
already written by an intermediate edit.

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{Supplied entry and frame}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
sha256_schedule:
\end{minted}

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{Supplied normal and development exits}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
schedule_done:
    move $v0, $zero
    jr $ra
    nop
schedule_development:
    li $v0, 3
    jr $ra
    nop

\end{minted}
