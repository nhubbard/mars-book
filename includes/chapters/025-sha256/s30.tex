\section{Step 30 Choose whether padding needs an extra block}\label{sha-s30}

Compare the working used count after writing the marker with fifty-seven. A value below fifty-seven leaves enough room for the eight length bytes and branches to final\_zero\_to\_length. Otherwise continue with the first-block fill path. The comparison is deliberately after the marker increment; its boundary corresponds to original used counts fifty-five and fifty-six.
