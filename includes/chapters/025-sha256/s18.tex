\section{Step 18 Advance constants and schedule entries together}\label{sha-s18}

Increment each word pointer by four and decrement the round count. Repeat at compress\_round while the count is nonzero. Both pointers must advance once per round, since Kt and Wt refer to the same round index. After sixty-four rounds the working vector is ready for feed-forward; it has not yet replaced the running state.

\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace only the temporary instructions between \texttt{\# BEGIN comp\_walk} and \texttt{\# END comp\_walk}. Keep the supplied labels outside that region.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    daddiu $t6, $t6, 4
    daddiu $t7, $t7, 4
    addiu $t8, $t8, -1
    bne $t8, $zero, compress_round
    nop
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s18-1} with the cumulative program through this edit. The exact recipe names the entry, prepared input and checkpoint. Observe the checkpoint before continuing to its temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The two pointers have advanced by 256 bytes and \texttt{t8} is zero; caller state remains unchanged before feed-forward.
