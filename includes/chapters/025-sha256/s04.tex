\section{Step 4 Place the first two bytes in their word fields}\label{sha-s04}

Load the first two bytes without sign extension. Shift the first into bits 31 through 24 and the second into bits 23 through 16, then combine them. The source has not moved, so offsets zero and one refer to the same four-byte group. The remaining low fields will be filled by the next edit.

\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace only the temporary instructions between \texttt{\# BEGIN sch\_high} and \texttt{\# END sch\_high}. Keep the supplied labels outside that region.
