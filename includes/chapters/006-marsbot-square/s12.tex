\section{Step 12 Poll x against zero and stop
west}\label{006-marsbot-square-s12-step-12-poll-x-against-zero-and-stop-west}

The target changes, but the feedback principle remains the same. A load
from x reports the current horizontal coordinate; \texttt{bne} repeats
until that value is zero.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{006-marsbot-square-s12-write-or-inspect}

Append the west polling loop.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
west_wait:
    lw $t2, 0x30($s0)
    bne $t2, $zero, west_wait
    nop
    sw $zero, 0x50($s0)
west_stopped:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{006-marsbot-square-s12-expected-observation}

The position is (0, side), and three strokes form an open path.

understanding}\label{006-marsbot-square-s12-check-your-understanding}
