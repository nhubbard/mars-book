\section{Step 9 Turn south and start the second
leg}\label{006-marsbot-square-s09-step-9-turn-south-and-start-the-second-leg}

Heading 180 increases y while leaving x constant. Because the prior leg
stopped, the heading change cannot create a moving diagonal or an extra
pixel along the old direction.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{006-marsbot-square-s09-write-or-inspect}

Append the south turn and start.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    li $t1, 180
    sw $t1, 0x10($s0)
    sw $t0, 0x50($s0)
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{006-marsbot-square-s09-expected-observation}

The next target is y = side, starting from (side, 0).

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{006-marsbot-square-s09-check-your-understanding}

Which coordinate changes on a southward leg?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} y increases.
\item
  \textbf{B} x decreases.
\item
  \textbf{C} Both must change equally.
\end{itemize}
