\section{Step 11 Turn west and start the third
  leg}\label{006-marsbot-square-s11-step-11-turn-west-and-start-the-third-leg}

Heading 270 decreases x. The next endpoint is zero, not another addition
of the side length. The zero register provides that target without
loading another constant.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{006-marsbot-square-s11-write-or-inspect}

Append the west turn and start.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    li $t1, 270
    sw $t1, 0x10($s0)
    sw $t0, 0x50($s0)
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{006-marsbot-square-s11-expected-observation}

Movement begins from (side, side) toward (0, side).

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{006-marsbot-square-s11-check-your-understanding}

What is the west leg\textquotesingle s x target?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} Twice the side length.
  \item
        \textbf{B} The original side length.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Zero.
\end{itemize}
