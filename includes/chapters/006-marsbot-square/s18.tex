\section{Step 18 Pause and rewind at a named
  boundary}\label{006-marsbot-square-s18-step-18-pause-and-rewind-at-a-named-boundary}

With no instructions completing, the robot clock does not advance. The
device also participates in MARS instruction checkpoints, so backstep
can restore its position, countdown, and pen history alongside CPU
state. The visible panel still needs to refresh after that restoration.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{006-marsbot-square-s18-write-or-inspect}

Use the first-corner breakpoint; inspect one-instruction forward and
backstep states in a fresh Debug run.

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{006-marsbot-square-s18-expected-observation}

A pause itself adds no travel. Record the instruction boundary as well
as the displayed position.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{006-marsbot-square-s18-check-your-understanding}

Does waiting at a Debug pause move this robot?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} No; its execution clock is paused.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Yes, at one pixel per host second.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Only when the editor has focus.
\end{itemize}
