\section{Step 7 Poll x and stop the east
  leg}\label{006-marsbot-square-s07-step-7-poll-x-and-stop-the-east-leg}

The loop reloads x, compares it with the target, and repeats while they
differ. \texttt{bne} is a retained delayed branch: its \texttt{nop}
executes for both outcomes. Once equal, \texttt{sw} disables movement
before the next heading change.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{006-marsbot-square-s07-write-or-inspect}

Append the polling loop, stop, and corner label.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
east_wait:
    lw $t2, 0x30($s0)
    bne $t2, $s1, east_wait
    nop
east_stop:
    sw $zero, 0x50($s0)
east_stopped:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{006-marsbot-square-s07-expected-observation}

At \texttt{east\_stopped}, position is (side, 0), movement is disabled,
heading is 90, and the pen remains down.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{006-marsbot-square-s07-check-your-understanding}

Why must \texttt{lw} remain inside the polling loop?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} The assembler refuses loads outside loops.
  \item
        \textbf{B} The register must be refreshed as the device coordinate
        changes.
  \item
        \textbf{C} The target length changes after every instruction.
\end{itemize}
