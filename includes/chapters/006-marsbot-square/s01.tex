\section{Step 1 Read the robot clock and starting
state}\label{006-marsbot-square-s01-step-1-read-the-robot-clock-and-starting-state}

MarsBot advances one pixel when enough simulated instructions have
completed while movement is enabled. A paused execution performs no such
instructions. Its initial position is the top-left corner, not the
center of the board.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{006-marsbot-square-s01-write-or-inspect}

Inspect the header and predict the first outward direction.

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{006-marsbot-square-s01-expected-observation}

A new session starts at (0, 0), heading zero, stopped, with pen up. The
header requests 64 instructions per tick.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{006-marsbot-square-s01-check-your-understanding}

What advances the connected robot?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} Elapsed wall-clock seconds alone.
\item
  \textbf{B} Completed simulated instructions while moving.
\item
  \textbf{C} Opening its tool tab.
\end{itemize}
