\section{Use a corner as an observation
  boundary}\label{006-marsbot-square-p07-use-a-corner-as-an-observation-boundary}

The \texttt{east\_stopped} label follows the movement-stop store. At
that boundary the robot has \texttt{finished} its first side and is not
accumulating further moving ticks. A screenshot can therefore show the
corner without depending on a race between the CPU and the UI.

A breakpoint on the load that first observes x = side names a different
state. The stop has not yet executed, and the moving flag is still true.
Both observations can be correct. Naming the source boundary explains
why their controls differ.

The device participates in instruction backstep checkpoints. The shared
model\textquotesingle s tests verify restoration of coordinates,
countdown phase, and pen history. Actual book figures will additionally
need a verified panel refresh; a restored model snapshot alone is
insufficient evidence that IntelliJ has displayed it.
