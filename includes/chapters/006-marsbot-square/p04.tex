\section{Prepare a leg before starting
motion}\label{006-marsbot-square-p04-prepare-a-leg-before-starting-motion}

A leg has a preparation phase and a moving phase. During preparation the
robot is stopped, its heading is selected, and the target coordinate is
already available. After movement is enabled, the program spends its
time sampling the relevant axis.

The pen is lowered before the first start. A lowered pen with no
displacement makes no stroke. Leaving it down at a stopped corner
preserves a continuous drawing: the next movement begins where the
previous stroke ended, with a changed direction.

The register value one can enable either pen or movement because the
destination address selects the control. Reusing that value is
convenient but not a requirement on a student\textquotesingle s register
allocation. Changing a scratch register must not change the meaning of
the device interface.
