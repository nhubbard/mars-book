\section{Step 6 Reflect at the upper wall}\label{ball-s06}

For a positive trial, compare with the upper raw bound 1792. Interior positions return unchanged. At or above the bound, subtract the trial from twice the bound and reverse the velocity. This preserves the overshoot rather than clamping it away.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append the upper-wall path. Step through the worked trial 2048 and compare both returned values with the reflection equation.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    li $t0, 1792
    slt $t1, $v0, $t0
    bne $t1, $zero, axis_return
    nop
    li $t0, 3584
    subu $v0, $t0, $v0
    subu $v1, $zero, $v1
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
The completed function returns raw position 1536 and velocity minus 320 for that example. Exact upper contact returns 1792 with inward velocity.
