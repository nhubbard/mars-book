\section{Step 14 Check the leaf functions as callers would}\label{ball-s14}

The independent checks install nonzero high bits in saved registers and supply their own pointers. A function that works only with fresh zero registers or the main frame label does not satisfy its interface. Temporary registers and result registers remain available as documented scratch state.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Read the kernel checks. Test both return values of the axis updater and the renderer’s alternate destination. The sum-probe cases are expected to fail until its later steps.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The completed axis and renderer satisfy their contracts independently of main. Full saved-register values and the stack pointer survive their calls.
