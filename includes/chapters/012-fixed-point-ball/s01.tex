\section{Step 1 Read the scale before adding a velocity}\label{ball-s01}

A raw word is an integer whose represented value is divided by 256. The same scale applies to position and velocity. Adding the two raw integers therefore advances position by one simulation step without a conversion between units. A display cell is derived later.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Locate the seed words in the starter. Convert raw 320, 448, 192, and 320 to their position or velocity values. Keep \texttt{DEMO} at zero.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The initial position is one and one quarter pixels right and one and three quarters pixels down. The velocity is three quarters of a pixel right and one and one quarter pixels down per step.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
What raw value represents one pixel?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} One.
  \item \textbf{B} 256.
  \item \textbf{C} Eight.
\end{itemize}
