\section{Step 26 Reproduce the wrapped word result}\label{ball-s26}

The ball’s bounded sums cannot overflow, so use the separate probe to study a larger numeric domain. Its signed word operands still share the same scale. A word addition without an overflow exception keeps the low 32 bits and sign-extends that result in the wider register view.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append the word addition in the \texttt{probe} region. Inspect the direct probe with raw 0x7fffff00 and 0x200. The wide result is not implemented yet.

\Needspace{4\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    addu $v0, $a0, $a1
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
The wrapped low word is 0x80000100, interpreted as minus 2147483392. That is a representation overflow for the intended positive sum, even though this instruction returns normally.

\subsection{Check your understanding}
Does returning normally from this word addition prove the mathematical sum fit?

\begin{itemize}

  \item \textbf{B} Yes, because the mnemonic ends in u.
