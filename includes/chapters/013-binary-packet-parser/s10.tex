\section{Step 10 Decode the sequence number}\label{packet-s10}

Combine the two sequence bytes using the same high-byte shift and low-byte OR. Keep the result separate from the checksum. The checksum adds the original bytes, not the decoded integer. Sequence numbers span the full unsigned 16-bit range.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{sequence} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stub.

\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    lbu $t2, 6($a0)
    sll $t2, $t2, 8
    lbu $t7, 7($a0)
    or $t2, $t2, $t7
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
Bytes 80 ff decode to positive 33023, even though a signed byte view may display -128 and -1.
