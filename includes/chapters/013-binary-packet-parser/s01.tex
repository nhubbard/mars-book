\section{Step 1 Read the byte layout}\label{packet-s01}

Start with the twelve bytes in the supplied arena. Mark the fixed header, the three-byte payload, and the final checksum. The sequence field occupies two bytes; it is not the two-character text "12" or "34". The body includes high-bit bytes to make signed debugger displays visible.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Perform the described inspection or experiment without changing the submitted interface.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The header ends at offset 7, the payload occupies offsets 8 through 10, and the trailer is at offset 11.
