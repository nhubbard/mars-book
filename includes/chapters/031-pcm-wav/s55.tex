\section{Step 55 Accumulate actual positive read progress}\label{pcm-wav-s55}
Request at most the remaining capacity and, for the illustrated implementation, at most 4096 bytes. Read into buffer plus accepted count. A positive result advances \texttt{s4} by that result; zero goes to \texttt{load\_eof}; a negative result closes through \texttt{load\_read\_error}. If capacity is already exhausted, go to \texttt{load\_probe}. A short positive read is not EOF and must not leave a hole in the buffer.

Author region \texttt{l\_read}. Use \texttt{s55-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_l\_read}.

\paragraph{Observe} With forced short reads, accepted bytes remain contiguous and the next request starts immediately after the actual prefix.

\paragraph{Check} What follows a positive short read?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Assume EOF.
\item[B.] Advance by the requested count.
\item[C.] Advance by the actual count and read again.
\end{itemize}
