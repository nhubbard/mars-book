\section{Step 24 Inspect the candidate before publication}\label{pcm-wav-s24}
Stop at \texttt{parse\_finish} after walking the trace. Inspect the two seen flags, retained rate and channels, PCM offset and length, and private frame count. The destination descriptor must still be untouched. This boundary separates knowing enough to describe the audio from having accepted the complete container and published that description.

Use \texttt{s24-1} and stop at \texttt{parse\_finish}.

\paragraph{Observe} Private state describes 16000 Hz, two channels, four frames, offset fifty-six and sixteen bytes; the caller descriptor is unchanged.

\paragraph{Check} Which object is still unchanged at parse\_finish?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] The caller descriptor.
\item[B.] The parser stack frame.
\item[C.] Every temporary register.
\end{itemize}
