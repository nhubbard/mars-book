\section{Step 40 Finish one channel before starting the next}\label{pcm-wav-s40}
Increment \texttt{s5} and compare it with the channel count. Branch to \texttt{filter\_channel} if another channel remains, which clears history and resets the current frame. Otherwise continue to the final return. The right-channel pass reads its own interleaved samples; it must not start where the left-channel frame cursor finished.

Author region \texttt{f\_advance\_channel}. Use \texttt{s40-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_f\_advance\_channel}.

\paragraph{Observe} After the second channel, channel index equals two and both interleaved outputs are complete.

\paragraph{Check} What must happen when starting the right channel?
\begin{itemize}
  \item[A.] Keep the left channel's final history.
  \item[B.] Skip its first seven frames.
  \item[C.] Reset its frame cursor and zero its own history.
\end{itemize}
