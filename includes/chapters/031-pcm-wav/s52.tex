\section{Step 52 Read the canonical output independently}\label{pcm-wav-s52}
Run the encoder observation to its caller return, then inspect all sixty output bytes. Parse the result with an independent reader and compare its PCM with the supplied raw sample bytes. A round trip through your own parser alone can conceal a shared layout mistake. Check the byte immediately after the result as a sentinel, not as part of the file.

Use \texttt{s52-1} and stop at \texttt{encode\_return}.

\paragraph{Observe} The result has a forty-four-byte canonical header, four stereo frames and unchanged sample bytes; the next byte remains a5.

\paragraph{Check} Why use an independent reader?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] A WAV parser must always be external.
\item[B.] The simulator cannot expose bytes.
\item[C.] Two routines can agree on the same incorrect layout.
\end{itemize}
