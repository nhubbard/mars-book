\section{Use playback after exact sample checks}
The longer fixed input contains 32000 stereo frames at 16000 frames per second,
occupying two seconds. Its left channel combines an integer triangle with period
eighty frames and an alternating component; its right uses a triangle with period
sixty-four and the opposite alternating component. The triangle repetition rates are
200 and 250 cycles per second. The helper performs integer arithmetic, so the input
bytes do not depend on a platform's trigonometric rounding.

Use the same eight averaging coefficients as in the short trace. Listen to input and
output as an additional observation of high-frequency attenuation, channel placement
and retained duration. Compare decoded samples first. A player may accept containers
outside the teaching profile and may conceal a damaged size field by reading to host
EOF. Successful playback cannot establish strict rejection behavior or exact Q15
arithmetic.

Keep the sample rate unchanged when writing the result. Relabeling the same codes with
a different rate changes their playback timing and frequencies; it does not resample
the signal. The chapter's filter operates on the discrete frame sequence at the
specified rate. Listening companions and measurements must identify whether they came
from the host model or actual MARS execution; host output is not simulator evidence.
