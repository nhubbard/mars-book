\section{Sources and scope}
The container and padding rules follow Microsoft's
\href{https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/xaudio2/resource-interchange-file-format--riff-}{RIFF overview}.
The PCM field relationships follow its
\href{https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/mmeapi/ns-mmeapi-waveformatex}{WAVEFORMATEX documentation}.
The supported rates, channel/frame/overhead bounds, accepted format lengths, required
chunk ordering, strict exact extent, descriptor layout and canonical writer are local
teaching contracts. The exact filter and its oldest-first coefficient convention
reuse the earlier FIR chapter.

Python's \href{https://docs.python.org/3/library/wave.html}{wave module} and an
independent FFmpeg decode provide host interoperability observations. Their acceptance
of a file is not the definition of this bounded profile. The first draft currently
provides the reading foundation and fixed inputs. Complete MIPS implementations,
cumulative construction steps, public grading exports and separate chapter archives
remain subsequent work. Automated light-theme IDE capture and native annotation
coordinates also remain pending; no capture recipe or drawing is an actual screenshot.
