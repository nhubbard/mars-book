\section{Step 9 Establish the next complete chunk span}\label{pcm-wav-s09}
At \texttt{parse\_chunk}, compare \texttt{s3} with the complete count. Equality goes to \texttt{parse\_finish}; otherwise require at least eight remaining bytes before reading the chunk header. Form the payload pointer in \texttt{t2}, unsigned payload length in \texttt{t3} and next offset in \texttt{t4}. Add the header, payload and optional odd-length pad in sixty-four-bit arithmetic, then reject a next offset beyond the file. Do not dereference the payload to decide whether its extent exists.

Author region \texttt{p\_chunk}. Use \texttt{s09-1} and stop at \texttt{after\_p\_chunk}.

\paragraph{Observe} The first chunk has payload length three, payload offset twenty and next chunk offset twenty-four.

\paragraph{Check} Where does an odd payload length place the next chunk?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] After one additional pad byte.
\item[B.] Immediately after the last payload byte.
\item[C.] At the next sixteen-byte boundary.
\end{itemize}
