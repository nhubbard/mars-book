\section{Step 9 Print signed
decimal}\label{004-bit-inspector-s09-step-9-print-signed-decimal}

Service 1 prints the signed interpretation of the low 32 bits in
\texttt{\$a0}. The prefix and newline use service 4 as in the console
chapter. The arithmetic results remain in their own registers and memory
while \texttt{\$v0} selects services.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{004-bit-inspector-s09-write-or-inspect}

Append the signed report line.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    la $a0, signed_msg
    li $v0, 4
    syscall
    move $a0, $s0
    li $v0, 1
    syscall
    la $a0, newline
    li $v0, 4
    syscall
signed_printed:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{004-bit-inspector-s09-expected-observation}

The partial report is signed: -1 followed by one newline for the default
sample.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{004-bit-inspector-s09-check-your-understanding}

Which width does this signed integer service format?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist

\item

\item
  \textbf{C} Only the lowest byte.
