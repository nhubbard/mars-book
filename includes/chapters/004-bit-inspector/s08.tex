\section{Step 8 Store the other three full-width
results}\label{004-bit-inspector-s08-step-8-store-the-other-three-full-width-results}

Successive doublewords begin eight bytes apart. The remaining stores
preserve the distinction between zero extension, word arithmetic, and
doubleword arithmetic. The grader will inspect all eight bytes rather
than only the low word.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{004-bit-inspector-s08-write-or-inspect}

Append the three stores and their checkpoint label.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    sd $s1, 8($t1)
    sd $s2, 16($t1)
    sd $s3, 24($t1)
values_stored:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{004-bit-inspector-s08-expected-observation}

For -1, unsigned\_value has low bytes ff ff ff ff and high bytes 00 00
00 00. Both plus-one fields are zero.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{004-bit-inspector-s08-check-your-understanding}

Why is a low-word-only check insufficient?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} A debugger automatically repairs the high half.
\item
  \textbf{B} It cannot distinguish results whose upper 32 bits differ.
\item
  \textbf{C} The low word never contains useful information.
\end{itemize}
