\section{A word shift by zero can change the high
  half}\label{004-bit-inspector-x-normalization-a-word-shift-by-zero-can-change-the-high-half}

At the positive signed-word boundary, a doubleword addition produces:

\MARSCodeLabel{Literal output}
\begin{verbatim}
0000000080000000
\end{verbatim}

Applying \texttt{sll\ \$s2,\ \$s2,\ 0} keeps low word 80000000 and
sign-extends that word:

\MARSCodeLabel{Literal output}
\begin{verbatim}
ffffffff80000000
\end{verbatim}

The low-word bits do not move, but the complete 64-bit pattern changes.
This is a word-width normalization, not a general full-register no-op.
