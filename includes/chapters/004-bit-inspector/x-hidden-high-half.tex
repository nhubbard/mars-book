\section{Equal output from unequal
  registers}\label{004-bit-inspector-x-hidden-high-half-equal-output-from-unequal-registers}

Suppose register A contains \texttt{ffffffffffffffff} and register B
contains \texttt{00000000ffffffff}. These are unequal 64-bit values, but
service 34 prints \texttt{0xffffffff} for either argument. Service 1
prints \texttt{-1} for either argument, and service 36 prints
\texttt{4294967295} for either argument.

A console-only test cannot distinguish these registers through those
services. The eight-byte result fields can: their high four bytes
differ. Check the precision required by the contract, not just the
precision visible in one report.
