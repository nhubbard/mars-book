\section{Step 5 Add one at word
  width}\label{004-bit-inspector-s05-step-5-add-one-at-word-width}

addiu forms a non-trapping 32-bit word sum and sign-extends the result
into its destination. The u does not request zero extension. For input
-1, adding one produces zero. A maximum positive signed word exposes the
wraparound boundary.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{004-bit-inspector-s05-write-or-inspect}

Append the word addition.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    addiu $s2, $s0, 1
word_added:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{004-bit-inspector-s05-expected-observation}

For -1, \texttt{\$s2} is zero. For 2147483647, it becomes
0xffffffff80000000, whose signed value is -2147483648.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{004-bit-inspector-s05-check-your-understanding}

What does the u mean for this addition?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} The result is always a positive signed integer.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Signed overflow does not cause a trap.
  \item
        \textbf{C} The result is always zero-extended.
\end{itemize}
