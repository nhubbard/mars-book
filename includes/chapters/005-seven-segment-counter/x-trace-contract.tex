\section{A final F is not a sixteen-step
counter}\label{005-seven-segment-counter-x-trace-contract-a-final-f-is-not-a-sixteen-step-counter}

These two histories have the same final right-port byte:

\MARSCodeLabel{Literal output}
\begin{verbatim}
correct: 3f 06 5b 4f 66 6d 7d 07 7f 6f 77 7c 39 5e 79 71
wrong:   71
\end{verbatim}

The contract distinguishes them. It also distinguishes sixteen byte
stores from sixteen word stores that touch adjacent ports. A
student-maintained RAM log alone cannot establish either transaction
property.
