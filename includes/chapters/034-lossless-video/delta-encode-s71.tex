\section{Step 71 Determine whether this sequence requires a full frame}
Fill \texttt{dr\_schedule}. Retain previous pointer, sequence, period and scratch pointer in \texttt{\$s3} through \texttt{\$s6}. Compute the unsigned doubleword remainder of sequence divided by period, then save it at private offset eighty. The period has already been checked nonzero before the remainder instruction. Zero selects a required full-frame boundary; a nonzero result permits prediction.

Use \texttt{s71-1} and stop before \texttt{dr\_schedule\_ready}.

\paragraph{Observe} At \texttt{dr\_schedule\_ready}, sequence one and period four give remainder one. The sequence-four recipe takes the independent full-frame route instead.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\paragraph{Check} When is a full frame mandatory?
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
  \item Whenever a single pixel changes.
  \item Whenever scratch has sixteen-byte alignment.
  \item When sequence modulo period is zero.
\end{enumerate}
