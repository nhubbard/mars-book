\section{Walk two updates and an unchanged frame}
Frame one's payload is eleven bytes:
\texttt{00 00 00 01 01 00 00 0F FF 00 00}. The first command skips zero pixels,
changes one pixel with red delta one, and leaves the cursor at one. The second skips
4095 pixels and changes zero, ending at 4096. Only the first working word changes.

Frame two changes a two-by-two region beginning at $(x,y)=(5,5)$. Packed row-major
indices are 325, 326, 389 and 390. The first command skips 325 and changes two. The
next skips 62 and changes two, because the cursor already advanced to 327. The last
skips 3705 and changes zero. Every changed pixel uses hexadecimal delta
\texttt{10 20 40}. The payload occupies 24 bytes.
\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\begin{longtable}{p{19mm}p{42mm}p{34mm}p{42mm}}
  \toprule Pixel index & Previous display word & XOR word            & Frame-two word      \\\midrule
  325                  & \texttt{0x00C22384}   & \texttt{0x00102040} & \texttt{0x00D203C4} \\
  326                  & \texttt{0x00E748A9}   & \texttt{0x00102040} & \texttt{0x00F768E9} \\
  389                  & \texttt{0x00F758B9}   & \texttt{0x00102040} & \texttt{0x00E778F9} \\
  390                  & \texttt{0x001C7DDE}   & \texttt{0x00102040} & \texttt{0x000C5D9E} \\\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
Frame three is identical to frame two. Its entire payload is
\texttt{10 00 00 00}: skip 4096, change zero. The frame index and display interval
still advance. An unchanged image is not an absent frame; it retains its place in
the timeline and its duration.
