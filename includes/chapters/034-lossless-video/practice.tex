\section{Use boundary inputs to explain a result}
\paragraph{An unchanged interval}
Encode four identical frames with period four. Compare all-full output with a full
frame and three skip-only deltas. Account for both header kinds and explain why
three unchanged intervals still exist in the animation's logical duration.

\paragraph{A longer delta}
Encode the all-change pair. Explain the four-byte difference between its one-command
delta payload and a full payload. Let your encoder choose another full frame, then
verify identical decoded pixels and a seek beginning at that optional full frame.

\paragraph{A late invalid count}
Construct a command stream whose first commands are valid but whose last skip exceeds
the remaining pixels. Rebuild the record CRC so the command validator, rather than
the checksum check, establishes the failure. Retain an earlier displayed frame and
sequence index throughout that failed application.

\paragraph{A vector remainder}
Compare kernel counts three, four, five and seventeen with separate and exactly
aliased output. Verify complete XOR words, changed counts and untouched guards in
both byte orders. A correct answer for 4096 words does not expose a loop that
unconditionally loads one more vector when only a scalar remainder remains.

\paragraph{A seek with another starting frame}
Seek frame six in both moving and extra-full inputs. Record which full frame starts
each replay and how many records are applied. Include one-time indexing separately.
The output words must agree even when the replay paths differ.
