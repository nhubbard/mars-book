\section{Step 12: Initialize the record checksum}
The checksum covers header bytes zero through eleven followed immediately by
the payload bytes. It excludes the stored checksum at offsets twelve through
fifteen. This is a logical concatenation of two source windows; it does not
require a temporary copy.

Fill \texttt{record\_crc\_state}. Set \texttt{\$t0} to the source, its current
exclusive end \texttt{\$t1} to source plus twelve, the accumulator
\texttt{\$t2} to \texttt{0x00000000ffffffff}, and the reflected polynomial
\texttt{\$t3} to \texttt{0x00000000edb88320}. Clear the phase flag
\texttt{\$t7}. The supplied control flow switches to the payload window once,
then finishes. These constants must have zero high halves; an unintended
sign extension would enter later right shifts.

\paragraph{Observe} Prepare \texttt{s12-1} and stop before
\texttt{record\_crc\_state\_ready}. At \texttt{record\_crc\_state\_ready}, inspect the full 64-bit
accumulator and polynomial. The current window is the twelve header bytes and
\texttt{\$t7} is zero.
