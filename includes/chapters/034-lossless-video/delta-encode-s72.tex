\section{Step 72 Validate the images before choosing a representation}
Fill \texttt{dr\_validate}. Walk all 4096 source words and reject a nonzero high byte. At a nonperiodic index, validate corresponding previous-image words too. At a required full index, skip those old-image loads. Keep both cursors full-width and advance four bytes per word. An invalid late color must fail before output or prediction-state stores.

Use \texttt{s72-1} and stop before \texttt{dr\_pixels\_valid}.

\paragraph{Observe} At \texttt{dr\_pixels\_valid}, default source and old image are valid RGB words. A required-full call may arrive here even when the old previous image has an invalid high byte, because it has not been read.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\paragraph{Check} Why can a required full frame ignore old image contents?
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
  \item Its pixels are independent of prediction history.
  \item It has no payload.
  \item Its sequence is always zero.
\end{enumerate}
