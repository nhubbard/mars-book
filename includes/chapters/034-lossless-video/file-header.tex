\section{Read the fixed file header before its records}
The file begins with 24 bytes. All multibyte file fields are unsigned and big-endian.
The four-byte magic includes the format version. Reject unsupported flags and
reserved values; do not guess another layout from them.
\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\begin{longtable}{p{18mm}p{42mm}p{86mm}}
\toprule Offset & Field & Required value or meaning \\\midrule
0 & Magic, 4 bytes & ASCII \texttt{MLV1} \\
4 & Width, 2 bytes & 64 \\
6 & Height, 2 bytes & 64 \\
8 & Frame count, 4 bytes & 1 through 256 \\
12 & Full-frame period, 2 bytes & 1 through 64 \\
14 & Flags, 2 bytes & Zero \\
16 & Duration, 4 bytes & 1 through 1000 milliseconds per frame \\
20 & Reserved, 4 bytes & Zero \\\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
The moving fixture declares eight frames, period four and duration forty milliseconds.
Its header is hexadecimal \texttt{4D 4C 56 31 00 40 00 40}, followed by
\texttt{00 00 00 08 00 04 00 00}, then \texttt{00 00 00 28 00 00 00 00}.
Forty milliseconds is a logical display interval; it is not an instruction count.

Reject a short file before reading any field. Bound frame count and period before
using them in allocation, indexing or a remainder operation. A directory needs at
most 256 native address offsets, or 2048 bytes. A conservative maximum file count
is $24+256(16+7\cdot4096)=7\,344\,152$ bytes. Reject a larger count before deriving
record addresses. Adapt the earlier bounded file loader's capacity admission to
this video bound while retaining its exact-fill EOF probe and descriptor ownership.
