\section{Step 62 Store the RGB payload length separately from record size}
Fill \texttt{ef\_length}. Serialize 12\,288 as a four-byte big-endian value at header offsets eight through eleven. This is the payload length; the complete record has sixteen additional bytes. Do not include the header in the stored length and do not put the destination capacity here. The CRC field remains untouched for now.

Use \texttt{s62-1} and stop before \texttt{ef\_length\_ready}.

\paragraph{Observe} At \texttt{ef\_length\_ready}, offsets eight through eleven are \texttt{00 00 30 00}. The caller will eventually receive 12\,304 as the produced record count.

\paragraph{Check} What does the header length field count?
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
\item The entire record including its header.
\item The destination allocation.
\item Payload bytes only.
\end{enumerate}
