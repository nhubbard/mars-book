\section{Measure a checksum alternative before adopting it}
The conditional CRC recurrence and the mask-based recurrence from step 14 produce
the same accepted images and the same rejections. They do different amounts of
simulated work. Table~\ref{tab:video-record-work} records complete scalar record
calls, from function entry through return, after the caller has supplied the
record and previous image. Each call includes validation, checksum calculation,
application, publication, saved-state handling and the construction's observation
\texttt{nop} instructions. Assembly, fixture preparation, printing, file I/O and
presentation are outside the interval.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Simulated instructions for two checked CRC implementations.}
\label{tab:video-record-work}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
Record input & Conditional CRC & Mask-based CRC \\
\midrule
Full frame zero & 1\,000\,606 & 1\,049\,737 \\
One changed pixel & 2\,090 & 2\,175 \\
Unchanged frame & 1\,472 & 1\,535 \\
4096 one-pixel commands & 2\,508\,218 & 2\,622\,594 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The mask-based version executes about four to five percent more instructions for
these inputs. It constructs and combines a polynomial mask on every bit, including
bits for which the conditional version skips the polynomial XOR. Retain the
conditional recurrence for this construction. These counts do not measure elapsed
host time or the host processor's branch prediction. A claim about either would
need a separate timing experiment. Both byte orders and both normal and
above-four-GiB address layouts produce the tabulated counts. A correct version
with a larger private stack frame produces the same conditional counts here.

A table-driven CRC is a possible next experiment. Its table must implement the same
polynomial, reflection, initialization, final complement and two-window coverage.
Include any per-call table construction in the measured interval, or state and
test a shared immutable table's initialization boundary. Keep all malformed-record
and failure-preservation checks. An optimization that accepts the late invalid
command has changed the routine's contract, regardless of its count.
