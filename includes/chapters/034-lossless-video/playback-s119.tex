\section{Step 119 Move to the next frame without moving the anchor}
Fill \texttt{vp\_advance}. Increment the current frame index after the interval completes. Continue while it differs from the admitted frame count. The next iteration advances the existing deadline by one duration and uses direct record application.

The last frame also receives its interval. Eight forty-millisecond intervals have logical duration 320 milliseconds, rather than the 280 milliseconds between the first and last frame's starting positions. A suffix beginning at six has two intervals and duration eighty milliseconds. Retain every frame, including unchanged ones.

Use \texttt{s119-1} and stop before \texttt{vp\_advanced}.

\paragraph{Observe} After the first interval, the current index is one. After the final interval, it equals the admitted frame count.
