\section{Step 68 Advance sequence only after the record is complete}
Fill \texttt{ef\_publish}. Store input sequence plus one at context offset eight, full kind zero at offset forty, and encoded count 12\,304 at forty-eight. Return success zero and that count. Leave capacity, period, previous-image inputs and suffix unchanged. Sequence 255 can succeed and publish next sequence 256; a further call with 256 must fail before touching any source or output bytes.

Use \texttt{s68-1} and stop before \texttt{ef\_published}.

\paragraph{Observe} At \texttt{ef\_published}, default sequence zero has advanced to one and the record count is 12\,304. Continue through the supplied caller: the existing decoder accepts the new bytes and reconstructs the original numeric pixels.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\paragraph{Check} What follows a successful encoding at sequence 255?
\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
\item Sequence wraps to zero automatically.
\item The record must be discarded because 255 cannot be encoded.
\item Next sequence 256, which a further call rejects.
\end{enumerate}
