\section{Step 25 Write the magic and first LF}\label{ppm-s25}

Initialize the serialization cursor from the output pointer. Write ASCII P, ASCII 6 and one LF byte, then advance by three positions. These bytes identify a text header prefix; they are not pixel samples or a numerical word.

Author region \texttt{e\_magic}, keeping its BEGIN/END markers and the supplied caller, checkpoint labels and cleanup paths. Stop at \texttt{after\_e\_magic} for the observation.

Use recipe \texttt{s25-1} with the cumulative source through this edit.
