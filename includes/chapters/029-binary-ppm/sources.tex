\section{Sources and development status}
The raw format, header/raster distinction and general sample representation follow the
\href{https://netpbm.sourceforge.net/doc/ppm.html}{Netpbm PPM specification}. The
sixty-four-pixel dimension limit, canonical numeric tokens, bounded header, LF-only
final separator and single-image exact-extent policy are local teaching choices.
Framebuffer word construction follows the display convention developed earlier in the
book. The file-operation discussion follows the current MARS syscall implementation.

This directly edited reading foundation establishes the boundaries that the implementation
will use. The grader's declared output fixtures, short writes, failure injection and
byte comparisons are verified by 107 CLI tests. A separate byte-transport probe passes
27 checks at ordinary addresses and again above 4 GiB. It copies arbitrary bytes and
does not parse PPM. Ten host-generated reading fixtures open with an independent image
reader and retain their exact samples; these are not assembly-writer results. Full
scalar image routines, independent image-reader comparisons, executable chapter examples,
cumulative exercises and separate distributions remain subsequent work. This draft is
not included in the main manuscript. Actual annotated light-theme IDE captures, remote
progression and independent accessibility review remain unfinished. The author's
substantial prose rewrite remains deferred until all thirty-five chapter drafts exist.
