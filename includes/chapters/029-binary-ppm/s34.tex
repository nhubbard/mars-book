\section{Step 34 Accumulate short reads}\label{ppm-s34}

Read into the unused suffix of the buffer and request only its remaining capacity. Distinguish a negative error, zero EOF and a positive byte count. Advance by the positive result and continue; a short positive result does not establish EOF.

Author region \texttt{l\_read}, keeping its BEGIN/END markers and the supplied caller, checkpoint labels and cleanup paths. Stop at \texttt{load\_eof} for the observation.

Use recipe \texttt{s34-1} with the cumulative source through this edit.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The full worked file is buffered at EOF, but metadata is still unchanged before finalization.

\subsection{Check your understanding}
What does a short positive read mean?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} The header is malformed.
\item \textbf{B} Some bytes arrived; continue until EOF or capacity.
\item \textbf{C} The file has ended.
\end{itemize}
