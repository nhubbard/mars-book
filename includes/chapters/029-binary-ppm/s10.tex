\section{Step 10 Retain width and height across tokens}\label{ppm-s10}

After a dimension token, require its value to be at most sixty-four. Retain width and height separately while advancing the field index and returning to the gap scanner. When the field index reaches Maxval, transfer control to its special final-token check.

Author region \texttt{p\_dimensions}, keeping its BEGIN/END markers and the supplied caller, checkpoint labels and cleanup paths. Stop at \texttt{parse\_maxval} for the observation.

Use recipe \texttt{s10-1} with the cumulative source through this edit.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Width and height are both two, and the Maxval token has numerical value 255.
