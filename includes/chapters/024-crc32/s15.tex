\section{Step 15 Generate all 256 entries}\label{crc-s15}

Advance the entry index, compare it with the exclusive bound 256, and repeat the entry preparation while it remains smaller. The prepared return follows the final word and reports 256. The destination walker then points immediately beyond the logical 1024-byte table.

\subsection{Expected observation}
All 256 words match the independent table, including index 255=\texttt{2d02ef8d}; \texttt{v0}=256.

\begin{itemize}
