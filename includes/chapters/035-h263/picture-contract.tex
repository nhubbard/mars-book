\section{Expose one complete picture as a callable boundary}
Implement \texttt{h263\_encode\_picture}, receiving an eight-byte-aligned 96-byte
request in \texttt{\$a0}. Every field is an unsigned native-endian doubleword:
\begin{center}
\tagpdfsetup{table/header-rows={1}}
\begin{tabular}{rll}
\textbf{Offset} & \textbf{Field} & \textbf{Meaning}\\
0 & Current picture & Tight 38\,016-byte Y/Cb/Cr input\\
8 & Reference picture & Same layout for P; zero for I\\
16 & New reconstruction & Writable 38\,016-byte output\\
24 & Writer context & Earlier aligned 24-byte record\\
32 & Workspace & Sixteen-byte-aligned private arena\\
40 & Workspace capacity & At least 169\,088 bytes\\
48 & Picture selector & Zero I; one P\\
56 & Temporal reference & Zero through 255\\
64 & Quantizer & One through 31\\
72 & Arithmetic mode & Zero scalar; one MSA\\
80 & GFID & Zero through three\\
88 & Reserved & Zero
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The writer context's used count must initially be zero. Capacity is at most one
MiB, and its buffer may start at any byte address. It need not hold the entire
worst case; insufficient capacity is a normal rejected result. Both output
areas, request, context, workspace and private stack are disjoint from one
another and from input extents. Read-only current and reference may overlap.
All required mapped extents must be non-wrapping. Only the first 169\,088 workspace
bytes belong to this entry even when more are supplied.

Validate request/context/workspace alignments, complete extents and full-width
controls before any picture or workspace access. I-picture reference is zero;
an I-call must not access a reference picture. Preserve request, input pictures,
context pointer/capacity, saved GPRs and incoming aligned stack pointer. Vector
registers are caller-saved. The public harness provides 65\,536 private stack bytes.

Return status zero and encoded byte count on success. Publish exactly those
stream bytes, the complete reconstruction and used count equal to eight times
the byte count. Return one/zero for invalid metadata, two/zero when the complete
chosen picture exceeds caller capacity, and three/zero after a failed child.
Every failure retains both caller output areas and the context. Workspace and
private stack may change after valid admission. The starter's development status
four is not a completed result.

Use \path{drafts/h263-picture-construction/main.asm}. The ordinary caller owns
constant 128-valued planes and a fresh writer context. Complete the picture entry,
then carry in your completed search, six-block predictor, block encoder and
macroblock syntax groups, including their children and data. The composition
helper accepts the earlier working formats and refuses an existing output:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 drafts/h263-picture-construction/compose.py \
    --source my-picture.asm --search my-search.asm \
    --prediction my-macroblock-prediction.asm \
    --block my-block.asm --syntax my-macroblock-syntax.asm \
    --output joined-picture.asm
\end{verbatim}
The preparation helper changes only the supplied constant planes and controls.
For a P-picture observation use \texttt{--picture 1 --tr 6 --gfid 1}. At
\texttt{picture\_caller\_return}, before its first instruction, stream, reconstruction,
context and returned registers are complete, while named status/byte fields still
hold their previous values. The default I-picture occupies 696 bytes with the
selected compact tables; an all-zero-residual P-picture can occupy 48 bytes with
skips. Equivalent coded macroblocks can increase the latter length.
