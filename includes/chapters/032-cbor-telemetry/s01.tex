\section{Step 1 Identify the object passed to the head reader}\label{cbor-s01}
Open a working copy of the supplied source and stop at \texttt{head\_call}. Follow \texttt{a0} into \texttt{source}, rather than interpreting the address itself as an item. The first bytes are \texttt{b8 04}; the selected count is fifty-two. A head call describes one item head, so the count supplied to it is an available extent, not its promised result.

Use \texttt{s01-1} and stop before \texttt{head\_call}.

\paragraph{Observe} a0 addresses b8 04 and a1 is fifty-two; no head has executed.

\paragraph{Check} What does fifty-two mean at this call?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] The number of map pairs.
\item[B.] The expected head width.
\item[C.] Available input bytes.
\end{itemize}
