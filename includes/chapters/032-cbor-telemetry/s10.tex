\section{Step 10 Retain the parent cursor across a helper call}\label{cbor-s10}
Inside the supplied frame, retain the current pointer, remaining extent, inherited depth and original pointer in saved registers. Call the completed head reader, propagate unfinished status four during construction, and reject a completed format failure. On success, advance the pointer and reduce the remaining count by the same head width. The original pointer will later turn the final cursor into a consumed count.

Author region \texttt{k\_head}. Use \texttt{s10-1} and stop before \texttt{after\_k\_head}.

\paragraph{Observe} After the root head, the cursor is source plus two and fifty bytes remain.

\paragraph{Check} Why retain the original pointer?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] To reread every earlier byte.
\item[B.] To use its low bits as the inherited depth.
\item[C.] To calculate the returned consumed extent.
\end{itemize}
