\section{Step 55 Observe capacity and exact-ending failures}\label{cbor-s55}
Prepare the thirty-six-byte encode capacity and confirm status two with zero count and an unchanged destination. Then prepare the separate trailing-byte decode recipe and confirm status one with zero count and an unchanged record. These experiments test different boundaries. A correct result on the valid trace alone does not establish either failure guarantee.

Use \texttt{s55-1} and stop before \texttt{encode\_return}. Use \texttt{s55-2} and stop before \texttt{decode\_return}.

\paragraph{Observe} The short encode and trailing-byte decode preserve their respective caller outputs.

\paragraph{Check} Which output may a completed failed decode change?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Only a prefix that looked valid.
\item[B.] Every field except the sample tail.
\item[C.] None of the caller record.
\end{itemize}
