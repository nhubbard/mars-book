\section{Change the representation while preserving a required value}
\begin{MARSExercise}{Move a field}
Move the counter pair to the beginning of the worked map without altering its value.
Record the new offsets of identifier and readings. The recovered flat record must stay
the same even though a fixed-offset decoder would read different bytes.
\end{MARSExercise}

\begin{MARSExercise}{Recognize a duplicate by value}
Construct a map containing key zero as both \texttt{00} and \texttt{18 00}. Explain which
mask bit the second key would try to set. Repeat with two distinct nested maps that
each legitimately contain key zero and explain why their masks must remain separate.
\end{MARSExercise}

\begin{MARSExercise}{Keep the eighth container but reject the ninth}
Wrap a small optional integer in arrays until the root and those arrays total eight
open containers. Add one more empty array at the deepest position. Identify the exact
entry at which inherited depth must prevent accepting that new container.
\end{MARSExercise}

\begin{MARSExercise}{Retain the complete counter}
Replace the counter with eight hexadecimal \texttt{ff} argument bytes. Write its local
record bytes, its unsigned mathematical value and the signed register display. Explain
why those observations must not cause a valid counter to fail a signed range check.
\end{MARSExercise}
