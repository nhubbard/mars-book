\section{Step 13 Advance over a bounded byte string}\label{cbor-s13}
For a byte string, reject an unsigned argument above thirty-two or above the remaining selected extent. After both checks, advance by its byte count and reduce the remaining count by that same amount. Its contents need not be loaded. Use \texttt{42 80 ff}: the two payload bytes resemble item tokens, but belong to this byte string.

Author region \texttt{k\_string}. Use \texttt{s13-1} and stop before \texttt{after\_k\_string}.

\paragraph{Observe} The three-byte item is consumed as one byte string, without interpreting 80 or ff.

\paragraph{Check} What must the walker do with the payload bytes?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] Recursively decode each byte as an item.
\item[B.] Stop at ff as a container break.
\item[C.] Prove their extent and advance over them.
\end{itemize}
