\section{Step 46 Preflight the record before touching output}\label{cbor-s46}
Retain the record, destination and unsigned capacity, then call the completed bound routine. Propagate an invalid-record or development result without reading identifier bytes or writing destination bytes. A successful bound supplies the exact required extent. Keep that extent across later helper calls instead of treating their head-width results as the whole output length.

Author region \texttt{e\_bound}. Use \texttt{s46-1} and stop before \texttt{after\_e\_bound}.

\paragraph{Observe} The bound result is thirty-seven and destination bytes remain sentinels.

\paragraph{Check} What can a successful bound establish for the encoder?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] That the caller's capacity is sufficient.
\item[B.] That identifier bytes have already been copied.
\item[C.] The exact output extent for this record.
\end{itemize}
