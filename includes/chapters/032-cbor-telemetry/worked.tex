\section{Walk the complete fifty-two-byte input}
The helper's trace begins with optional key three and ends with the required counter.
All offsets below are relative to the selected input base. A payload address is that
base plus the relevant offset, not the offset alone.

\tagpdfsetup{table/header-rows={1}}
\begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{rll}
    Offset & Bytes or extent                     & Meaning                            \\
    \hline
    0      & \texttt{b8 04}                      & Four-pair root map, head width two \\
    2      & \texttt{03}                         & Optional field key                 \\
    3..11  & \texttt{a2 01 82 42 80 ff 21 00 80} & Nested optional map                \\
    12     & \texttt{02}                         & Readings key                       \\
    13..14 & \texttt{98 0a}                      & Ten-element array head             \\
    15..34 & Twenty bytes                        & Ten supported reading items        \\
    35     & \texttt{01}                         & Identifier key                     \\
    36..37 & \texttt{58 03}                      & Three-byte identifier head         \\
    38..40 & \texttt{00 ff 41}                   & Identifier payload                 \\
    41..42 & \texttt{18 00}                      & Counter key zero in a wider head   \\
    43..51 & \texttt{1b 01 02 03 04 05 06 07 08} & Counter item                       \\
  \end{tabular}
\end{center}

The readings are zero, twenty-three, twenty-four, minus one, minus twenty-four, minus
twenty-five, 255, 256, 32767 and minus 32768. In the input, twenty-three uses
\texttt{18 17}, one byte longer than the writer's chosen form. When the final counter
passes, the cursor reaches fifty-two and the required-key mask is complete. Only then
is this record eligible for publication.
