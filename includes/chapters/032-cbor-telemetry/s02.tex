\section{Step 2 Establish failure results before reading}\label{cbor-s02}
Clear the head length and both extra results before inspecting input. Reject a zero available count before loading the initial byte; otherwise load that byte unsigned. Leave a format-failure status until a supported complete head has been established. This ordering makes the zero-count path safe even when the supplied pointer cannot be read.

Author region \texttt{h\_prefix}. Use \texttt{s02-1} and stop before \texttt{after\_h\_prefix}. 

\paragraph{Observe} The initial byte is 184 and the cleared head results remain zero.

\paragraph{Check} Which input permits the first load?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] A count of at least nine.
\item[B.] A pointer with its low bit clear.
\item[C.] A nonzero unsigned available count.
\end{itemize}
