\section{Step 36 Read the flat prefix, not serialized input}\label{cbor-s36}
Retain the flat-record pointer and read its two unsigned thirty-two-bit lengths. The counter occupies the first eight bytes; lengths are at offsets eight and twelve. The helper is not parsing \texttt{source}. Keep the record pointer stable across later size-helper calls so an argument loaded into \texttt{a0} does not lose the object's address.

Author region \texttt{b\_fields}. Use \texttt{s36-1} and stop before \texttt{after\_b\_fields}. 

\paragraph{Observe} The trace prefix supplies identifier length three and ten samples.

\paragraph{Check} Where does the bound routine get its lengths?
\begin{itemize}
\item[A.] From the original input head.
\item[B.] From the caller's output capacity.
\item[C.] From the flat record prefix.
\end{itemize}
