\section{Sources and scope}
The wire type and head conventions follow
\href{https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8949.html#section-3}{RFC 8949, Section 3}.
Its \href{https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8949.html#section-4.2.1}{core deterministic encoding requirements}
provide the shortest-argument and encoded-key-order rules used by this writer.
The particular telemetry fields, numeric and nesting bounds, small-key map policy,
projection of optional fields, local record layout and five entry contracts are
teaching choices, not a universal CBOR application schema.

The independent \href{https://cbor2.readthedocs.io/en/latest/}{cbor2 implementation}
is used for interoperability observations with its version recorded alongside private
verification evidence. A library's acceptance is not the definition of this bounded
profile. The fixed inputs are host-verified. Both complete private prototypes pass 648
head cases, 1000 walker cases and 2436 telemetry cases each, at ordinary and above-4-GiB
addresses, for 16336 simulator cases. Twelve new assembled telemetry regressions are
rejected, in addition to the earlier eight head/walker regressions. Twelve actual
MIPS-produced encodings are decoded independently with cbor2. These are finite checks,
not a proof for every input. The cumulative draft now has fifty-six steps and fifty-two unfilled regions. All 114
prepared observation/layout combinations assemble, and thirty-two initial unfinished
entry/caller cases pass. Completed cumulative checkpoint execution, public feedback,
separate chapter archives and actual IDE captures remain subsequent work. Earlier chapters are retained
for the later substantive editing pass.
