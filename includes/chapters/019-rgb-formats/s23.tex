\section{Step 23 Gather eight zero-extended channel values}\label{rgb-s23}

Shuffle from \texttt{w0} as the right source and \texttt{w1} as the left source, using the prepared destination controls. The alternating component and zero selectors make eight halfwords per channel. The next arithmetic uses halfword lanes, so a zero high byte is part of the representation invariant rather than cosmetic padding.

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{vector\_pack\_shuffle}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}

    vshf.b $w4, $w1, $w0

Use \texttt{s23-1} (count 8) from the companion input recipes. Pause at \texttt{pack\_ready} before comparing the construction state.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The low halfwords of \texttt{w4}, \texttt{w5} and \texttt{w6} are the eight unsigned red, green and blue component values in pixel order.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why place a zero after each component byte?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} To add another output pixel.
\item \textbf{B} To make that halfword contain only the component value.
\item \textbf{C} To preserve the previous shuffle controls.
\end{itemize}
