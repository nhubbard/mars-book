\section{Step 41 Store the bounded overlapping output windows}\label{rgb-s41}

Store the first window at output offset zero and the second at offset eight. Both transfers fit the twenty-four-byte output group. Repeated stores inside this logical slice are allowed. The second store must begin at eight; placing it at sixteen would extend to byte thirty-one and violate ownership.
