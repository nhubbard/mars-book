\section{Step 42 Finish the group loop and scalar tail}\label{rgb-s42}

Advance packed input by sixteen bytes, RGB output by twenty-four and count by eight pixels. Repeat while another full group remains, then fall through to the scalar tail. Both public functions now implement every legal count with vector groups and byte-safe tails. Their original count remains in \texttt{v0}.

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{vector\_unpack\_next}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Eight, nine and sixteen packed pixels expand correctly; the nine-pixel input needs one scalar tail pixel.
