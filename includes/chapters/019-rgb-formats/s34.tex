\section{Step 34 Expand red through two nonoverlapping parts}\label{rgb-s34}

Retain a copy shifted right by two, shift red left by three, then OR those parts. Eight red fields now become eight full eight-bit code values in halfwords. The high bytes remain zero, which will matter when the later packing operation selects even byte positions.
