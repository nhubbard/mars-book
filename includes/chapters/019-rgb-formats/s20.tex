\section{Step 20 Load reusable channel selector vectors}\label{rgb-s20}

Load the supplied red, green and blue control tables into \texttt{w16}, \texttt{w17} and \texttt{w18}. Each table has sixteen bytes: a component selector followed by selector 64 for the zero high byte of each halfword. The controls are immutable constants; retain them while working destinations are overwritten each block.

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{vector\_pack\_controls}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.
