\section{Step 38 Alternate the red and green bytes}\label{rgb-s38}

Interleave the right, low halves of the compacted registers with red as the right source and green as the left. The sixteen-byte result alternates red and green in pixel order. The final shuffle can combine that sequence with the separate blue halfwords to construct complete RGB triples.

Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{vector\_unpack\_interleave}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.
