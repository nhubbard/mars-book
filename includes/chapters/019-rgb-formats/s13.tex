\section{Step 13 Reconstruct the little-endian halfword}\label{rgb-s13}

Load the low and high bytes unsigned. Shift the high byte left by eight and OR it into the low byte. This reconstructs a zero-extended value from zero through 65535 without requiring a halfword-aligned source. Each of its component fields can now be extracted from the same numerical value.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{unpack\_load}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.
