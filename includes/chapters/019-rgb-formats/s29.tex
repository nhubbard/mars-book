\section{Step 29 Repeat full groups and hand off the tail}\label{rgb-s29}

Advance input by twenty-four bytes, output by sixteen and remaining count by eight pixels. Test eligibility again; repeat a full block when possible, otherwise fall through to the existing scalar bound check. A count exactly divisible by eight reaches that check with zero and performs no tail access.

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{vector\_pack\_next}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    daddiu $a0, $a0, 24
    daddiu $a1, $a1, 16
    addiu $a2, $a2, -8
    sltiu $t0, $a2, 8
    beq $t0, $zero, pack_block
    nop
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s29-1} (count 8), \texttt{s29-2} (count 9), \texttt{s29-3} (count 16) from the companion input recipes. Pause at \texttt{pack\_ready} before comparing the construction state.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Eight, nine and sixteen pixels all pack correctly. The nine-pixel call executes one full group and one scalar pixel.
