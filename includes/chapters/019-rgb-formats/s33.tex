\section{Step 33 Isolate red, green and blue lanes}\label{rgb-s33}

Extract red with a halfword shift by eleven. Shift green by five and mask with a vector of 63, then mask the original packed values with 31 for blue. The masks are halfword values, so their high bytes are zero. This removes other fields before expansion.

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{vector\_unpack\_fields}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    srli.h $w1, $w0, 11
    srli.h $w2, $w0, 5
    ldi.h $w7, 63
    and.v $w2, $w2, $w7
    ldi.h $w7, 31
    and.v $w3, $w0, $w7
\end{minted}
Use \texttt{s33-1} (count 8) from the companion input recipes. Pause at \texttt{unpack\_ready} before comparing the construction state.

\subsection{Expected observation}
\texttt{w1}, \texttt{w2} and \texttt{w3} contain the retained five-, six- and five-bit channel fields.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why is green masked after shifting?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} To swap byte order.
\item \textbf{B} To restore the original low bits.
\item \textbf{C} To remove the red bits that remain above it.
\end{itemize}
