\section{Step 35 Expand all green values}\label{rgb-s35}

Repeat the green high bits using right shift four, left shift two and OR. The result fills one byte in each halfword and reaches 255 for the maximum six-bit input. Reusing the five-bit expansion shifts would produce the wrong scale and repeated bits.

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{vector\_unpack\_green}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided entry and return.

Each \texttt{w2} halfword equals the scalar green expansion.
