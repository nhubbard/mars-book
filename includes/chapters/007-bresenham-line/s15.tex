\section{Step 15 Apply the independent x
  decision}\label{007-bresenham-line-s15-step-15-apply-the-independent-x-decision}

The condition for stepping x is e2 \ensuremath{\geq} dy. \texttt{slt} computes the
opposite strict comparison, e2 \textless{} dy; \texttt{bne} skips the
update only when that opposite is true. An x step adds the negative
vertical difference to err and increments x by one.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{007-bresenham-line-s15-write-or-inspect}

Append the x decision and its join label.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    slt $t3, $t2, $s5
    bne $t3, $zero, skip_x
    nop
    addu $s7, $s7, $s5
    addiu $s0, $s0, 1
skip_x:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{007-bresenham-line-s15-expected-observation}

For the first default update, x becomes two and err becomes \ensuremath{-}1; the
saved e2 is still four.
