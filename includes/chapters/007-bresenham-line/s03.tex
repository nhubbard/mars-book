\section{Step 3 Load the four endpoint
  coordinates}\label{007-bresenham-line-s03-step-3-load-the-four-endpoint-coordinates}

\texttt{la} obtains the endpoint-block address. The four \texttt{lw}
instructions read consecutive four-byte fields into working registers.
Keep the source words unchanged when reordering the working endpoints.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{007-bresenham-line-s03-write-or-inspect}

Append the endpoint loads.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    la $t0, endpoints
    lw $s0, 0($t0)
    lw $s1, 4($t0)
    lw $s2, 8($t0)
    lw $s3, 12($t0)
inputs_loaded:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{007-bresenham-line-s03-expected-observation}

\texttt{\$s0}, \texttt{\$s1} hold the first point; \texttt{\$s2},
\texttt{\$s3} hold the second. The default is 1,1,6,4.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{007-bresenham-line-s03-check-your-understanding}

What is the offset of x1 in this four-word block?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} One byte.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Four bytes.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Eight bytes.
\end{itemize}
