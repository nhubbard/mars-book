\section{Step 7 Compute horizontal and signed vertical
  differences}\label{007-bresenham-line-s07-step-7-compute-horizontal-and-signed-vertical-differences}

\texttt{subu} computes a word difference without an overflow exception.
Here all coordinates are zero through seven, so each difference is small
and exact. The ordered horizontal difference cannot be negative.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{007-bresenham-line-s07-write-or-inspect}

Append the difference calculations.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    subu $s4, $s2, $s0
    subu $s5, $s3, $s1
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{007-bresenham-line-s07-expected-observation}

The default horizontal difference is five and signed vertical difference
is three.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{007-bresenham-line-s07-check-your-understanding}

Can the ordered horizontal difference be negative?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} No, because endpoint ordering made x0 no greater than x1.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Yes, for every steep line.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Only for a white color.
\end{itemize}
