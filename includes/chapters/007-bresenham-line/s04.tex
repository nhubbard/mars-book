\section{Step 4 Specify canonical order before
rounding}\label{007-bresenham-line-s04-step-4-specify-canonical-order-before-rounding}

Lexicographic order compares x first and y only when the x values are
equal. Applying one fixed direction before tie decisions makes reversed
inputs describe the same raster.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{007-bresenham-line-s04-write-or-inspect}

Apply the ordering rule to the worked endpoint pairs.

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{007-bresenham-line-s04-expected-observation}

(6,4) to (1,1) is reordered to (1,1) to (6,4). Equal-x inputs are
ordered upward in numeric y.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{007-bresenham-line-s04-check-your-understanding}
