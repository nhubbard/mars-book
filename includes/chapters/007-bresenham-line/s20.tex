\section{Step 20 Cover steep, falling, and axis-aligned
lines}\label{007-bresenham-line-s20-step-20-cover-steep-falling-and-axis-aligned-lines}

A steep line sometimes advances y without x. A negative slope uses
direction \ensuremath{-}1. A horizontal or vertical line leaves its other coordinate
unchanged. Canonical endpoint ordering also handles originally leftward
inputs.

inspect}\label{007-bresenham-line-s20-write-or-inspect}
