\section{Step 2 Read the frame and input
  contract}\label{007-bresenham-line-s02-step-2-read-the-frame-and-input-contract}

The endpoint block stores four coordinates, not four addresses. The
frame is a separate row-major word array, and its existing off-line
cells must survive.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{007-bresenham-line-s02-write-or-inspect}

Locate the frame, guards, endpoint block, and color word.

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{007-bresenham-line-s02-expected-observation}

An 8 by 8 frame uses 64 words, or 256 bytes.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{007-bresenham-line-s02-check-your-understanding}

What should happen to a cell outside the selected line?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} It must always become black.
  \item
        \textbf{B} It must keep its complete original word.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Only its red byte must survive.
\end{itemize}
