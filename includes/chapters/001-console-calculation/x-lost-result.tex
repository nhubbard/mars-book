\section{A result overwritten during syscall
setup}\label{001-console-calculation-x-lost-result-a-result-overwritten-during-syscall-setup}

Consider this incorrect setup after the result has been computed in
\texttt{\$t0}:

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
move $v0, $t0
li $v0, 1
syscall
\end{minted}

The first line copies the result into \texttt{\$v0}. The second replaces
it with the selector 1. Neither supplies the required integer argument
in \texttt{\$a0}. Repair the argument setup by putting the result in
\texttt{\$a0} before requesting service 1.
