\section{Step 27 Gather the left contribution to each sextet}\label{base64-s27}

Load the private vector, then load selectors into the gather destination. The gather overwrites those selectors with source bytes. Apply per-byte left and logical right shifts for the left contribution, retaining the independent group structure.

Replace the body of region \texttt{enc12\_left}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    ld.b $w0, 0($sp)
    dla $t1, enc_left
    ld.b $w1, 0($t1)
    vshf.b $w1, $w0, $w0
    dla $t1, enc_left_shift
    ld.b $w2, 0($t1)
    sll.b $w1, $w1, $w2
    dla $t1, enc_left_right
    ld.b $w2, 0($t1)
    srl.b $w1, $w1, $w2
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s27-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{after\_enc12\_left} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

The working vector holds the shifted left contribution for all sixteen sextets.
