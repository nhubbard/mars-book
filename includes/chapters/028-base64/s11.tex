\section{Step 11 Map lowercase and numeric intervals}\label{base64-s11}

Apply the same unsigned-width test to lowercase and digits. The supplied return adds the appropriate sextet offset. Keep the character code separate from its biased difference: the offset into a range is not yet necessarily its final sextet.

Replace the body of region \texttt{digit\_ranges}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.
