\section{Step 12 Handle the final symbols and invalid octets}\label{base64-s12}

Recognize plus and slash explicitly after the ordinary ranges. Return minus one for every other octet. The supplied caller records a successful sextet as data and translates invalid or unfinished helper results into its status control; those caller statuses are not the helper numerical return.

Replace the body of region \texttt{digit\_symbols}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

Plus and slash return 62 and 63; at-sign returns minus one.
