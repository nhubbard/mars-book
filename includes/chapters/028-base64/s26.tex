\section{Step 26 Copy exactly twelve bytes to private storage}\label{base64-s26}

The supplied kernel frame contains sixteen private bytes. Initialize them to zero and copy exactly twelve selected source bytes. The remaining four zeros support internal lane layout. They do not extend the caller input interval or permit an oversized source load.

Replace the body of region \texttt{enc12\_copy}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.
