\section{Step 16 Validate the final pair of pads}\label{base64-s16}

When two input characters remain, require the next one to be another pad. Check the preceding sextet low four bits, then subtract two from the provisional size. Keep this case separate from merely ignoring equal signs: both position and otherwise-unused bits constrain valid text.

Replace the body of region \texttt{val\_two}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

validate_two:
