\section{Step 35 Add the digit contribution}\label{base64-s35}

Mask ASCII digits and translate them to sextets52 through61. The character code for zero is48; adding four produces its Base64 sextet 52. Keep that character code distinct from a binary zero byte.

Replace the body of region \texttt{dec16\_digit}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    ldi.b $w1, 47
    clt_u.b $w2, $w1, $w0
    ldi.b $w1, 58
    clt_u.b $w3, $w0, $w1
    and.v $w2, $w2, $w3
    ldi.b $w1, 4
    addv.b $w4, $w0, $w1
    and.v $w4, $w4, $w2
    or.v $w5, $w5, $w4
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s35-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{after\_dec16\_digit} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.
