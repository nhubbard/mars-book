\section{Step 17 Inspect the supplied restores}\label{base64-s17}

Locate each provided frame and its matching development return. Whole routines use development status four, while the classifier uses minus two. That distinction lets a valid sextet four remain numerical data and lets unfinished helpers propagate through nested calls.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Every allocated frame is restored before an unfinished result reaches its caller.
