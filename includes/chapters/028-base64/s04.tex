\section{Step 4 Read only the available source bytes}\label{base64-s04}

Start each group by loading its first byte and clearing registers for the other two. Load a second and third byte only when their positions lie inside the remaining interval. A missing byte contributes zero bits without authorizing a read beyond the caller input.

Replace the body of region \texttt{enc\_load}, retaining its BEGIN/END markers and the labels in the excerpt.

\Needspace{17\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
encode_scalar_group:
    lbu $t0, 0($s0)
    move $t1, $zero
    move $t2, $zero
    sltiu $t3, $s1, 2
    bnez $t3, encode_pack
    nop
    lbu $t1, 1($s0)
    sltiu $t3, $s1, 3
    bnez $t3, encode_pack
    nop
    lbu $t2, 2($s0)
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s04-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{encode\_pack} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The Man fixture loads decimal 77, 97, 110 before any output store.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why clear the missing-byte registers?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Their zero bits form canonical short-tail sextets.
\item \textbf{B} A byte load always clears every temporary.
\item \textbf{C} The caller guarantees three readable bytes for every tail.
\end{itemize}
