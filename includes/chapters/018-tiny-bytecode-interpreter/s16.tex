\section{Step 16 Reject targets at or beyond image length}\label{vm-s16}

Load the branch operand as an unsigned byte and require target less than length. This also rejects the one-past-end position. Perform this check before shifting a mask: a variable shift is not itself a range test and can discard high shift-count bits.

\Needspace{14\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch in \texttt{target\_range}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    lbu $t3, 1($t0)
    sltu $t4, $t3, $s1
    beq $t4, $zero, bad_target
    nop
\end{minted}
\subsection{Expected observation}
For \texttt{JMP} two in a two-byte image, validation reports minus three at branch offset zero.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why is a target equal to length invalid?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} It wraps to the first operand.
  \item \textbf{B} It is a signed displacement.
  \item \textbf{C} No instruction begins at the one-past-end position.
\end{itemize}
