\section{Step 43 Retire a branch to its validated target}\label{vm-s43}

Load the unsigned target byte as the next PC and join common retirement. Range and boundary membership were established before execution, so this path performs no stack operation. A branch to its own address still retires and consumes budget.

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch in \texttt{jump}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    lbu $t8, 1($t0)
    beq $zero, $zero, vm_retire
    nop
\end{minted}
\subsection{Expected observation}
\texttt{JMP} zero with budget five returns status zero, PC zero and retired count five.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Does a self-branch consume guest budget?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} No, because PC does not change.
  \item \textbf{B} Only after the second visit.
  \item \textbf{C} Yes, for every successful retirement.
\end{itemize}
