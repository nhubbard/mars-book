\section{Step 37 Choose the checked arithmetic operation}\label{vm-s37}

Select \texttt{ADD}, \texttt{SUB} or \texttt{MUL} from the cached opcode; the remaining arithmetic opcode is \texttt{DIV}. The operands and destination address stay in temporary registers until a candidate has passed its check. Each operation transfers to the same representability test.
