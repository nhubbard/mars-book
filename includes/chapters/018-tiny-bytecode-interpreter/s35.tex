\section{Step 35 Exchange two live words without changing depth}\label{vm-s35}

Require two values and address the older of the top pair. Load both words before storing either, then write them in the opposite order. Preserve depth and select the next byte. Reusing one loaded value for both stores would lose an operand.

\Needspace{23\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch in \texttt{swap}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    sltiu $t1, $s7, 2
    bne $t1, $zero, underflow_stack
    nop
    addiu $t1, $s7, -2
    sll $t1, $t1, 2
    daddu $t1, $s3, $t1
    lw $t2, 0($t1)
    lw $t3, 4($t1)
    sw $t3, 0($t1)
    sw $t2, 4($t1)
    addiu $t8, $s6, 1
    beq $zero, $zero, vm_retire
    nop
\end{minted}
\subsection{Expected observation}
Seven, twelve, \texttt{SWAP} leaves twelve below seven.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why load both words before the first store?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} To retain both original values for the exchange.
  \item \textbf{B} To increase capacity.
  \item \textbf{C} To convert their bytes into opcodes.
\end{itemize}
