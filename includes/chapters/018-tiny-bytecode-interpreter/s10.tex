\section{Step 10 Require all encoded operand bytes}\label{vm-s10}

Subtract the current cursor from length and compare the remaining count with the selected size. Both quantities are small nonnegative values in a valid decode progression. An insufficient remainder selects truncated-image status. Do this before interpreting or reading missing operand bytes.
