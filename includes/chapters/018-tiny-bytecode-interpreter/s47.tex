\section{Step 47 Distinguish yielding from a stack fault}\label{vm-s47}

Set capacity two and compare fuel two with fuel three. Both calls begin fresh and reach the \texttt{DUP} at byte four with values zero and three. The smaller budget returns before attempting \texttt{DUP}. The larger call attempts it, discovers no room and preserves the two live values.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Fuel two yields with PC four and fault minus one. Fuel three reports status minus six and fault four; both have depth two and retired count two.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Does the faulting \texttt{DUP} increase retired count?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} No.
  \item \textbf{B} Yes, because it was decoded.
  \item \textbf{C} Only when its target is in range.
\end{itemize}
