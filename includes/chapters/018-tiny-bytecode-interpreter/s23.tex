\section{Step 23 Share the arithmetic operand path}\label{vm-s23}

Route all four arithmetic opcodes to vm\_binary. That common path will enforce two live operands and locate the left and right values. A later inner dispatch selects the actual arithmetic operation while retaining the original opcode.

\Needspace{22\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch in \texttt{dispatch\_arithmetic}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    li $t1, 16
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_binary
    nop
    li $t1, 17
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_binary
    nop
    li $t1, 18
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_binary
    nop
    li $t1, 19
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_binary
    nop
\end{minted}
\subsection{Expected observation}
An \texttt{ADD} byte selects vm\_binary; its stack handling remains unfinished.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why retain the opcode through the common entry?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} The arithmetic operation still has to be selected.
\item \textbf{B} It is the public return status.
\item \textbf{C} It is always equal to stack depth.
\end{itemize}
