\small
\begin{enumerate}
\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
\item The blue \texttt{nop} at \texttt{vm\_ready} follows the function return. Descriptor outputs are published; the caller\textquotesingle s separate result stores are still ahead.
\item \texttt{\$v0 = 0} reports budget exhaustion; \texttt{\$v1 = 4} is the retired count. Two pushes, a duplicate and the conditional branch have retired. The nonzero branch test was popped, so execution would next attempt the swap at byte seven.
\item This is the same valid eighteen-byte image as the halt fixture. Its \texttt{31 10} record at byte five is \texttt{JZ} with target hexadecimal \texttt{10}, or decimal 16. A nonzero test selects the following instruction at decimal byte seven.
\item The descriptor contains PC 7, depth 2, retired 4 and status 0. Fault is $-1$: exhaustion is a normal stopping condition. The budget check occurs before the next fetch. A later call starts over rather than resuming this returned PC.
\item The first two stack words are 0 and 3, in bottom-to-top order. The third physical word also contains 3 from the duplicate, but depth excludes it after \texttt{JZ} pops its test. Reading mapped bytes alone does not identify the logical stack.
\item Fuel precedes the caller\textquotesingle s two result words and guard. Both results still contain 99 (\texttt{63 00 00 00}); continuing copies \texttt{v0} and \texttt{v1} into them.
\end{enumerate}
