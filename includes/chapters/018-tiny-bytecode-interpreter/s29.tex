\section{Step 29 Assemble an unaligned little-endian constant}\label{vm-s29}

Check push capacity first, then load four unsigned payload bytes. Shift the higher bytes into place and combine them. The word shift of the high byte supplies the signed-word representation when bit thirty-one is set. Byte loads keep this path valid for unaligned image addresses. Storage and retirement remain in the next edit.
