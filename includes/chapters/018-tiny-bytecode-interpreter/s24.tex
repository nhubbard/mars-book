\section{Step 24 Route the four stack operations}\label{vm-s24}

Select separate \texttt{DUP}, \texttt{DROP}, \texttt{SWAP} and \texttt{OVER} entries. Their live-value requirements and depth changes differ, so sharing their opcode width does not make their execution effects interchangeable. \texttt{DUP} and \texttt{OVER} will later share the actual copy after choosing different source indices.
