\section{Step 21 Check budget before the next guest fetch}\label{vm-s21}

Test remaining guest budget first. With positive budget, require PC below length before loading an opcode. The validated control flow can reach a beginning or the image end; the latter produces the end-fetch fault when an instruction is actually attempted. The input address still requires full-width addition.
