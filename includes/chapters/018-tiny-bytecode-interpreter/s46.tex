\section{Step 46 Stop at a budget boundary}\label{vm-s46}

Change fuel to four and run from the beginning. The first four retired instructions are \texttt{PUSH8} zero, \texttt{PUSH8} three, \texttt{DUP} and \texttt{JZ}. The nonzero test falls through, but \texttt{JZ} still removes its duplicate. The next guest instruction has not been attempted when the budget return occurs.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Status zero, PC seven, depth two, retired four, fault minus one and live values zero then three.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
What happened to the nonzero test value?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} It remains as a third live value.
  \item \textbf{B} It was converted to a branch offset.
  \item \textbf{C} \texttt{JZ} popped it while falling through.
\end{itemize}
