\section{Step 51 Write another interpreter under the same contract}\label{vm-s51}

In free-writing mode, retain run\_vm and the caller/data harness while replacing private implementation details. A table, bitmap, byte map or another proven membership strategy can work. Preserve complete-image priority, checked arithmetic, fresh-call behavior, bounds and instruction retirement before measuring an optimization.
