\section{Step 7 Recognize the one-byte stack operations}\label{vm-s07}

Recognize decimal 32 through 35 as the four stack opcodes. They also have encoded size one. Structural validity is independent of whether the future execution stack has enough live values; a well-formed \texttt{DROP} can still fault if execution later reaches it with depth zero.

\subsection{Check your understanding}
What can a structurally valid stack instruction still do at runtime?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Change its encoded size.
\item \textbf{B} Turn an operand into a new opcode.
\item \textbf{C} Fail for insufficient live values.
\end{itemize}
