\section{Step 22 Route halt and both push forms}\label{vm-s22}

Use the fetched opcode to select halt, \texttt{PUSH8} or \texttt{PUSH32}. These are native branches to interpreter labels, not direct guest jumps to native addresses. The handlers remain temporary returns until their later edits; selecting a path is not yet executing its complete guest effect.

\Needspace{18\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch in \texttt{dispatch\_constants}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    beq $t9, $zero, vm_halt
    nop
    li $t1, 1
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_push8
    nop
    li $t1, 2
    beq $t9, $t1, vm_push32
    nop
\end{minted}
\subsection{Expected observation}
A halt byte reaches vm\_halt without retiring at this unfinished stage.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
What is the destination of this native dispatch branch?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} The guest operand byte as a native address.
  \item \textbf{B} A caller-selected stack slot.
  \item \textbf{C} Interpreter code that implements the selected guest operation.
\end{itemize}
