\section{Step 34 Remove one logical operand}\label{vm-s34}

Check that a top value exists, decrement depth, select PC plus one, and retire. Reducing depth makes the old top slot inactive. Its stored bits need not be cleared: inactive cells inside capacity are unspecified workspace, while the live prefix defines the stack state.

\Needspace{17\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary branch in \texttt{drop}. Preserve surrounding labels and the provided frame and exits.

\Needspace{10\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    sltiu $t1, $s7, 1

    addiu $s7, $s7, -1
    addiu $t8, $s6, 1
    beq $zero, $zero, vm_retire
    nop

\subsection{Expected observation}
Pushing seven and dropping it before halt leaves depth zero after three retirements.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Does \texttt{DROP} need to zero its former top slot?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} Always.
  \item \textbf{B} Only for a negative value.
  \item \textbf{C} No; that slot becomes inactive.
\end{itemize}
