\section{Step 1 Separate the program from the interpreter}\label{vm-s01}

The MIPS source implements a second, smaller instruction set. Its code\_a bytes are data to MARS and instructions to run\_vm. A native debugger step executes one MIPS instruction; one guest retirement can require many of those steps. Use byte offsets for guest PC and full addresses for native loads.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The completed default call retires thirty-three guest instructions and returns one live value, six.
