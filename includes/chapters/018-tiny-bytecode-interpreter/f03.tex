\small
\begin{enumerate}
\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}
\item The blue \texttt{nop} at \texttt{vm\_ready} follows the function return. Descriptor outputs are published; the caller\textquotesingle s separate result stores are still ahead.
\item \texttt{\$v0 = -6} reports stack overflow; \texttt{\$v1 = 2} counts only the two successful pushes. Fuel 3 permits an attempt at the following duplicate, but a failing instruction consumes no retirement unit.
\item The unchanged image begins with \texttt{01 00 01 03 20}: push zero, push three, then duplicate. The duplicate is the opcode at decimal byte four. Its input is structurally valid; the failure occurs during execution.
\item The descriptor contains PC 4, depth 2, retired 2 and status $-6$. Its capacity input is 2 and fault is 4. PC and fault identify the same runtime instruction. The failed duplicate leaves PC at its own byte rather than advancing.
\item Only two words belong to the selected capacity: 0 and 3. Although the backing allocation has more mapped cells, those cells do not extend this call\textquotesingle s capacity. The duplicate leaves both live operands unchanged and makes no third stack store.
\item Fuel precedes the caller\textquotesingle s two result words and guard. Both results still contain 99 (\texttt{63 00 00 00}); continuing copies \texttt{v0} and \texttt{v1} into them.
\end{enumerate}
