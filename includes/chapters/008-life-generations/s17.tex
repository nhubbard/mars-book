\section{Step 17 Complete all rows before changing
roles}\label{008-life-generations-s17-step-17-complete-all-rows-before-changing-roles}

Advance the interior column through eight, then the row through eight.
Every destination cell is computed from the same current board. No halo
cell is part of the write sweep.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s17-write-or-inspect}

Append the sweep-loop control.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    addiu $s5, $s5, 1
    slti $t0, $s5, 9
    bne $t0, $zero, cell_loop
    nop
    addiu $s4, $s4, 1
    slti $t0, $s4, 9
    bne $t0, $zero, cell_row
    nop
generation_computed:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s17-expected-observation}

At \texttt{generation\_computed}, the destination is complete and the
current board is still the old generation.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s17-check-your-understanding}

Which board supplied every neighbor in this sweep?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} The unchanged current board.
\item
  \textbf{B} Whichever byte was written most recently.
\item
  \textbf{C} The framebuffer.
\end{itemize}
