\section{Step 23 Keep the external world dead at every
step}\label{008-life-generations-s23-step-23-keep-the-external-world-dead-at-every-step}

The halo remains zero throughout execution. This models a finite board
whose exterior is permanently dead. It is not a torus, and it differs
from an infinite universe once off-board births could influence later
generations.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s23-write-or-inspect}

Predict the top-left corner pattern and an edge-moving pattern.

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s23-expected-observation}

No neighbor wraps from the left edge to the right edge or from the top
to the bottom.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s23-check-your-understanding}

What happens to a possible birth outside this finite board?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} It is suppressed by the boundary definition.
\item
  \textbf{B} It appears on the opposite edge.
\item
  \textbf{C} It enlarges the allocation automatically.
\end{itemize}
