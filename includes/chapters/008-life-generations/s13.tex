\section{Step 13 Address matching source and destination
  cells}\label{008-life-generations-s13-step-13-address-matching-source-and-destination-cells}

The physical offset is ten times the interior row number plus its
interior column, both from one through eight. Shifts by three and one
form 8\ensuremath{\times}row+2\ensuremath{\times}row. Adding the same offset to the two bases gives
corresponding cells.

  inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s13-write-or-inspect}

Append the destination-sweep setup and address calculation.

The first source cell is current+11 and the first destination cell is

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s13-check-your-understanding}

Why use the same offset at both bases?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} The two buffers have different row widths.
  \item
        \textbf{B} They represent the same logical cell in two generations.
  \item
        \textbf{C} It makes the pointers alias.
\end{itemize}
