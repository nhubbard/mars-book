\section{Complete a destination before exchanging
roles}\label{008-life-generations-p08-complete-a-destination-before-exchanging-roles}

During a sweep, some destination cells are new and others may still be
stale. None of them may supply a neighbor count for the same sweep. The
current pointer continues to identify the old complete generation until
all sixty-four destination bytes have been computed.

At \texttt{generation\_computed}, the next generation exists but has not
taken the current role. The three pointer moves exchange current and
destination addresses using a temporary. They copy no board bytes.
Incrementing the working generation number and returning to the renderer
then makes the newly current board available for display and
publication.

The old source remains useful: it is the previous complete generation
and becomes the next destination. Its interior will be overwritten
during the following sweep. This is why source protection is
phase-dependent rather than a permanent ban on writes to one physical
allocation. The halo, seed, requested count, and guards remain protected
throughout.
