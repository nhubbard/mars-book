\section{Step 28 State the phase invariant and
  limits}\label{008-life-generations-s28-step-28-state-the-phase-invariant-and-limits}

During a sweep, current is the completed generation g and is read-only;
destination is a partially computed g+1. After all cells, exchange
roles, render the new current, and publish its number. The loop
terminates after the requested number of such transitions.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s28-write-or-inspect}

Write the invariant and propose a separately specified larger-board or
wraparound exercise.

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{008-life-generations-s28-expected-observation}

Changing size or boundary conditions requires new layouts,
neighbor-address reasoning, and expected-state checks.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s28-check-your-understanding}

Why can cell traversal order vary safely here?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} The same source generation supplies every cell and
        destination writes cannot change it.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Life ignores neighbors.
  \item
        \textbf{C} The frame stores bytes instead of words.
\end{itemize}
