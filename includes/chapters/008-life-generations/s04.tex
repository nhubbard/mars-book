\section{Step 4 Load current and destination
pointers}\label{008-life-generations-s04-step-4-load-current-and-destination-pointers}

The reference begins with \texttt{board\_a} as current and
\texttt{board\_b} as destination. It loads the requested evolution count
and initializes the working generation number to zero.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s04-write-or-inspect}

Append the pointer and count setup.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    la $s0, board_a
    la $s1, board_b
    la $t0, generations
    lw $s2, 0($t0)
    move $s3, $zero
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s04-expected-observation}

\texttt{\$s0} identifies \texttt{board\_a}, \texttt{\$s1}
\texttt{board\_b}, \texttt{\$s2} is the requested count, and
\texttt{\$s3} is zero.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s04-check-your-understanding}

What does a requested count of two mean?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} Display only generations one and two.
\item
  \textbf{B} Initialize generation zero, then evolve twice.
\item
  \textbf{C} Copy the seed twice without evolving.
\end{itemize}
