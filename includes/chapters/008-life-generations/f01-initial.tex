\small
\begin{enumerate}
\item The blue instruction begins the first render. The seed-copy loops have finished; no framebuffer store or generation-publication store has executed.
\item \texttt{\$s0}=\texttt{0x10010148} selects \texttt{board\_a}; \texttt{\$s1}=\texttt{0x100101b0} selects \texttt{board\_b}. The copied seed is the complete current board. \texttt{\$s2}=2 is the requested number of updates, and \texttt{\$s3}=0 identifies this initial board.
\item All 64 framebuffer words are still zero. The live cells already stored in board A do not appear here until \texttt{render} converts the board bytes to pixel words. The outline in the upper-left cell is the native selection marker.
\item The first four bytes at \texttt{0x10010220} are \texttt{ff ff ff ff}: the unpublished marker $-1$. The next four bytes, \texttt{26 59 41 31}, are the unchanged guard word \texttt{0x31415926} in little-endian order. The working counter and the published generation describe different stages here.
\end{enumerate}
