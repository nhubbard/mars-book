\section{Step 5 Prepare to copy the seed
interior}\label{008-life-generations-s05-step-5-prepare-to-copy-the-seed-interior}

The first interior byte is eleven bytes after the board base. The copy
walks eight cells per row and uses separate row and column counters.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s05-write-or-inspect}

Append the seed-copy cursor setup.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    la $t0, seed
    daddiu $t1, $s0, 11
    li $t2, 8
seed_row:
    li $t3, 8
seed_cell:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s05-expected-observation}

The input cursor starts at seed and the destination cursor at
\texttt{board\_a}+11.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s05-check-your-understanding}

Why is the first destination not \texttt{board\_a} itself?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} Offset zero is part of the dead halo.
\item
  \textbf{B} The first eleven cells are always alive.
\item
  \textbf{C} The board is word-addressed.
\end{itemize}
