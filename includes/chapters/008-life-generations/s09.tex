\section{Step 9 Prepare a complete frame
  render}\label{008-life-generations-s09-step-9-prepare-a-complete-frame-render}

Rendering begins at the current board\textquotesingle s first interior
byte and the frame base. The board cursor advances in bytes; the output
cursor advances in four-byte words. White is the constant color for live
cells.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s09-write-or-inspect}

Append the rendering cursor setup.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
render:
    daddiu $t0, $s0, 11
    la $t1, frame
    li $t2, 8
    li $t5, 0x00ffffff
render_row:
    li $t3, 8
render_cell:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{008-life-generations-s09-expected-observation}

The renderer has independent source and frame cursors.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s09-check-your-understanding}

How far should the output cursor move after one cell?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} One byte.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Ten bytes.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Four bytes.
\end{itemize}
