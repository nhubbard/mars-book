\section{Step 11 Finish rendering and publish the
checkpoint}\label{008-life-generations-s11-step-11-finish-rendering-and-publish-the-checkpoint}

Close the row and column loops, skip the board\textquotesingle s halo
columns, then store the working generation number.
\texttt{generation\_ready} marks the boundary after both board and frame
are complete.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s11-write-or-inspect}

Append the loop control and publication fragment.
Remove the starter placeholder: the \texttt{generation\_ready} label
and the following \texttt{nop} instruction.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    addiu $t3, $t3, -1
    bne $t3, $zero, render_cell
    nop
    daddiu $t0, $t0, 2
    addiu $t2, $t2, -1
    bne $t2, $zero, render_row
    nop
    la $t0, generation
    sw $s3, 0($t0)
generation_ready:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s11-expected-observation}

At the first ready checkpoint, generation is zero and the frame shows
the seed.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s11-check-your-understanding}

When is it valid to enter \texttt{generation\_ready}?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} After the complete frame and generation word agree with the
  current board.
\item
  \textbf{B} After computing just the first row.
\item
  \textbf{C} Whenever the destination pointer changes.
\end{itemize}
