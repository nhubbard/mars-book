\section{Step 19 Exchange roles without copying the
  board}\label{008-life-generations-s19-step-19-exchange-roles-without-copying-the-board}

Three moves exchange current and destination base pointers. Increment
the working generation count once. No board bytes move during this
exchange.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s19-write-or-inspect}

Append the pointer swap and count update.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    move $t0, $s0
    move $s0, $s1
    move $s1, $t0
    addiu $s3, $s3, 1
buffers_swapped:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{008-life-generations-s19-expected-observation}

After the first swap, \texttt{board\_b} is current, \texttt{board\_a} is
the next destination, and \texttt{\$s3} is one.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s19-check-your-understanding}

Why is swapping pointers cheaper in memory traffic than copying a board?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} It doubles the physical board size.
  \item
        \textbf{B} It changes two addresses instead of moving one hundred
        bytes.
  \item
        \textbf{C} It changes the Life rule.
\end{itemize}
