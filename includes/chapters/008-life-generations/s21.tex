\section{Step 21 Follow the oscillator through two
steps}\label{008-life-generations-s21-step-21-follow-the-oscillator-through-two-steps}

horizontal again after two. The physical current buffer changes even
when the visible pattern later repeats.

inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s21-write-or-inspect}

observation}\label{008-life-generations-s21-expected-observation}

Generation one has live cells (3,2), (3,3), (3,4); generation two
matches the seed.

understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s21-check-your-understanding}

occurred?
