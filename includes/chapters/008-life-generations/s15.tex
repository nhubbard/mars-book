\section{Step 15 Evaluate birth or survival with Boolean
values}\label{008-life-generations-s15-step-15-evaluate-birth-or-survival-with-boolean-values}

XOR with a constant yields zero exactly on equality. \texttt{sltiu}
value,value,1 converts that zero test into a binary flag. Combine
count==3 with current\_alive AND count==2 to implement the rule.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s15-write-or-inspect}

Append the rule expression.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    xori $t4, $t3, 3
    sltiu $t4, $t4, 1
    xori $t5, $t3, 2
    sltiu $t5, $t5, 1
    lbu $t6, 0($s7)
    and $t5, $t5, $t6
    or $t4, $t4, $t5
rule_decided:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s15-expected-observation}

\texttt{\$t4} is exactly zero or one, the state to write into the
destination.
