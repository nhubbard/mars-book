\section{Step 3 Distinguish the three row
strides}\label{008-life-generations-s03-step-3-distinguish-the-three-row-strides}

The seed has eight bytes per row. A working board includes a dead halo
and has ten. The framebuffer has eight words per row, or thirty-two
bytes. These are different representations of the same logical cells.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s03-write-or-inspect}

Locate the allocations and the stride table.

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s03-expected-observation}

Working interior (0,0) is at offset eleven; framebuffer (0,0) is at
offset zero.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s03-check-your-understanding}

Which stride belongs to a padded working board?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} Eight words.
\item
  \textbf{B} Thirty-two bytes.
\item
  \textbf{C} Ten bytes.
\end{itemize}
