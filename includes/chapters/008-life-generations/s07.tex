\section{Step 7 Close the copy loops and skip halo
  columns}\label{008-life-generations-s07-step-7-close-the-copy-loops-and-skip-halo-columns}

After eight cells, the destination cursor must skip two bytes before the
next interior row: the current row\textquotesingle s right halo and the
next row\textquotesingle s left halo. The seed needs no such skip. Both
retained branches have \texttt{nop} delay slots.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s07-write-or-inspect}

Append the copy-loop control.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    addiu $t3, $t3, -1
    bne $t3, $zero, seed_cell
    nop
    daddiu $t1, $t1, 2
    addiu $t2, $t2, -1
    bne $t2, $zero, seed_row
    nop
seed_copied:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{008-life-generations-s07-expected-observation}

At \texttt{seed\_copied}, all 64 interior bytes equal the seed; halos
remain zero.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s07-check-your-understanding}

Why add two only to the working-board cursor?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} The seed rows also contain two hidden cells.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Only the padded representation has halo columns.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Two is the live-cell color.
\end{itemize}
