\section{Step 14 Count exactly eight source
  neighbors}\label{008-life-generations-s14-step-14-count-exactly-eight-source-neighbors}

The relative byte offsets are \ensuremath{-}11, \ensuremath{-}10, \ensuremath{-}9, \ensuremath{-}1, +1, +9, +10, and +11.
Each \texttt{lbu} reads the stable source, and \texttt{addu} adds its
binary contribution. Offset zero is deliberately absent.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s14-write-or-inspect}

Append the unrolled neighbor sum.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    move $t3, $zero
    lbu $t1, -11($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, -10($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, -9($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, -1($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, 1($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, 9($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, 10($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
    lbu $t1, 11($s7)
    addu $t3, $t3, $t1
neighbors_counted:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{008-life-generations-s14-expected-observation}

At the first default cell, the neighbor count is zero. Every read
remains within the padded allocation.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s14-check-your-understanding}

Why does a corner need no special load branch?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} Its off-board neighbors are represented by zero halo bytes.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Corners always have eight live neighbors.
  \item
        \textbf{C} The simulator ignores invalid memory reads.
\end{itemize}
