\section{Step 1 Read the local update
rule}\label{008-life-generations-s01-step-1-read-the-local-update-rule}

A cell\textquotesingle s next state depends on its current state and the
eight surrounding cells in the same generation. Three neighbors cause
birth or survival; two preserve an already live cell. All other counts
produce zero.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s01-write-or-inspect}

Apply the rule table to one live and one dead center.

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s01-expected-observation}

A dead center with two neighbors stays dead; a live center with two
survives.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s01-check-your-understanding}

Is the center included in its own neighbor count?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} Yes, always.
\item
  \textbf{B} No; only the eight surrounding cells count.
\item
  \textbf{C} Only at the edge.
\end{itemize}
