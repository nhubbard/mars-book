\section{Step 22 Use the public checkpoint as an observation
interface}\label{008-life-generations-s22-step-22-use-the-public-checkpoint-as-an-observation-interface}

The grader pauses at \texttt{generation\_ready} and checks the actual
current and previous boards and frame. It then watches the current
allocation for stores until the next ready boundary. The checkpoint
label is required; private reference labels and registers are not.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s22-write-or-inspect}

Read the public checks and identify their observed state.

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s22-expected-observation}

A temporary source write followed by restoration still violates the
source-stability check.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s22-check-your-understanding}

Why is the ready label part of the assignment contract?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} To require every reference scratch register.
\item
  \textbf{B} To expose complete generations without requiring private
  implementation details.
\item
  \textbf{C} To add a host-time delay.
\end{itemize}
