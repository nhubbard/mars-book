\section{Step 24 Exercise every local binary
configuration}\label{008-life-generations-s24-step-24-exercise-every-local-binary-configuration}

A 3 by 3 neighborhood has nine binary positions including its center,
giving 512 combinations. These exhaust the local rule inputs at one
interior location. Edge configurations need separate treatment because
some positions are forced dead.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s24-write-or-inspect}

Use the local-rule table and coverage fixture to state the tested
domain.

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s24-expected-observation}

Local exhaustive coverage is distinct from all 2\^{}64 possible complete
boards.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s24-check-your-understanding}

What does testing 512 neighborhoods prove directly?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} Every possible long-term Life trajectory.
\item
  \textbf{B} Every possible framebuffer color.
\item
  \textbf{C} The local nine-bit input combinations at the tested
  interior position.
\end{itemize}
