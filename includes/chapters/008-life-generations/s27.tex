\section{Step 27 Measure one verified
workload}\label{008-life-generations-s27-step-27-measure-one-verified-workload}

The default two-step oscillator completes 7,007 simulated instructions
in the unrolled/Boolean reference and 14,197 in the
offset-loop/branching alternative. Counts include initialization, three
renders, two sweeps, control flow, and exit.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s27-write-or-inspect}

Record the fixture and metric when comparing the methods.

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{008-life-generations-s27-expected-observation}

The instruction totals describe this pinned workload, not host timings
or physical CPU cycle counts.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{008-life-generations-s27-check-your-understanding}

What does the lower count establish?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} A universal twofold wall-time speedup.
\item
  \textbf{B} That the two programs perform different Life rules.
\item

  verified workload.
\end{itemize}
