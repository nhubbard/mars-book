\section{Step 12 Test the requested count at the
  boundary}\label{008-life-generations-s12-step-12-test-the-requested-count-at-the-boundary}

If the current generation equals the requested step count, exit through
the supplied done label. Otherwise fall through to compute another
complete destination.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{008-life-generations-s12-write-or-inspect}

Append the boundary exit test.

  observation}\label{008-life-generations-s12-expected-observation}

A request of zero exits after the initial publication, without
evolution.
