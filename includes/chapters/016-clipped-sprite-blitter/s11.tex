\section{Step 11 Recover the clipped source origin}\label{blit-s11}

Subtract the original placement from the destination intersection origin. L minus x tells how many source columns were removed at the left. T minus y tells how many source rows were removed at the top. These offsets are used only after the intersection is known to be nonempty.
