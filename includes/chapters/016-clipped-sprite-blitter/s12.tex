\section{Step 12 Load both full pixel addresses}\label{blit-s12}

Load the eight-byte base fields with \texttt{ld}. Do not use the descriptor address itself as the pixel base. Later calls may choose another descriptor or a base within a different allocation.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{bases} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return section.

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    ld $s0, 0($a0)
    ld $s1, 0($a1)
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
\texttt{s0} and \texttt{s1} hold the selected destination and source pixel addresses.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
What does \texttt{ld} from offset zero of \texttt{a0} load?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} The framebuffer pixel pointer.
\item \textbf{B} The first pixel color.
\item \textbf{C} The descriptor width.
\end{itemize}
