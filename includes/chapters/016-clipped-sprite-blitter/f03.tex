      \item The image has sixteen physical columns and eight rows. Logical framebuffer cell $(0,0)$ is physical column 1, row 0, after the leading guard; each logical row has eight active cells and a sixteen-word stride. The dashed white outline marks the eight-by-six logical framebuffer. Gray cells include padding and the guard. The display omits the final padding word and trailing guard, which are checked separately in memory. White is at logical $(7,0)$, physical column 8, row 0; red is at logical $(6,1)$, physical column 7, row 1. The other two visible cells retain the background because their source words are transparent. No destination cell exists at logical column 8.

White at $(7,0)$ is the next word, \texttt{0x10010020}, just beyond this detail; its position is visible in the Bitmap view. Every logical row begins 64 bytes after the preceding row, regardless of this two-column clipped width. The trace separately checks all padding, unseen guards and omitted source cells.
