\section{Step 8 Clamp the right edge}\label{blit-s08}

Form x plus source width with \texttt{daddu}, then compare the resulting signed edge with destination width. Retain the smaller edge. Although widths fit in a word, the coordinate sum need not; a trapping word addition here can raise an exception for a valid off-screen call. Full-width addition also keeps the computed edge equal to the stated geometric formula.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{right} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return section.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    daddu $s7, $a2, $t2
    slt $t4, $t0, $s7
    beq $t4, $zero, clip_right_ready
    nop
    move $s7, $t0
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
For x minus two and source width five, \texttt{s7} becomes three.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why use full-width addition for this edge?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} The valid signed coordinate can be near the word limit.
  \item \textbf{B} Every pixel has eight bytes.
  \item \textbf{C} The stride is signed.
\end{itemize}
