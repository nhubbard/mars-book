\section{Step 13 Convert each stride to bytes}\label{blit-s13}

Load each word stride and shift it left by two. One pixel word occupies four bytes. The supported stride range makes this multiplication safe in word arithmetic. Keep the two results separate; neither depends on the clipped copy width.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{strides} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return section.

\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    lw $s2, 16($a0)
    lw $s3, 16($a1)
    sll $s2, $s2, 2
    sll $s3, $s3, 2
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
The default byte strides in \texttt{s2} and \texttt{s3} are sixty-four and twenty-eight.
