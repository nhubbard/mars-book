\section{Step 28 Look for the next term operator}\label{expression-s28}

Skip whitespace, stop at the input bound, and inspect the next byte. Recognize only multiplication or division at this level. A different byte returns the accumulated value without consuming that byte. This is how the grammar transfers responsibility between rules rather than requiring every helper to understand every operator.

\Needspace{21\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary return in the \texttt{term\_probe} region. Preserve its surrounding labels and frame scaffolding.
