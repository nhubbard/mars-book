\section{Step 34 Look for the next expression operator}\label{expression-s34}

Skip whitespace, stop at the input bound, and inspect the next byte. Recognize only addition or subtraction at this level. A different byte returns the accumulated value without consuming that byte. This is how the grammar transfers responsibility between rules rather than requiring every helper to understand every operator.

\Needspace{21\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary return in the \texttt{expr\_probe} region. Preserve its surrounding labels and frame scaffolding.

\Needspace{15\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    jal skip_space
    nop
    beq $s1, $s2, expr_value
    nop
    daddu $t0, $s0, $s1
    lbu $t0, 0($t0)
    li $t1, 43
    beq $t0, $t1, expr_operator
    nop
    li $t1, 45
    bne $t0, $t1, expr_value
    nop
\end{minted}
\subsection{Expected observation}
For 1+2*3, the operator probe finds decimal 43 at cursor one.

What should an inner expression do when it encounters its closing parenthesis?

\begin{itemize}

  \item \textbf{B} Leave it for the surrounding caller.
