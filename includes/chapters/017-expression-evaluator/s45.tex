\section{Step 45 Write another implementation under the same contract}\label{expression-s45}

In free-writing mode, replace the helper design while keeping eval\_expr and the caller/data harness. An operator-stack evaluator or a precedence-climbing implementation is acceptable. Reproduce intermediate range checks and diagnostic ordering, not only successful numerical values. In guided mode, retain the named regions for the stated intermediate inspections.

\subsection{Expected observation}
Final grading accepts equivalent behavior without requiring internal labels, scratch registers, traversal order or exact instruction counts.
