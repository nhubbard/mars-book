\section{Step 2 Assign operators to grammar levels}\label{expression-s02}

Compare 1+2*3 with (1+2)*3 and compare 1-2-3 with 1-(2-3). Precedence determines which operand a call must obtain; association determines how repetitions at one level combine. The guided functions encode these two choices separately.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The first pair yields seven and nine; the second yields minus four and two.
