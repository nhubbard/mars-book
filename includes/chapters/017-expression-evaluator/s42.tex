\section{Step 42 Check division and signed range boundaries}\label{expression-s42}

Compare -7/3, 7/-3, 1/(2-2), and (-2147483647-1)/-1. Distinguish truncation toward zero from flooring and distinguish a zero denominator from an out-of-range nonzero-denominator quotient. The helper checks the divisor before the instruction and range after the quotient.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The first two values are minus two. Zero division reports minus three; minimum divided by minus one reports overflow.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
What status belongs to minimum signed word divided by minus one?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} Division by zero.
  \item \textbf{B} Overflow.
  \item \textbf{C} Success with a wrapped minimum.
\end{itemize}
