\section{Step 41 Separate literal overflow from unary overflow}\label{expression-s41}

Test -2147483648, (-2147483647-1), and -(-2147483647-1). The first fails while reading a positive digit token. The second constructs the minimum through valid operations. The third then attempts an unrepresentable negation of that valid value.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The literal error is at ten; the minimum construction succeeds; the outer negation error is at zero.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
May unary minus rescue an already overflowing decimal token?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} No.
\item \textbf{B} Yes, for every ten-digit token.
\item \textbf{C} Only when a later multiplication is zero.
\end{itemize}
