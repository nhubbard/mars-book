\section{Step 7 Skip a run without reading past its end}\label{expression-s07}

Increment the cursor once and branch back to the bound check. A whitespace byte at the final position therefore advances to the length and returns before another load. Keep the update inside this loop; branching back without it would repeatedly classify the same byte.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write or inspect}
Replace the temporary return in the \texttt{space\_advance} region. Preserve its surrounding labels and frame scaffolding.
