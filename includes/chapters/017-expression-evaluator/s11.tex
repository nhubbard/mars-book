\section{Step 11 Preserve operand order in subtraction}\label{expression-s11}

Use the left value minus the right value. Subtraction is not commutative: reversing \texttt{a0} and \texttt{a1} changes both normal results and some boundary failures. As with addition, the exact candidate fits in a doubleword for two signed-word operands.
