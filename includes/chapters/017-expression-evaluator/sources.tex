\section*{Related reading}
Robert Nystrom's \emph{Crafting Interpreters}, chapter 6, discusses precedence and
recursive-descent parsing. Theodore Norvell's \emph{Parsing Expressions by Recursive
  Descent} develops precedence climbing as another expression-parsing method.
These are background reading for the parser designs; this chapter's byte bounds,
integer range and diagnostic-order rules are defined by the project contract.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \href{https://github.com/munificent/craftinginterpreters/blob/master/book/parsing-expressions.md}{Nystrom: Parsing expressions (author\textquotesingle s source)}

\end{itemize}
