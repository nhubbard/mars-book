\section{Step 7 Prepare a byte
value}\label{002-single-pixel-s07-step-7-prepare-a-byte-value}

A byte store uses the low eight bits of its source register. Loading 255
supplies the pattern 0xff in those bits. The destination address will
select which byte of the packed word changes.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{002-single-pixel-s07-write-or-inspect}

Append the byte-value load after base\_ready.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    li $t2, 255
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{002-single-pixel-s07-expected-observation}

The low byte of \$t2 is ff. The pixel has not changed merely because
this register was loaded.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{002-single-pixel-s07-check-your-understanding}

How many bits will sb take from the source register?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} All 64 bits.
\item
  \textbf{B} Exactly 24 bits because the display uses RGB.
\item
  \textbf{C} Eight low bits.
\end{itemize}
