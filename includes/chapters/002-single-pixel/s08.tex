\section{Step 8 Replace the lowest-addressed
byte}\label{002-single-pixel-s08-step-8-replace-the-lowest-addressed-byte}

sb writes one byte to the specified address. At offset zero in
little-endian memory, that byte is the least significant byte of the
word, which this format uses for blue. The other three bytes retain
their prior values.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{002-single-pixel-s08-write-or-inspect}

Append the byte store and final checkpoint label.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
modify:
    sb $t2, 0($t0)
final_ready:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{002-single-pixel-s08-expected-observation}

The final bytes are ff, 66, 33, and 00. The word is 0x003366ff, so red
and green remain 51 and 102 while blue becomes 255.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{002-single-pixel-s08-check-your-understanding}

What is the final packed word?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} 0x000000ff because sb clears the rest of the word.
\item
  \textbf{B} 0x003366ff.
\item
  \textbf{C} 0xff336699 because offset zero always means the highest
  byte.
\end{itemize}
