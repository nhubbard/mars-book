\section{Step 5 Store the complete
  word}\label{002-single-pixel-s05-step-5-store-the-complete-word}

sw writes the low 32 bits of a register to memory. The operand 0(\$t0)
adds a byte displacement of zero to the address in \$t0. The display
observes the completed write and interprets the word as an RGB value.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{002-single-pixel-s05-write-or-inspect}

Append the store and the checkpoint label. The label marks the following
instruction location and performs no work itself.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    sw $t1, 0($t0)
base_ready:
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{002-single-pixel-s05-expected-observation}

The word at pixel is 0x00336699. In the configured grid, the upper-left
cell should show that color. The neighboring word is unchanged.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{002-single-pixel-s05-check-your-understanding}

When is the register color transferred into memory?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} When the assembler reads the label base\_ready.
  \item
        \textbf{B} When sw executes.
  \item
        \textbf{C} When li loads the constant.
\end{itemize}
