  understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s26-check-your-understanding}

Why is subtract-and-test not universally interchangeable with
\texttt{slt}?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} It always treats zero as negative.
  \item
        \textbf{B} The subtraction can overflow for unrestricted signed
        values.
  \item
        \textbf{C} \texttt{slt} writes to memory.
\end{itemize}
