\section{Step 17 Exchange a selected minimum
  once}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s17-step-17-exchange-a-selected-minimum-once}

If the minimum is already at the suffix start, skip a self-exchange.
Otherwise load and exchange the two selected words, increment swap
metadata, and render the complete new array.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s17-write-or-inspect}

Insert this fragment in selection\_exchange.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    beq $s4, $s2, selection_next
    nop
    sll $t0, $s2, 2
    daddu $t0, $s0, $t0
    lw $t1, 0($t0)
    sll $t2, $s4, 2
    daddu $t2, $s0, $t2
    lw $t3, 0($t2)
    sw $t3, 0($t0)
    sw $t1, 0($t2)
selection_swapped:
    addiu $s6, $s6, 1
    la $t0, swap_count
    sw $s6, 0($t0)
    li $t0, 1
    la $t1, phase
    sw $t0, 0($t1)
    move $a0, $s0
    move $a1, $s1
    jal render
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s17-expected-observation}

The first selection exchange gives {[}\ensuremath{-}4,\ensuremath{-}2,3,2,0,1,\ensuremath{-}3,\ensuremath{-}1{]}.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s17-check-your-understanding}

Why not publish a self-exchange?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} It changes the multiset.
  \item
        \textbf{B} The contract publishes only actual index-changing
        exchanges.
  \item
        \textbf{C} The frame cannot show equal values.
\end{itemize}
