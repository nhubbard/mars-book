\section{Step 9 Dispatch to the selected
  algorithm}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s09-step-9-dispatch-to-the-selected-algorithm}

Zero chooses insertion sort; one takes the selection path. The algorithm
selector is input, so read it but do not overwrite it to simplify a
branch.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s09-write-or-inspect}

Insert this fragment in dispatch.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    beq $s7, $zero, insertion_sort
    nop
    beq $zero, $zero, selection_sort
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s09-expected-observation}

Each valid selector enters its own loop setup.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s09-check-your-understanding}

Why use separate algorithms under one visualization contract?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} They must have equal instruction counts.
  \item
        \textbf{B} They require the same private registers.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Their observed states can be compared under the same frame
        and preservation rules.
\end{itemize}
