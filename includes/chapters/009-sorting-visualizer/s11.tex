\section{Step 11 Compare an adjacent pair
  safely}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s11-step-11-compare-an-adjacent-pair-safely}

Stop when the inner index reaches zero. Otherwise load the left and
right words and use signed \texttt{slt} to ask whether right is strictly
smaller. Equality stops the movement.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s11-write-or-inspect}

Append this fragment in insertion\_inner.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    beq $s3, $zero, insertion_next
    nop
    sll $t0, $s3, 2
    daddu $t0, $s0, $t0
    lw $t1, -4($t0)
    lw $t2, 0($t0)
    slt $t3, $t2, $t1
    beq $t3, $zero, insertion_next
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s11-expected-observation}

For the default first pair, \ensuremath{-}2 is smaller than 2, so an exchange is
required.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s11-check-your-understanding}

Why is the comparison strict?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} Equal items must always exchange.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Equal keys need not cross each other in this insertion
        variant.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Strict comparison ignores negative values.
\end{itemize}
