\section{A leaf call and its
  continuation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-x-call-a-leaf-call-and-its-continuation}

The actual sequence is: caller prepares \texttt{a0}/\texttt{a1};
\texttt{jal} writes \texttt{ra}=P+8; the delay-slot \texttt{nop}
executes; render uses temporary registers; \texttt{sort\_ready} exposes
the frame; \texttt{jr} \texttt{ra} and its \texttt{nop} complete the
return; main continues at P+8. Register preservation comes from the
function body and contract, not automatic restoration by a jump.
