\section{Step 7 Close the renderer
loops}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s07-step-7-close-the-renderer-loops}

Advance through eight columns and eight rows. The output cursor moves
four bytes per cell. The final fall-through reaches \texttt{sort\_ready}
only after every frame word is complete.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s07-write-or-inspect}

Append this fragment inside render.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    addiu $t2, $t2, 1
    slti $t4, $t2, 8
    bne $t4, $zero, render_cell
    nop
    addiu $t1, $t1, 1
    slti $t4, $t1, 8
    bne $t4, $zero, render_row
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s07-expected-observation}

The initial array now has a complete 8 by 8 frame, including black
cells.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s07-check-your-understanding}

Why store black on every render?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} A bar may shrink or a column may become inactive.
\item
  \textbf{B} Black writes change the array values.
\item
  \textbf{C} Only black counts as a word store.
\end{itemize}
