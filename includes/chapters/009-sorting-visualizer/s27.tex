\section{Step 27 State invariants and extension
  contracts}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s27-step-27-state-invariants-and-extension-contracts}

Insertion preserves a sorted prefix and moves the new item left through
inversions. Selection fixes the next smallest item after a suffix scan.
Every publication preserves the active multiset and frame
correspondence.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s27-write-or-inspect}

Write both invariants and propose a separately specified shift-based or
tagged-record extension.

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s27-expected-observation}

Changed data width, record identity, publication policy, or rendering
scale needs corresponding new checks.

  understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s27-check-your-understanding}
