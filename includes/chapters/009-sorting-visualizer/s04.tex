\section{Step 4 Initialize inputs and publish the initial
  call}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s04-step-4-initialize-inputs-and-publish-the-initial-call}

Load the array base, length, and algorithm selector into persistent
working registers. Initialize the output metadata, pass \texttt{a0} and
\texttt{a1}, and call render. \texttt{jal} saves the return location;
its \texttt{nop} delay slot still executes.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s04-write-or-inspect}

Insert this fragment in setup.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    la $s0, values
    la $t0, count
    lw $s1, 0($t0)
    la $t0, algorithm
    lw $s7, 0($t0)
    move $s6, $zero
    la $t0, swap_count
    sw $zero, 0($t0)
    la $t0, phase
    sw $zero, 0($t0)
    move $a0, $s0
    move $a1, $s1
    jal render
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s04-expected-observation}

The renderer stub returns to main, but no correct image exists until its
body is filled.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s04-check-your-understanding}

Why keep sorting state in saved registers across the call?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} The renderer contract preserves them while temporary
        registers are scratch.
  \item
        \textbf{B} All registers automatically restore after \texttt{jal}.
  \item
        \textbf{C} The simulator forbids temporary registers.
\end{itemize}
