\section{Step 15 Initialize a selection suffix
  scan}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s15-step-15-initialize-a-selection-suffix-scan}

The sorted prefix starts empty. If fewer than two positions remain,
finish. Otherwise begin with the suffix\textquotesingle s first index as
the minimum and scan from the next index.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s15-write-or-inspect}

Insert this fragment in selection\_init.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    move $s2, $zero
selection_outer:
    addiu $t0, $s1, -1
    slt $t0, $s2, $t0
    beq $t0, $zero, sort_end
    nop
    move $s4, $s2
    addiu $s3, $s2, 1
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s15-expected-observation}

For the default input, the initial candidate minimum is value two at
index zero.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s15-check-your-understanding}

Why can selection sort stop before scanning a final single item?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} It is always zero.
  \item
        \textbf{B} Its address is invalid.
  \item
        \textbf{C} Only one remaining item is already in its final suffix
        position.
\end{itemize}
