\section{Step 3 Read the regions and public
  labels}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s03-step-3-read-the-regions-and-public-labels}

The starter separates main control flow from a renderer located after
the exit. Empty regions can be filled incrementally. A label names a
location; a BEGIN comment only marks an editing site.

\subsection{Write or
  inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s03-write-or-inspect}

Locate setup, the renderer region, and done.

\subsection{Expected
  observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s03-expected-observation}

render, \texttt{sort\_ready}, and done are public interfaces; most loop
labels are guide scaffolding.

\subsection{Check your
  understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s03-check-your-understanding}

What prevents main from falling into the renderer after completion?

\begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
        \textbf{A} Comments stop execution.
  \item
        \textbf{B} The renderer is not executable.
  \item
        \textbf{C} The supplied normal exit executes first.
\end{itemize}
