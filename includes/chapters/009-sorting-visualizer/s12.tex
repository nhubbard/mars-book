\section{Step 12 Exchange both words before
publication}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s12-step-12-exchange-both-words-before-publication}

Keep both loaded values in temporaries until their opposite stores are
complete. Decrement the inner index, update swap metadata, call the
renderer, and return to the adjacent comparison.

\subsection{Write or
inspect}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s12-write-or-inspect}

Append this fragment in insertion\_inner.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    sw $t2, -4($t0)
    sw $t1, 0($t0)
insertion_swapped:
    addiu $s3, $s3, -1
    addiu $s6, $s6, 1
    la $t0, swap_count
    sw $s6, 0($t0)
    li $t0, 1
    la $t1, phase
    sw $t0, 0($t1)
    move $a0, $s0
    move $a1, $s1
    jal render
    nop
    beq $zero, $zero, insertion_inner
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected
observation}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s12-expected-observation}

The first snapshot after an exchange is {[}\ensuremath{-}2,2,3,\ensuremath{-}4,0,1,\ensuremath{-}3,\ensuremath{-}1{]}, with
\texttt{swap\_count} one.

\subsection{Check your
understanding}\label{009-sorting-visualizer-s12-check-your-understanding}

Why are both values loaded before either is overwritten?

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{A} To preserve the frame base.
\item
  \textbf{B} To avoid every branch.
\item
  \textbf{C} The second old value would otherwise be lost.
\end{itemize}
