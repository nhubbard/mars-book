\section{Step 9 Assemble B without changing A}\label{pcm-s09}

Shift B’s high byte in \texttt{t2} and combine it with its low byte in \texttt{t1}. The same byte-order rule applies to both inputs. Registers have working roles rather than permanent types; \texttt{t1} now contains a numerical halfword instead of one byte. A remains in \texttt{t0} and both pointers still refer to this sample.
