exercise's explicit contract. Native arithmetic and mask operations are checked
against pinned MARS \texttt{MsaIntegerInstructions.kt},
\texttt{MsaRegisterInstructions.kt}, \texttt{InstrSeh.kt} and the vector integer
tests. The independent mixing oracle uses signed integer sums and explicit bounds.

describes signed sixteen-bit PCM samples. Its
\href{https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/mmreg/ns-mmreg-waveformatex}{WAVEFORMATEX reference}
describes sample rate, channels and block alignment. The listening files use a
two-byte mono frame and record the little-endian sample interpretation explicitly.
