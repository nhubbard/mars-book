\section{Step 13 Keep in-range sums out of the clipping path}\label{pcm-s13}

Test whether the exact sum is less than -32768. On success branch to \texttt{mix\_low}. Otherwise transfer directly to \texttt{mix\_store}, bypassing both endpoint replacement and count increment. This second test uses the opposite comparison order from the positive bound. A negative input does not itself imply clipping; only the exact sum matters.

\subsection{Write and inspect}
