\section{Step 25 Mark lanes whose two sums agree}\label{pcm-s25}

Compare \texttt{w2} and \texttt{w3} with \texttt{ceq.h}. Equal halfwords become all one bits, hexadecimal ffff; unequal halfwords become zero. A representable sum is unchanged by either operation. An overflowing signed sum wraps to the opposite sign and cannot equal its saturated endpoint, so equality is a complete test in this two-input domain.
