\section{Step 18 Establish a valid modular-addition entry}\label{rsa-s18}

Combine all four modulus limbs to test for zero. Do not test only the low word: a positive modulus can have that word zero. Once the modulus is known nonzero, initialize the result offset and carry. The caller remains responsible for supplying two residues below that modulus.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{add\_guard}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    lwu $t0, 0($a2)
    lwu $t1, 4($a2)
    lwu $t2, 8($a2)
    lwu $t3, 12($a2)
    or $t0, $t0, $t1
    or $t0, $t0, $t2
    or $t0, $t0, $t3
    beqz $t0, rsa_invalid
    nop
    move $t0, $zero
    move $t1, $zero
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s18-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{addmod\_sum} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
A positive multiword modulus reaches the sum boundary; zero returns status one without output access.

\subsection{Check your understanding}
Which test recognizes the all-zero modulus?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} OR all four limbs and test the combined value.
\item \textbf{B} Test the lowest limb alone.
\item \textbf{C} Interpret the output pointer as the modulus.
