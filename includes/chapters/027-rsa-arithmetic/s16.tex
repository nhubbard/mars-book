\section{Step 16 Close the full-product interface}\label{rsa-s16}

The accumulated bytes already hold the full result. Finish with a status-zero return. There is no implicit remainder at this boundary: all 256 product bits belong to the caller. Keep this routine useful as a separate arithmetic check even though the later modular multiplier uses another strategy.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{product\_publish}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.
