\section{Step 3 Follow the decreasing remainder}\label{rsa-s03}

Guard the second value before dividing. Save the remainder in a third register, then move the old divisor into the first position and the remainder into the second. The branch repeats this replacement until the divisor is zero. A zero divisor at that boundary terminates the algorithm.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{gcd\_remainders}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

\Needspace{13\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
gcd_loop:
    beqz $t1, gcd_done
    nop
    dmodu $t2, $t0, $t1
    move $t0, $t1
    move $t1, $t2
    b gcd_loop
    nop
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s03-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{gcd\_done} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The pair reaches 1 and 0 after the divisions shown earlier.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why compute the remainder before overwriting the pair?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} Both old values are still needed by the division.
  \item \textbf{B} The result must be written to a string.
  \item \textbf{C} Moving a register changes memory byte order.
\end{itemize}
