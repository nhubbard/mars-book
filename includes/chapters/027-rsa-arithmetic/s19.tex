\section{Step 19 Keep the final sum carry}\label{rsa-s19}

Walk from low limb to high limb. Add two zero-extended inputs and the incoming carry in a doubleword register. Load both input limbs before storing, which permits the exact in-place aliases in the contract. Preserve the final carry after the fourth store; it represents bit 128 of the mathematical sum.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{add\_limbs}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

\Needspace{19\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
addmod_sum:
    daddu $t2, $a0, $t0
    lwu $t3, 0($t2)
    daddu $t2, $a1, $t0
    lwu $t4, 0($t2)
    daddu $t3, $t3, $t4
    daddu $t3, $t3, $t1
    daddu $t2, $a3, $t0
    sw $t3, 0($t2)
    dsrl $t1, $t3, 32
    daddiu $t0, $t0, 4
    sltiu $t2, $t0, 16
    bnez $t2, addmod_sum
    nop
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s19-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{after\_add\_limbs} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
For the near-maximum modulus example, the stored low sum is \(2^{128}\) minus 320 and carry is one.

What does the final carry represent?
