\section{Step 13 Split one widened partial sum}\label{rsa-s13}

Form a 64-bit product, add the existing output limb and the previous carry, then split the temporary at bit 32. Store its low word before using a logical shift to recover the high carry. The earlier bound explains why this entire sum fits unsigned 64-bit arithmetic.

Replace only the body of region \texttt{product\_partial}, retaining its BEGIN and END comments.

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
mul_inner:
    daddu $t4, $a1, $t2
    lwu $t4, 0($t4)
    dmulu $t4, $t4, $t1
    daddu $t5, $t0, $t2
    daddu $t5, $a2, $t5
    lwu $t6, 0($t5)
    daddu $t4, $t4, $t6
    daddu $t4, $t4, $t3
    sw $t4, 0($t5)
    dsrl $t3, $t4, 32
\end{minted}

Use recipe \texttt{s13-1} with the cumulative source through this edit. Stop at \texttt{after\_product\_partial} before continuing to the temporary or normal return.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The first low limb becomes 1 and its outgoing carry is hexadecimal fffffffe.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Which temporary must retain the whole partial sum?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} The four-byte output slot after its store.
  \item \textbf{B} The 64-bit accumulator before splitting.
  \item \textbf{C} Only the byte pointer.
