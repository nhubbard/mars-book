\section{Step 10 Exercise a product with its top bit set}\label{rsa-s10}

Use base fffffffe, exponent two and modulus ffffffff in the scalar routine. The widened square has bit 63 set. Compare the unsigned remainder with the signed interpretation worked earlier, then inspect the four output bytes. This case distinguishes arithmetic interpretations that small toy values cannot.

\subsection{Expected observation}
The scalar output is one; a signed-remainder substitution fails this case.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Which interpretation does the scalar product require?

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A} An address into the output object.
  \item \textbf{B} Unsigned 64-bit magnitude.
  \item \textbf{C} Signed 32-bit value.
\end{itemize}
