\section{Step 6 Load the oldest sample in this window}\label{fir-s06}

Use two unsigned byte loads from \texttt{t1}. The first tap sees the oldest history sample, not the first current sample at window position seven. Arbitrary byte alignment makes byte loads a direct way to respect the two-byte sample boundary. \texttt{t4} and \texttt{t5} still hold separate byte values; their combination and sign interpretation follow in separate edits.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
