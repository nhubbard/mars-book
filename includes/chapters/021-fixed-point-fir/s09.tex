\section{Step 9 Load the matching Q15 coefficient}\label{fir-s09}

Read two bytes from \texttt{t2} into \texttt{t5} and \texttt{t6}. Reusing \texttt{t5} is safe because the complete signed sample is retained in \texttt{t4}. This first coefficient multiplies the oldest sample. Its stored code supplies a weight only after the binary scale is applied to the complete dot product; the two loaded bytes are not separate weights.

\subsection{Check your understanding}
Why may \texttt{t5} be reused here?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} All coefficients are positive.
\item \textbf{B} Loading a coefficient stores the sample automatically.
\item \textbf{C} The signed sample no longer depends on its former high-byte temporary.
\end{itemize}
