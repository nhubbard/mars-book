\section{Step 29 Widen the first four coefficient lanes}\label{fir-s29}

Interleave \texttt{w1}’s low halfwords with their corresponding mask halfwords into \texttt{w3}. Operand order makes each original code the low half of a word and its mask the high half. In little-endian interpretation this sign-extends the value. Zero extension would change a negative coefficient into a positive number before multiplication.
