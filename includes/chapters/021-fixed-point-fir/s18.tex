\section{Step 18 Keep rounded in-range values on the store path}\label{fir-s18}

Test whether the rounded result is smaller than -32768. Branch to \texttt{scalar\_low} when it is; otherwise transfer directly to \texttt{scalar\_store}. This bypasses both endpoint replacement and the clipping increment. An accumulator representing -32768.5 rounds to -32768 under this contract, so its clipping decision must use that rounded value.

\Needspace{12\baselineskip}
\subsection{Write and inspect}
Replace the temporary branch and nop in \texttt{lower\_bound}. Keep surrounding labels and region markers.

Rounded -262136 takes the low path. Rounded -32768 and zero go to storage without increasing the count.
