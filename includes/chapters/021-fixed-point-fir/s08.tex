\section{Step 8 Extend the sample before multiplication}\label{fir-s08}

Use \texttt{seh} so the halfword sign bit determines the complete signed register value. The pattern fc18 must become -1000 rather than positive 64536. A correct coefficient cannot repair an unsigned interpretation of the sample. This representation step precedes every product, including products in later windows.
