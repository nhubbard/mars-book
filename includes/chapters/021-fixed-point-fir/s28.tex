\section{Step 28 Build the coefficient sign-extension mask}\label{fir-s28}

Compare each signed halfword in \texttt{w1} with zero. \texttt{clti\_s.h} produces ffff where a coefficient is negative and zero otherwise. These bits will supply the high half of each word. A sign mask is a representation tool here; it does not count negative coefficients or change their scale.
