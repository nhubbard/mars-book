\section{Step 22 Admit only a well-formed optional palette}\label{png-collect-s22}
Implement \texttt{collect\_plte}. Reject a second palette or any palette after the IDAT sequence has begun. Require a nonempty payload of at most 768 bytes whose length is divisible by three. Then set the palette-seen bit and continue through \texttt{collect\_plte\_ready}.

In this RGB subset the palette does not replace the samples that will be decoded. It is nevertheless a recognized critical chunk with ordering and length rules. Use the palette fixture, which inserts a three-byte PLTE after IHDR. Its flag set becomes three: header seen and palette seen. An empty palette must fail rather than being treated as a harmless unknown chunk.

\paragraph{Observe} Use \texttt{s22-1}; stop before \texttt{collect\_plte\_ready}. Inspect the state described above, then continue to the next unfinished boundary.

\item[B.] Three complete scanlines.
\item[C.] A native doubleword.
\end{itemize}
