\section{Step 63 Place the three-bit lengths by their permutation}\label{png-huff-s63}
Implement \texttt{huff\_read\_alphabet}. For each advertised entry, read three bits, obtain the corresponding symbol from the supplied order table and store its length at that symbol's byte offset. Retain and advance the incoming position across helper calls. Stop after the advertised count.

The order begins 16, 17, 18, 0; it does not begin with symbol zero. The fixture gives these symbols lengths 4, 5, 5 and 1. Later transmitted positions give symbol two length three and symbol one length two. All other entries remain zero, including the untransmitted symbol fifteen.

\paragraph{Observe} Use \texttt{s63-1}; stop before \texttt{huff\_alphabet\_ready}. Inspect the state described above before continuing.
