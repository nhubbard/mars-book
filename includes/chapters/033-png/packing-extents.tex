\section{Separate active bytes from the writable extent}
The worked image has ten active pixels. With stride seventeen, its output intervals
are the following inclusive byte ranges relative to the framebuffer argument:
\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\begin{longtable}{p{20mm}p{38mm}p{38mm}p{45mm}}
  \toprule Row & First active byte & Last active byte & Gap before next active row \\\midrule
  0            & 0                 & 7                & 60 bytes                   \\
  1            & 68                & 75               & 60 bytes                   \\
  2            & 136               & 143              & 60 bytes                   \\
  3            & 204               & 211              & 60 bytes                   \\
  4            & 272               & 279              & No trailing gap required   \\\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
The returned active count is forty bytes, while the minimum writable extent is
280. A capacity of 279 must fail before the first store; a capacity of 280 admits
exactly the needed last word. Padding lies inside that extent but remains owned by
the caller. With the file caller’s stride 512, the required extent becomes 8200,
while the active count remains forty. Neither extent is an instruction to clear
all intervening framebuffer bytes.
