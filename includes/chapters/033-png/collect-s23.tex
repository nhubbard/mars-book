\section{Step 23 Distinguish skippable metadata from excluded features}\label{png-collect-s23}
Fill \texttt{collect\_ancillary}. This exercise rejects tRNS and the APNG chunks acTL, fcTL and fdAT rather than silently discarding features it cannot display. Reject any other unrecognized critical type, identified by a clear bit five in its first type letter. Other ancillary chunks may be skipped after their bounds, letters and CRC have passed.

If the IDAT-seen bit is already set, accepting a non-IDAT ancillary chunk also sets the sequence-ended bit. A later IDAT must then fail. Use the metadata fixture to observe tEXt between IHDR and IDAT: the header flag remains one because the sequence has not started yet.

\paragraph{Observe} Use \texttt{s23-1}; stop before \texttt{collect\_ancillary\_ready}. Inspect the state described above, then continue to the next unfinished boundary.

\paragraph{Check} What does an accepted non-IDAT chunk after IDAT establish?
\begin{itemize}
  \item[A.] A later IDAT may restart the same consecutive group.
  \item[B.] The IDAT group has ended.
  \item[C.] Its payload must be decoded as pixels.
\end{itemize}
