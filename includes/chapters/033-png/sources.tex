\section{Sources and draft status}
The format reference is the
\href{https://www.w3.org/TR/2025/REC-png-3-20250624/}{PNG Third Edition recommendation
of 24 June 2025}. The compression references are
\href{https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1950.html}{RFC 1950 (zlib)} and
\href{https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1951.html}{RFC 1951 (DEFLATE)}.
The bounded RGB subset, separate-buffer development order and failure-publication
requirements are exercise choices. The existing MARS PNG smoke program supplies a
scalar and MSA comparison; its current address assumptions and acceptance behavior
must be audited when adapting it to these contracts.

This is the chapter's initial reading foundation. Its fixed observations are
host-verified, including independent image decoding. New MIPS entries, cumulative
construction steps, executable public and private feedback, instruction measurements
and separate chapter distributions remain to be implemented and checked. No PNG IDE
screenshots have been captured for this chapter. Capture recipes and native annotations
will accompany actual light-theme IDE observations later. This foundation is not yet
a registered exercise package or part of the integrated manuscript.
