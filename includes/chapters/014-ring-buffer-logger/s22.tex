\section{Step 22 Publish the successful pop}\label{ring-s22}

Form encoded size as decoded length plus one, subtract it from used, and store the completed head and used. Return payload length in \texttt{v0} and encoded size in \texttt{v1}. Tail, capacity, pointer, and ring bytes remain unchanged.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{pop\_publish} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stubs.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    addiu $v1, $t5, 1
    subu $t2, $t2, $v1
    sw $t0, 0($a0)
    sw $t2, 8($a0)
    move $v0, $t5
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
Popping the first three-byte record changes 0/7/7 to head four, tail seven, used three.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
How much does used decrease?

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{B} L plus one.
\item \textbf{C} The destination capacity.
\end{itemize}
