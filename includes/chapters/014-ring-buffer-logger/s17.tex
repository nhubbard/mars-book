\section{Step 17 Step beyond the stored prefix}\label{ring-s17}

Increment head and wrap it at capacity before accessing payload. This advance accounts for the prefix itself. For a zero-length record it will also be the completed new head after publication.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{pop\_advance} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stubs.

\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    addiu $t0, $t0, 1
    bne $t0, $t3, pop_payload
    nop
    move $t0, $zero
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
A prefix at the last ring byte places its first possible payload byte at index zero.
