\section{Step 9 Copy one payload byte}\label{ring-s09}

Load one unsigned byte through the source cursor, form the current ring address, and store the byte. Advance the source cursor with full-width arithmetic. The byte values are copied exactly; no text interpretation or checksum is applied.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{push\_bytes} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stubs.

\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    lbu $t8, 0($t6)
    daddu $t9, $t4, $t1
    sb $t8, 0($t9)
    daddiu $t6, $t6, 1
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
The high-bit source bytes 80 and ff retain their stored eight-bit patterns.

\Needspace{11\baselineskip}
\subsection{Check your understanding}
Does a zero payload byte stop the copy?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Yes, as in a string.
\item \textbf{B} Only at the physical ring end.
\item \textbf{C} No; the supplied length controls the loop.
\end{itemize}
