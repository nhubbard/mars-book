\section{Step 18 Prepare the destination copy}\label{ring-s18}

Copy the destination pointer to a full-width cursor and the decoded length to the remaining count. Skip the loop when the length is zero. A zero-capacity destination is sufficient for an empty record and must receive no writes.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{pop\_init} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stubs.

\Needspace{7\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    move $t6, $a1
    move $t7, $t5
    beq $t7, $zero, pop_commit
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
An empty record succeeds with status zero and eventually consumes one queue byte.
