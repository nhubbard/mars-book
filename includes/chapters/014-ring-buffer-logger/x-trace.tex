\section{Work the supplied command sequence}\label{ring-x-trace}
Main uses capacity eight and a table of fourteen commands. The first three payloads
are \texttt{41 42 43}, \texttt{78 79}, and \texttt{7f 80 ff}, written here in
hexadecimal. The later seven-byte payload is \texttt{00 01 02 7f 80 fe ff}.
Each completed call publishes its status and auxiliary result before the
\texttt{operation\_ready} marker. The following table uses decimal counts and indices.

\Needspace{22\baselineskip}
\begin{longtable}{rlrrrrr}
  \toprule Op. & Call                    & Head & Tail & Used & \texttt{v0} & \texttt{v1} \\\midrule
  0            & Push three bytes        & 0    & 4    & 4    & 0           & 4           \\
  1            & Push two bytes          & 0    & 7    & 7    & 0           & 3           \\
  2            & Pop with room for 31    & 4    & 7    & 3    & 3           & 4           \\
  3            & Push three bytes        & 4    & 3    & 7    & 0           & 4           \\
  4            & Pop with room for 31    & 7    & 3    & 4    & 2           & 3           \\
  5            & Pop with room for 31    & 3    & 3    & 0    & 3           & 4           \\
  6            & Push an empty record    & 3    & 4    & 1    & 0           & 1           \\
  7            & Pop with room for zero  & 4    & 4    & 0    & 0           & 1           \\
  8            & Push seven bytes        & 4    & 4    & 8    & 0           & 8           \\
  9            & Push an empty record    & 4    & 4    & 8    & \(-1\)      & 0           \\
  10           & Pop with room for six   & 4    & 4    & 8    & \(-2\)      & 7           \\
  11           & Pop with room for seven & 4    & 4    & 0    & 7           & 8           \\
  12           & Pop an empty queue      & 4    & 4    & 0    & \(-1\)      & 0           \\
  13           & Push eight bytes        & 4    & 4    & 0    & \(-2\)      & 0           \\\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
Operation nine fails despite its zero-length payload: the full queue has no room
for another prefix. Operation ten reports the required payload length and leaves
the seven-byte record queued. Operation eleven then succeeds without requiring
another push. Operation thirteen can never fit this capacity, even while empty.
The returned status word and auxiliary word preserve these results after main later
reuses \texttt{v0} for the exit syscall.
