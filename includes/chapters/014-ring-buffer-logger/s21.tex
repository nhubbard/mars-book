\section{Step 21 Finish the payload countdown}\label{ring-s21}

Decrease the remaining count and repeat until all decoded payload bytes have been copied. Do not use destination capacity as the copy count: the caller may supply more room than the record needs.

\subsection{Write or inspect}
Append these instructions in the \texttt{pop\_count} region. Keep the scaffold labels and return stubs.

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}[samepage=true]{mars-idea}
    addiu $t7, $t7, -1
    bne $t7, $zero, pop_copy
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}
Bytes after the returned payload remain untouched even when the destination has extra capacity.
