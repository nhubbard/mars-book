\section{Step 19 Explain the layer ordering}\label{maze-s19}

FIFO processing places newly discovered cells after all waiting cells. Since they are one edge farther than the current cell, processing distances never decrease.

\subsection{Write or inspect}

Trace the four queue removals by hand. Compare their distances with the predecessor diagram.

\subsection{Expected observation}

The small room is processed at distances zero, one, one, two. The first discovery of nine records a shortest route even though another equally short route exists.

\subsection{Check your understanding}

Can a different fixed neighbor order choose another shortest route?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Yes; it can choose another predecessor at the same distance.
\item \textbf{B} No; cell IDs force one route.
\item \textbf{C} Only by changing the goal.
\end{itemize}
