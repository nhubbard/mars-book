\section{Step 4 Keep search state across renderer calls}\label{maze-s04}

The search calls a leaf renderer repeatedly. Its contract allows temporary and argument registers to change while saved registers retain the search state.

\subsection{Write or inspect}

Append the base pointers and initial counters in the initialize region. Inspect which values are addresses and which are small integers.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    la $s0, walls
    la $s1, states
    la $s2, parents
    la $s3, distance
    la $s4, queue
    move $s5, $zero
    li $s6, 1
    li $s7, -1
    move $t0, $zero
    li $t1, 255
    la $t2, path
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}

The saved registers hold five array bases, a head counter, a tail counter, and the current cell. The buffers have not yet been initialized.

\subsection{Check your understanding}

Why keep queue counters in saved registers?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} The renderer may overwrite temporary registers.
\item \textbf{B} Saved registers automatically increment.
\item \textbf{C} The queue resides in registers only.
\end{itemize}
