\section{Step 14 Test walls and discovery state}\label{maze-s14}

The candidate must be open and unseen. A cell already waiting in the frontier must not be inserted again when reached from another parent.

\subsection{Write or inspect}

Append the wall and state checks before the discovery writes.

\MARSCodeLabel{MIPS assembly}
\begin{minted}{mars-idea}
    daddu $t0, $s0, $t3
    lbu $t1, 0($t0)
    bne $t1, $zero, next_neighbor
    nop
    daddu $t0, $s1, $t3
    lbu $t1, 0($t0)
    bne $t1, $zero, next_neighbor
    nop
\end{minted}

\subsection{Expected observation}

A candidate with wall one or state one or two is skipped. The first north check is still outside the grid in the small fixture; the remaining directions are visited after the loop continuation is added.

\subsection{Check your understanding}

When may an open candidate be discovered?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} Whenever it is not the current cell.
\item \textbf{B} Only while its state is zero.
\item \textbf{C} Whenever its parent is zero.
\end{itemize}
