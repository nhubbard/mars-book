\section{Step 2 Map the small room to byte indices}\label{maze-s02}

The stepping fixture opens only cells zero, one, eight, and nine. All other cells are walls. This isolates a cycle and two equally short routes.

\subsection{Write or inspect}

Locate the four zero bytes in \texttt{walls}. Express each cell as a row and column in an eight-column grid.

\subsection{Expected observation}

Cell nine is row one, column one. Cell eight is directly below zero. Cell seven and cell eight belong to different rows and do not share a side.

\subsection{Check your understanding}

Are cell seven and cell eight horizontal neighbors?

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A} No; the row boundary lies between them.
\item \textbf{B} Yes; their indices differ by one.
\item \textbf{C} Only when the goal is eight.
\end{itemize}
