may be inserted anywhere in the native source. Project packages separate student material
from private instructor solutions and grading fixtures.

verified light-theme IDE figures with native annotations. The compiled manuscript
passes automated PDF/UA-2 validation. Human accessibility review, student walkthroughs
and substantive editing remain to be completed; automated checks do not replace them.
